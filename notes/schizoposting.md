### Against History

The framing of man as a historical, progressive creature ('Homo progressus') rather than an essential being has become the central pillar of modern identity, functioning as a quasi-religion that suppresses individual agency, scorns ancestral heritage, and narrows the range of human possibility to a ritually-managed consensus.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-001"></span>**Pre-modern peoples defined identity essentially and tribally—Apache, Inuit, Mexica, Greeks, Han Chinese all framed their group as 'the people' contra subhuman outsiders—whereas modernity replaced this with a universal 'Homo sapiens' framework that locates identity in historical process rather than essence.**
    - The Apache called their tribe Ndee (fully human) and outsiders Chidn (those who are like animals); the Mexica called outsiders chichimeca (dog-people).
    - Modern dogma holds there is no basic difference between you and a Khoisan tribesman—both are Homo sapiens shaped only by 'culture,' an abstruse construct developed by ancestral events.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-002"></span>**Man-as-historical-process redefines humanity as a zero-player procedural game governed by universal rules, stripping individual agency and culminating in competing religious sects of historiography that justify any action in defense of their 'One True Doctrine.'**
    - The strong version takes the form of Marxist historical materialism or Jared Diamond's geographic determinism, with a Whiggish presupposition that history iterates toward some eschaton.
    - Santayana's 'Those who cannot remember the past are doomed to repeat it' neuters the potentially revolutionary identity of Homo progressus into an anti-philosophy of infinitely narrowing possibilities.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-003"></span>**Modern identity is deeply historiographical rather than rootless: when asked why anything is the way it is, modern man answers not from first principles but from historical narrative, keeping the past alive as a ritual effigy whose ancestors must be regularly scorned to justify progressive identity.**
    - We dare not take pride in particular ancestry, yet our entire sense of self and social explanation is structured around historical causation—the '1619 Project' style of reasoning.
    - The Indo-European kóryos ritual had youth recite thirty generations of ancestors' names and heroic poetry—historical in form but identity-focused in function, not threatening to the young wolf-warrior the way modern historiographical dissent is to the modern obsessive.
* <span class="pull-quote">History itself did not come to be until Herodotus and Thucydides practically invented the concept in the 5th century BC, and polemical historiography found its genesis in Gibbon, over two thousand years later; its current, concretized form is no more than eighty years old.</span>
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-004"></span>**Cultures are defined by generative tensions—Greek agon, Roman order-vs-chaos, medieval duty-vs-desire—but modernity subsumes all these tensions into disputes over versions of the past, while the 'traditionalist' heel in this kayfabe unknowingly deranges himself from his own tradition by defining himself purely in opposition to the mainstream.**
    - "'Traditionalism' is not the same as the real traditional outlook, for it may be no more than a tendency, a more or less vague aspiration presupposing no real knowledge." —René Guénon
    - The Ramlila tradition—a stage-play reenacting the Ramayana, ending always with the defeat of the demon Ravana—illustrates how symbolic kayfabe consistently affirms group identity by acting out the tensions that define a people.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-005"></span>**The Turkmenistan of Saparmurat Niyazov, through the fabricated national history of the Ruhnama, demonstrates that the true form of history is an act of hyperstition—a people is founded not by 'validation' but by violent self-distinction and supremacy, while 'validation' as a modern imperative functions as a religious act enforcing historiographical consensus.**
    - Niyazov hallucinates an epic history of the Turkmen people, declaring their sovereignty in a document that defined a nation out of a loose collection of tribes; its urban oases of white marble and gold leaf are some of the last ambitious architecture on Earth.
    - History functions as a religious institution with moral commandments, a priestly caste, eschatology, and sins—dissenting belief amounts to blasphemy, and fringe podcasters would be burned at the stake if academics had military power.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-006"></span>**The atomic unit of civilization is martial male cooperation rather than the family or the community, as evidenced by the chariot team, Plato's Symposium, and the conspiracy as the atom of the state—meaning politics is best understood as chemistry: the breaking and consolidation of bonds between conspiracies.**
    - When Enkidu is enlightened, his first desire is not for more sex, knowledge, or material power—it is for a friend, mirroring the Ancient Greek concept of male friendship as the base unit of political society.
    - "The specific political distinction to which political actions and motives can be reduced is that between friend and enemy." —Carl Schmitt
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-007"></span>**The collapse of cultural tensions via man's redefinition as historical process inadvertently lays bare the core essentials of human nature—the tensions between man and woman, sex, and violence—making this the richest period for an autopsy of culture even as the academy abandons such inquiry.**
    - The new form of man is history piled atop history, a creature of sedimentation—like an onion, each layer of progress adds distance between him and the seed of his being.
    - The collapse of culture via the slackening of tensions prevents man from confronting the terror of first principles—but at the same time inadvertently lays them bare.

### On Violence

The systematic removal of violence from Western life has not reduced man's essential violent nature but has merely redirected it into the semiotic plane—producing a culture simultaneously alienated from real violence and pathologically obsessed with it through film, social media, active shooter drills, and ritual political catharsis—while the suppression of low-stakes constructive violence selects for spinelessness in leadership and learned helplessness across the population.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-020"></span>**Violence has declined empirically across all metrics—Steven Pinker documents a drop from 500 violent deaths per 100,000 in pre-state societies to below 10 in the modern US—but this represents not the removal of violence from the human condition but its redirection: even mundane violence like schoolyard fights and bar altercations has been suppressed, creating novel psychocultural pathologies.**
    - The US national violent crime rate has declined by over half since its 1991 peak; contact sports participation has declined; the hunting rate has fallen from nearly 10% to below 5% of the population since the 1980s.
    - Military language has undergone systematic euphemization: enemies are 'neutralized,' torture is 'enhanced interrogation,' bombing runs are 'precision strikes,' and the Marine Corps mantra to 'locate, close with, and destroy the enemy' feels almost archaic against the tooth-to-tail ratio shift since the Gulf War.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-021"></span>**Zero-tolerance policies against schoolyard fights constitute a form of anarcho-tyranny that particularly harms high-potential students: by threatening their 'permanent record,' these policies install lifelong future-anxiety, learned helplessness in the face of violence, and a population of highly-neurotic, low-risk-tolerance professional-managerial class adults.**
    - Foucault argued that observation was a primary mode of modern punishment; above and beyond observation is the continuity of observation, which allows no forgiveness or transience in identity, and ultimately crushes any sort of vital, youthful spirit.
    - The safetyism that addresses youth violence is the same social structure that builds education around neurotic future-planning, placing the pressure of life-defining choices on thirteen-year-olds without the responsibility that would accompany, say, being sent to sea as a midshipman.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-022"></span>**The cultural program of pacification redefines violence as something that only happens to people rather than something agents do, stripping criminals of agency ('generational trauma,' 'cycles of harm') while applying learned helplessness exclusively to the middle class, creating a new universal humiliation ritual for the median white man who must walk away from provocations his grandfather would have met with force.**
    - Verbal, social, and physical acts of provocation only increase when they go unpunished at scale; suppression of response has created a culture of more provocation, not less.
    - Would the American Revolution have occurred if not undertaken by the sort of men that met with pistols at dawn, or refused to polish a British soldier's boots? We would have been robbed of Hamilton, Burr, and Jackson—perhaps of the very spirit that animated the Declaration of Independence.
* <span class="pull-quote">Every single measurement of violence shows a decline in its actual occurrence. Fewer people strike in anger, or receive such a strike, than ever before—yet 'progress' has not succeeded in removing violence from the human condition, or even in dampening its role in society.</span>
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-023"></span>**The modern action movie has evolved from aesthetic expression (Bond, Bruce Lee) and power fantasy (Schwarzenegger, Stallone) into a participatory violence simulation via close filming and hundreds of cuts, culminating in politically-loaded films like Kingsman and The Beekeeper where approved targets are ritually slaughtered—the Violent Passion Surrogate of Brave New World realized as assembly-line catharsis.**
    - The fight scene has become 'gritty'—showing pain, damage, and effort—because the viewer watches not to be dazzled with visual flair but to go through the motions of combat himself, to experience the rush and pain via participatory simulation.
    - Real acts of violence, when they do occur, are rapidly converted into political tulpas—ritual objects for discourse and repetition—until they cease to feel real at all; events like 9/11 become an infinite well of comedy material.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-024"></span>**Active shooter drills—unlike duck-and-cover, which had at least a plausible civil defense rationale—offer little measurable benefit beyond the ritual infusion of psychological terror, implanting school shootings as a generational anxiety and functioning primarily as ideological tools, demonstrating that the reduction in actual violence has not produced psychological peace but chronic anxiety from faux-threat fixation.**
    - Everytown Research & Policy demonstrates active shooter drills to be of dubious effectiveness and extremely psychologically harmful to students of all ages.
    - The fear of violence is further reinforced by ritual reenactments of its prevention: just as duck-and-cover drills reinforced the intangible Cold War, active shooter drills vivid implant school shootings as generational anxiety far beyond what media already achieves.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-025"></span>**Martial training offers a potent antidote to modern alienation because it operates through a reality-reinforcement principle—what is effective is rewarded regardless of meta—and constitutes an esoteric act that trains the skill of recognizing and returning to the essential amidst complexity, a process described by Zen Buddhism and Musashi as both physical and metaphysical refinement.**
    - The medium of violence creates a reality-reinforcement principle within the continuum, preventing a spiral into absurdity: a knockdown karate fighter can use that style in the UFC provided he can make it work, just as Billy Mitchell's advocacy for aircraft carriers was a perception of reality that led to American naval dominance in WWII.
    - Musashi says that 'if you know the Way broadly, you will see it in everything;' training is an act of physical refinement, intellectual patterning, and ultimately metaphysical realization.

### Discourse as ARG

All online political and cultural discourse functions as a vast Alternate Reality Game (ARG) whose reality-shaping mechanics are structurally opposite to those of the stock market—instead of rewarding alignment with reality, the Discourse ARG rewards the enforcement of alternate-world perceptions on physical reality—and extended participation in this ARG produces emergent cult brainwashing that satisfies all eight of Robert Jay Lifton's criteria for thought reform, with no central authority required.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-026"></span>**An Alternate Reality Game (ARG) is a multi-player, real-time game that evolves via players' decisions and only progresses via their participation; the entire online environment—social media discourse, cancel culture, meme warfare—constitutes the supreme ARG, in which team-based conflicts determine acceptable political beliefs, personal behavior, and ultimately perceptions.**
    - Famous ARGs include Cicada 3301 (used cryptographic puzzles to vet initiates), 'I Love Bees' (Halo 2 marketing), Pokémon Go (used for geospatial data collection by Niantic), and Shia LaBeouf's 'He Will Not Divide Us'—demonstrating the multipurpose nature of the ARG dynamic.
    - Participation in this ARG is an esoteric, even occult, action—both high-level analysts and basic participants trend toward looking at the online battlespace as akin to Plato's realm of Forms, in which sorcerers can shift aspects of reality by interacting solely with those Forms.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-027"></span>**The Discourse ARG is structurally inverse to the stock market: whereas the market's reality-reinforcement principle rewards better comprehension of ground truth and ultimately pops speculative bubbles, the Discourse ARG rewards the enforcement of alternate-world perceptions on physical reality, making it capable of shaping base reality in a way the market is not.**
    - The stock market has operated since 1773 on reality-reinforcing principles—the most basic source of value in a stock is under- or overvaluation, a gap between price and intrinsic value, and investors are rewarded for finding and exploiting these misalignments.
    - From Dutch 'tulip mania' in 1637 to the 2008 financial crisis, it is the reality-reinforcement principle that ultimately pops speculative bubbles; at some point, tulips must demonstrate intrinsic value.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-028"></span>**Politics has been reinvented as fandom: the Discourse ARG has bifurcated all subjects into two opposed worldviews, such that knowing someone's interpretation of Starship Troopers (1997) allows accurate prediction of their views on dating, police use of force, religion, and tax policy—because the primary goal is no longer policy but better versedness in one's group shibboleths.**
    - The concept of an 'Overton Keyhole'—a vanishingly small aperture of 'permitted' behavior, chased constantly to avoid the boundless cruelty of becoming the center of Discourse even for a moment—was permanently imposed in 2018 when TikTok merged with musical.ly and infinite-scroll algorithmic shortform video took over.
    - Groups which engage in Discourse but do not keep up with the pace of the meta can be identified as artificial by default—NAFO, billed as an organic pro-NATO movement, consisted near-entirely of US military and government employees posting repetitive memes from Eglin Air Force Base.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-029"></span>**Extended participation in the Discourse ARG produces all eight of Robert Jay Lifton's criteria for cult thought reform as emergent behavior—milieu control, mystical manipulation, sacred science, loading the language, demand for purity, confession, doctrine over person, and dispensing of existence—without any central authority, because social media is structurally a cybernetic paperclip machine for brainwashing cultism.**

    - Social media's 'thought-terminating clichés' are memes: simple symbols serving as shorthand for complex arguments, meant to cut out cognitive effort—this process is roughly the entire basis of modern culture, with media, politics, and personal behavior all downstream of it.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-030"></span>**The emergent brainwashing of social media has broken the barrier between perception and reality: consensus reality is now governed by ARG-mediated perception alone, such that an ideological strawman will pick up unironic adherents by being summoned by its opponents, and the exercise of agency over reality necessarily takes place within the Discourse ARG.**
    - If people believe that something is popular, others will act as if it is popular; criticism of a behavioral pattern leads to popular overfitting to its larger frame, inducing a spike in its occurrence; performative defiance of a sexual norm leads to popular fetishization and rejection in line with the inversion.

## Part III: Manifesto

### On Liminality

Computerization kills liminality—the transitional, ambiguous state between binary categories—and in doing so kills religion, ritual coming-of-age, complex cultural tension, and ultimately the mythopoetic mode of existence that made historical greatness possible; recovery requires not restoration of old forms but a radical revival of religion derived from first principles, merging fanaticism and liminality as the only forces capable of operating outside consensus reality.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-047"></span>**Computerization represents the ultimate simplification of reality into zero and one, and in doing so kills liminality—the transitional state that is neither zero nor one—which plays a key role in human experience: the adolescent transitional state, apparitions 'neither here nor elsewhere,' the androgynous shaman, the unbaptized infant who is neither believer nor heretic.**
    - Arnold van Gennep first recognized the liminal state in folk rituals, cataloguing transitional moments across cultures; today there is no coming-of-age induction ritual, only an eighteenth-birthday toggle-switch, producing a population of childlike adults and adultlike children and a cultural fixation on what the transition actually means.
    - Without ritual acknowledgment of ambiguity, a culture loses the capacity to comprehend ambiguous ideas even in media; prophets like David Lynch are reinterpreted as moralists, and comedy is reframed not as the jester revealing contradiction but as the primary mode of social instruction.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-048"></span>**Computerized knowledge cannot accommodate 'I don't know'—flattening everything unexplained to 'unreal'—casting aside centuries of intellectual tradition that revolved around the unity of opposites; scientism has crystallized twice (late 19th century and now) despite theoretical physics briefly questioning the nature of reality, with the deconstructive implications of quantum mechanics quarantined to a mathematical priesthood.**
    - Homer, Herodotus, Ibn Battuta, Marco Polo, Bernal Díaz, Gaspar de Carvajal—all recorded things dismissed for decades as myth until hard evidence forced their acknowledgement; a new historiography based on default acceptance rather than default skepticism would look dramatically different.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-049"></span>**The collapse of liminality kills religion by transmogrifying it into a 'canon' alongside Marvel and Star Wars—the religious man defines himself primarily contra the mainstream, deranging himself from his intellectual lineage and forcing the mysteries of faith into binarized reality—while forgotten in popular religion is the trance or dream state, the intentional pursuit of liminality present in all traditions: Greek mystery-rites, Buddhist meditation, Sufi whirling-dervishes, shamanic entheogenic trances.**

### Alt-Transhumanism & Cavalierization

Transhumanism properly understood is not the Marxist mass-betterment fantasy of its current proponents but 'alt-transhumanism': the historically continuous drive of an elite to extend individual capabilities through technology for martial and eugenic ends, whose social expression is 'cavalierization'—the cyclical empowerment of the individual on the battlefield that disrupts entrenched hierarchies and creates new social orders—and whose true goal is the production of the Übermensch rather than the conversion of men into batteries for a technocratic Demiurge.

* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-053"></span>**The atlatl—man's first ranged weapon, a stick with a cup allowing more throwing leverage—was the first act of transhumanism because it created an extension of the individual man that allowed him to surpass his natural abilities; any valid definition of transhumanism must therefore apply backwards to include historic technologies, and the purpose of transhumanism in its original form was not comfort but war.**
    - Paleolithic man, if he had the language for it, would have viewed the first atlatl-throwers as cyborgs—their intrinsic abilities extended not only by a novel technology but by a novel concept of technology, one which integrates with and extends man in his natural state.
    - The 1998 Transhumanist Declaration does not even define its titular philosophy, instead pondering the ethics of a still-undefined thing; its presupposition that transhumanism only concerns technology downstream of the computer is incoherent without a principle that would also exclude the atlatl.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-054"></span>**Alt-transhumanism is the historically continuous drive of an elite to extend individual capabilities through technology toward martial excellence and eugenic refinement—the Greek polis as a breeding project for superior specimens, the Knights Templar as mental software for a warrior-monk elite—concerned with the vanguard rather than the rearguard, defined in Nietzsche's Roman good/bad paradigm rather than the Judaic good/evil framework of popular transhumanism.**
    - Costin Alamariu argues that the Greek polis was 'nothing more or less than a breeding project for superior specimens;' Plato and his contemporaries conceived of the city as a eugenic mechanism, a system for the elevation of types—so the philosophical impulse was not separate from the technological but a metaphysical framework for directed human advancement.
    - Andy Clark argues in Natural-Born Cyborgs that mental scaffolding functions as integrated technology; thus the ritualized lives of the Knights Templar and the samurai serve as mental software—an intentional effort at forging an insular warrior elite made up of men-but-better, each worth hundreds on the battlefield.
* <span id="schizoposting-11-essays-on-culture-055"></span>**Cavalierization is the historical process by which new military technologies cyclically de-anonymize warfare by empowering the individual on the battlefield, disrupting entrenched hierarchies and creating new social orders: the chariot created the aristocratic superstratum, the phalanx the Greek polis, the stirrup the feudal order, and the fighter plane revived chivalry—each allowing an independent man to act as a self-directed military unit.**
    - The story of warfare oscillates between mass-tactics that anonymize the individual and technological revolutions that return disproportionate agency to individual warfighters: pike squares neutralized the individual cavalryman, improved firearms returned agency to the individual sharpshooter, artillery created the meat-grinder of WWI, air warfare created 'knights of the sky.'
    - The War on Spontaneous Global Organization is, on a grand historical scale, a war against the individual's capability to leverage the internet for outsized power in the noetic battlespace—analogous to the historical trend of warfare away from the individual soldier's influence on the battlefield.
