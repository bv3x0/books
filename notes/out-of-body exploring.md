# Out-of-Body Exploring: A Beginner's Approach

## Metadata
- Thesis: Out-of-body experiences are a natural, learnable human capacity that anyone can develop through practice, and conscious astral travel provides verifiable evidence of life after death, psychic ability, and higher spiritual dimensions.
- Topics: astral projection techniques, lucid dreaming, life after death, astral dimensions exploration, psychic phenomena
- Categories: self-help, religion, psychology
- Author: Preston Dennett
- Year: 2004
- Mode: quick

## Introduction

The author's mother died unexpectedly in 1984, triggering a series of ghost sightings and dream visitations that shattered his skepticism and led him to research and eventually master out-of-body travel, accumulating over a thousand conscious OBEs across nearly two decades.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-001"></span>**The death of Dennett's mother in October 1984 from a sudden heart attack served as the inciting trauma that launched his investigation into the paranormal; two weeks later he saw her apparition in broad daylight seated in his father's car.**
    - She was found in her hotel room in San Francisco, having suffered the attack while blow-drying her hair after ordering room service.
    - Dennett initially dismissed the apparition as a hallucination because he did not believe in ghosts or life after death.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-002"></span>**Repeated vivid dream visitations from his deceased mother felt unmistakably real and conveyed an urgent message of continued existence after death, though Dennett's rationalism initially caused him to dismiss them.**
    - In April 1986, his mother walked into his bedroom in a false awakening and their embrace felt completely physical; he woke convinced it was real but rationalized it away.
    - His reasoning at the time: 'It can't be, therefore it isn't' — yet the visitations persisted and slowly eroded his skepticism.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-003"></span>**The visitations drove Dennett to study shamanism, UFOs, ghosts, channeling, and eventually OBEs, leading him to attempt astral projection — which worked almost immediately and developed into a lifelong practice.**
    - After years of effort and practice he learned to control the OBE state and eventually logged more than a thousand conscious out-of-body experiences.
    - Astral exploration revealed not glowing beings in mist but something more like physical life — cities, oceans, forests — only more vivid and real.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-004"></span>**The book's central argument is that the astral dimensions are an objectively real frontier that remains poorly mapped, and first-person accounts of advanced astral travel are rare enough to justify Dennett's own record.**
    - Dennett compares the state of astral knowledge to sending twenty people to Earth for short periods — each returns with widely varying accounts of an incomprehensibly vast place.
    - He argues that if everyone had conscious OBEs, the world would be transformed, and presents exercises for both inducing and navigating the OBE state.

## Early Out-of-Body Experiences

Dennett's first years of practice (1987–1996) were marked by confusion between lucid dreams and genuine OBEs, brief and uncontrolled excursions dominated by excitement or fear, and a gradual recognition that critical self-awareness — not special talent — is the essential key to the out-of-body state.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-005"></span>**Lucid dreams and OBEs occupy a spectrum rather than being sharply distinct phenomena: in lucid dreams the environment is internally generated by mental projection, while in genuine OBEs the environment is externally real and independent of the projector's thoughts — though the boundary blurs because the astral plane is itself thought-responsive.**
    - "A lucid dream is a genuine type of OBE, although the dimensional gate traveled through to achieve it is best thought of as being internal." —Robert Bruce
    - "One of the best ways to initiate an out-of-body experience is to become aware or lucid within a dream." —William Buhlman
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-006"></span>**Dennett's first confirmed OBE in February 1988 involved powerful physical sensations — extreme heaviness followed by electric-like vibrations and a 'whoosh' — that Robert Monroe had documented but which felt far more intense than Monroe's mild description suggested.**
    - He zoomed from his bedroom into the bathroom, grabbed the counter, and repeated 'I'm doing it! I'm doing it!' before a wave of tiredness pulled him back into sleep.
    - Feelings of bodily heaviness, vibrations, vertigo, and lightheadedness continued to mark the onset of OBEs across subsequent years of practice.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-007"></span>**False awakenings — waking into what feels like ordinary reality but is actually still a dream — were Dennett's most persistent obstacle; he would repeatedly rationalize bizarre anomalies (standing in his bed, waking on the kitchen floor) rather than recognizing them as dream cues.**
    - Dream cues identified over time included distorted text or screens, impossible weather, celebrities, levitation, and unusual reflections in mirrors.
    - Patricia Garfield's observation that seeing one's own reflection in a dream signals the activation of the astral double helped Dennett interpret recurring mirror imagery.
* <span class="pull-quote">Most advanced astral travelers believe that everyone goes out-of-body every night. At first this seemed absurd to me, but as I began to remember more and more of what happened to me at night, I realized it was true.</span>
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-008"></span>**Memory, not technique, is the fundamental bottleneck to OBE practice: virtually everyone leaves the body nightly but operates in a state of profound amnesia, forgetting the experiences almost immediately upon returning to waking consciousness.**
    - A near-forgotten OBE in July 1989 — in which Dennett stood beside his sleeping body in terror before diving back in — convinced him that vast numbers of nightly events go completely unrecalled.
    - Keeping a dream journal and developing memory exercises were among the most practical tools for increasing OBE frequency and recall.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-009"></span>**Projectors consistently return to the location of their first conscious projection, a pattern Sylvan Muldoon and Robert Bruce both documented; Dennett repeatedly emerged in the backyard of his childhood home even after moving miles away.**
    - "When you once become conscious in a certain place in the astral, you will probably become conscious in the same spot under similar conditions." —Sylvan Muldoon
    - Bruce similarly found that a large shopping mall served as his default OBE launch point, suggesting the phenomenon is neurological or habitual rather than arbitrary.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-010"></span>**The first year of practice yielded OBEs that were almost universally brief, uncontrolled, and clouded by excitement or doubt; achieving sustained, purposeful out-of-body travel required years of incremental improvement across movement, vision, emotional regulation, and mental clarity.**
    - Staying out of body for more than two minutes would have been a great accomplishment through the first year; most experiences ended when emotion overwhelmed awareness.
    - Even a late OBE over Las Vegas in November 1996 — where Dennett recognized real buildings from hundreds of feet up — was still cut short by loss of consciousness.

## The Desire Body

The astral body is also the emotional or 'desire' body, meaning every thought and feeling instantly manifests in the astral environment with overwhelming force, and novice projectors — including Dennett over many years — routinely find their out-of-body experiences hijacked by uncontrolled excitement, destructive impulses, hunger, and sexual desire before they can conduct any intentional experiments.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-011"></span>**On the astral plane, emotions are not just felt but become the projector's entire reality with psychotic intensity; all major OBE researchers — Monroe, Bruce, Muldoon, Taylor — document this as the single greatest obstacle for new projectors.**
    - "Fear, anxiety, anger, excitement, and the sexual urge — especially primal urges such as fear and sex — all have very strong emotional undercurrents that can cause particularly strong emotional feedback between the physical/etheric, real-time, and astral bodies." —Robert Bruce
    - "As a man thinks, so is he! When I think of trying to convey just what this implies, I almost give up in despair." —Sylvan Muldoon
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-012"></span>**Dennett's desire body repeatedly commandeered his OBEs through destructive rampages — especially in grocery stores — that he recognized as projections of his psyche but could not suppress, a pattern that persisted for years despite his best intentions.**
    - In multiple experiences he found himself knocking over shelves, smashing bottles, throwing cans, and eating compulsively — then losing consciousness mid-rampage.
    - He later understood these outbursts as the desire body's demand to be satisfied before nobler pursuits were possible, and discovered that submitting briefly to appetites helped him proceed to experiments.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-013"></span>**Physical sensations triggering OBE onset extended beyond vibrations to include intense thirst, choking, hunger, and sexual arousal — each representing the desire body asserting itself — and learning to recognize rather than rationalize these sensations was a critical skill.**
    - In one recurring pattern, an insatiable thirst while drinking from a faucet would suddenly propel him into full OBE lucidity, after which his astral body would float horizontally back into his bedroom.
    - The real secret about the out-of-body state is the importance of memory — we are nearly all in a condition of profound amnesia, constantly forgetting who we are and where we are.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-014"></span>**Movement through solid objects during an OBE depends primarily on the projector's degree of awareness and subconscious belief: when fully conscious Dennett passed through walls effortlessly, but at lower awareness levels he bounced off them, confirming Robert Bruce's thesis about belief systems.**
    - "If they believe they can move easily through a solid wall or door and are willing to just do it, without even thinking about it, novices will be able to do so." —Robert Bruce
    - Dennett never managed to affect the physical environment (move physical objects) despite being able to pass through it freely when sufficiently conscious.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-015"></span>**Learning to maintain awareness during an OBE is like walking a tightrope — any emotional surge, distraction, or lapse in critical thinking collapses the state — and Robert Bruce's 'running commentary' technique of repeating 'This is a dream!' was among the most effective tools Dennett discovered.**
    - During a prolonged April 1994 OBE, whenever Dennett felt his awareness dip he chanted 'This is a dream!' and found it maintained full lucidity even while knowing he was actually out of body.
    - It took over four years of practice before OBEs became reliable enough to attempt meaningful experiments, and his OBEs remained predominantly brief even into the mid-1990s.

## Experiments on the Astral Plane

Once Dennett gained sufficient control to attempt deliberate experiments, he tested astral matter, reading, sound, smell, object creation, and the search for his deceased mother — discovering that the astral plane is indistinguishable from physical reality in texture and detail, that reading is nearly impossible, and that calling for his mother almost never worked on his schedule.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-016"></span>**The astral plane is composed of a thought-responsive material that instantly manifests whatever the projector imagines with photorealistic fidelity — trees, cars, buildings, entire environments — which is why lucid dreams and OBEs are so easily confused and why 'thoughts are things' is axiomatic in occult traditions.**
    - In a December 1990 experiment, Dennett created an apple, then his garage, then trees, cars, and buildings in rapid succession — each coalescing like a 'high-speed watercolor painting' into pristine, glowing reality.
    - When we dream, we are really out of our bodies on the astral plane, manifesting our thoughts and playing out personal dramas created by our minds.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-017"></span>**Reading is extremely difficult during OBEs and lucid dreams: letters dart, transpose, and blur when directly focused upon, and while numbers and words can sometimes be glimpsed, coherent text almost never transfers into waking memory.**
    - Robert Monroe's attempt to read an astral book yielded only four words — 'Evoke unhappy beings by...' — after laborious letter-by-letter effort.
    - Dennett's own reading experiments produced random alphanumeric sequences (A-T-V-0-10-11-G4-T) and words that made sense while dreaming but vanished upon waking.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-018"></span>**The astral body is capable of full sensory experience including smell and temperature — Dennett successfully smelled moist earth by bending down to sniff a strip of soil beside a house, the first reported smell experiment he was aware of.**
    - He also felt the warmth of a friend's cheek and the coolness of astral water, confirming that temperature perception is available but was conspicuously absent from most of his early experiences.
    - When out of body and fully conscious, passing through solid objects like closed windows produced no physical sensation whatsoever — as if they were not there.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-019"></span>**A key experimental finding was that shouting while out of body does not cause the physical body to make audible sounds — Dennett confirmed this when his partner J-, who was awake and lying next to him, reported hearing nothing and observing no physical movement.**
    - He shouted 'J-, I'm dreaming! J-, I'm dreaming!' several times and could clearly hear his own voice inside the OBE state, but upon returning and asking J-, was told neither sound nor movement had occurred.
    - This finding — that the physical body remains completely inert during even the most dramatic OBE experiences — is consistent across the OBE literature.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-020"></span>**Task-orientation is the single most effective strategy for maintaining OBE control: projectors who enter the state without a plan are immediately overwhelmed by emotion and distraction, while those with primary, secondary, and tertiary goals can focus past the desire body.**
    - "Plan projections and get into the habit of sticking to your plan. Have primary, secondary, and tertiary mission goals, and focus on these during projection." —Robert Bruce
    - Dennett's repeated attempts to visit his brother Marco's house to gather verifiable evidence were consistently derailed by emotional overwhelm, demonstrating that intention alone is insufficient without practiced discipline.

## Further Astral Experiments

As Dennett's OBEs grew longer and more controlled, he expanded his experiments to include bilateral consciousness (experiencing both astral and physical environments simultaneously), the 'Clarity!' command, radio and television experiments, weight increase phenomena, and ultimately verified physical-world details — including dirt banks under a bridge — that he confirmed the following day.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-021"></span>**William Buhlman's single-word command 'Clarity!' became Dennett's most reliable tool for rescuing a fading OBE — shouting it repeatedly triggered an immediate flood of lucidity that restored both vision and awareness.**
    - In a pivotal November 1996 OBE, Dennett used 'Clarity!' to stabilize after rolling out of body into outer space, then back into his bedroom, successfully returning to and re-exiting his body multiple times in one session.
    - The command works because on the astral plane thought is action — stating the intention for clarity immediately manifests it in the projector's consciousness.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-022"></span>**Dennett experienced bilocation — simultaneously perceiving both his physical bedroom and a distant astral environment with equal clarity — suggesting that the astral and physical bodies can maintain co-awareness rather than the physical merely being switched off during projection.**
    - In one OBE, he could feel his exact physical body position, sense his bedroom around him, and know he could stand up and walk, while remaining simultaneously active in another dimension miles away.
    - Both Robert Bruce and Bruce Moen had independently reported this dual perception, validating what Dennett initially thought was a unique anomaly.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-023"></span>**Exit sensations — including exit noises (furniture moving, voices, jet sounds), feelings of cold hands grabbing the body, and startling vocalizations — are extremely common for new projectors and represent the most frequent cause of OBE abortion; experienced projectors learn to ignore them entirely.**
    - "Astral noises are quite common during the exit, but always cease the moment the exit is complete... Any and all astral noises must be totally ignored during the exit stage of an OBE." —Robert Bruce
    - In one of Dennett's attempts, his nephew's voice urgently cried 'Take me with you!' causing him to snap back to waking awareness just as he was about to lift out.
* <span class="pull-quote">The next day, I went to the bridge to see if there really were dirt banks. To my shock, a dirt bank about two feet thick covered the cement river bank under the bridge, exactly where I had seen it.</span>
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-024"></span>**An OBE television-radio experiment yielded striking results when Dennett tuned an astral radio and heard Jim Croce, Cher, and Steve Miller Band playing songs whose lyrics — 'Moving on Down the Highway,' 'Do You Believe in Life after Love,' and 'Fly Like an Eagle' — each seemed to carry a direct personal message.**
    - The music sounded exactly as it does in real life, and Dennett interpreted the sequential song choices as meaningful communication rather than random projection.
    - Alan Worsley had pioneered dream-television experiments as a control technique, but Dennett found the content — not just the control — to be the more interesting phenomenon.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-025"></span>**Dennett obtained his most convincing personal proof that OBEs are objectively real when he observed unexpected dirt banks along the cement-sided L.A. River during an OBE and confirmed their existence the next day — a detail he could not have predicted.**
    - He had assumed the entire river bottom was cemented, so the two-foot soil bank with grass and litter he observed seemed like a projection error — yet it was physically verified.
    - This was particularly convincing because I could not have predicted it. I would never have thought that such a huge volume of soil could collect there, and yet it did.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-026"></span>**Robert Bruce's claim that the astral body is fundamentally a point of consciousness whose human-looking form is itself a projection — and that staring at one's astral hands will cause them to melt — was confirmed when Dennett watched his fingers and then arms dissolve before his eyes.**
    - The effect was startling and very strange — each finger melted and faded away, then his hands disappeared, leaving only two stumps before his arms began melting as well.
    - This experience suggested that the sense of having a body while out of body is itself a habitual mental construction rather than a reflection of any actual astral anatomy.

## People and Animals on the Astral Plane

The most emotionally significant dimension of Dennett's OBE practice involved visiting his deceased mother, deceased and living family members, and even departed pets — experiences characterized by floods of memory, intense reunion feelings, and consistent evidence that the dead remain active and accessible on the astral plane while the living are largely unaware of their nightly astral activity.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-027"></span>**Visitations from Dennett's deceased mother had a distinctive quality — floods of remembered past meetings, overwhelming love, and her physical reality — that differed sharply from dream projections of her; upon waking he would forget the accumulated memories but remembered having remembered them.**
    - During a November 1990 visit, his mother lifted him by his armpits and swung him around — something she could never have done physically — demonstrating the liberation of the astral body from physical limitations.
    - In each visitation Dennett would remember hundreds of previous meetings, then lose access to those memories upon waking, leaving only the memory of having remembered.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-028"></span>**The deceased family dog Maxine repeatedly visited Dennett in pairs — twice, usually a few days apart — radiating unmistakable love and presence, proving to him that animal consciousness also survives death.**
    - In one visit Maxine appeared in a gray, featureless environment that Dennett recognized as the meeting place for deceased contacts — she licked him and he was convinced it was genuinely her.
    - After a later family dog named Rouster died, Dennett found him within weeks, looking like a puppy and biting playfully — followed on three subsequent occasions, each time apparently thriving.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-029"></span>**Living people encountered during OBEs can be distinguished from mental projections by the quality of their presence — real people exude self-awareness, love, and personality, while projections act woodenly with vacant eyes — a distinction Dennett found reliable across many encounters.**
    - During a June 1994 OBE he encountered his sister-in-law Christy and held her face in both hands; she felt completely physical and her personality was fully present, though she had no memory of the meeting upon waking.
    - Most family members Dennett visited were 'subconscious astral' — Marilyn Hughes's term for people who have OBEs but lack the awareness to remember them.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-030"></span>**A striking apparent verification occurred when Dennett encountered a family friend named Rex in the astral realm, glowing with health and radiating white light, only to learn that Rex had just suffered a severe stroke in the physical world.**
    - Rex normally had health problems and walked hunched over; in the OBE he appeared completely healthy, literally glowing — which Dennett took as evidence that the astral body reflects true health independent of physical condition.
    - Dennett had planned to call Rex to ask about unusual dreams but instead received news of the stroke, suggesting the astral encounter coincided with a physical health crisis.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-031"></span>**Dennett's father appeared in his bedroom during an OBE standing beside the bed, and when Dennett told him the next morning, his father confirmed he had dreamed of having wings and flying all over the house — consistent with the widely reported observation that flying dreams are half-remembered OBEs.**
    - As most OBEers will tell you, flying dreams are almost always half-remembered OBEs — the physical body's waking amnesia erases the experience but the flying sensation remains.
    - This parallel experience between two family members on the same night provided informal mutual corroboration that astral travel is an objectively occurring nightly event, not a private hallucination.

## Food and Sex on the Astral Plane

The desire body's two most powerful appetites — for food and for sex — dominate early and intermediate OBE experiences; both are fully sensory on the astral plane, and Dennett argues following Robert Monroe and Patricia Garfield that sexual energy is directly linked to the mechanism that enables astral projection, making these urges both obstacles and, paradoxically, launching pads.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-032"></span>**Astral food is fully sensory — cold, flavorful, textured — but with subtly reduced intensity compared to physical food; Dennett confirmed this by eating Neapolitan ice cream, orange juice, sour cream potato chips, peanut butter, and Skittles, each tasting genuinely real though slightly muted.**
    - The ice cream melted in his mouth and seemed to disappear halfway down his throat; the potato chips were 'incredibly crunchy and delicious' with the taste described as 'absolutely fantastic.'
    - Stephen LaBerge's lucid dream respondents had already documented astral feasting; Dennett used this as a strategy to satisfy his desire body before attempting higher experiments.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-033"></span>**Patricia Garfield argued that orgasm is a natural component of lucid dreaming — she experienced sexual energy culminating in orgasmic release in fully two-thirds of her lucid dreams — and proposed that conscious dreaming stimulates brain regions associated with all ecstatic states including flying and mystical union.**
    - "My present hypothesis is this: Orgasm is a natural part of lucid dreaming. My own experience convinces me that conscious dreaming is orgasmic." —Patricia Garfield
    - "Sensations of flying, sexual heights, acute pleasurable awareness, and a sense of oneness are all natural outcomes of a prolonged lucid dream." —Patricia Garfield
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-034"></span>**Robert Monroe concluded that the sexual drive and the force enabling astral projection are the same energy redirected — the vibrations that launch an OBE may be a manifestation of redirected sexual energy — and he was never able to fully suppress the drive during projections.**
    - During Monroe's second projection he was overcome by sexual feelings so strongly he returned to his body embarrassed and irritated at his inability to control the emotion.
    - Dennett confirms this from personal experience: 'Until you go out of body and feel the power of the sexual force, it's impossible to convey an accurate description.'
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-035"></span>**Dennett's most powerful early OBE was ignited by sexual energy: each step he took in his astral body produced an escalating wave of erotic feeling that doubled with every step into a state of continuous explosion — an experience he describes as the most powerful of his life at that point.**
    - The experience in November 1989 — his astral body 'zinging with energy' and running helplessly as pleasure became 'unbearably powerful' — convinced him that Garfield's sexual-OBE link was not metaphor but literal mechanism.
    - Gnostic teacher Samael Aun Weor specifically taught that sexual energy is the key to kundalini and thus to the OBE state, connecting these phenomena to established esoteric traditions.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-036"></span>**Over time, Dennett found that the compulsion to satisfy desire-body appetites gradually faded — not through suppression but through satiation — and that submitting briefly to desires was more effective than fighting them as a strategy for reaching higher OBE goals.**
    - None of his sexual OBEs resulted in physical wet dreams, unlike Monroe's experiences, and he never fully set the desires aside — instead they slowly faded as he progressed.
    - The desire body's demands represent the lowest vibrational layer of the astral experience; experienced projectors must pass through this layer to reach finer dimensions.

## Mantra Experiences

Gnostic teacher Samael Aun Weor's mantra 'gaom-raom-om-bour-bu-mama-papa' — nicknamed 'the dynamite of the dream state' — proved genuinely powerful across multiple uses, either downloading the night's accumulated OBE memories simultaneously or propelling Dennett into a major high-level projection; two dreams recalled on a single mantra morning were confirmed as precognitive and mutually shared with his sister.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-037"></span>**Samael Aun Weor's mantra, pronounced during the hypnagogic moment of waking, triggered simultaneous access to all the night's dreams at once — up to ten playing in parallel streams of consciousness — overwhelming Dennett but allowing him to recover six distinct dream memories in one December 1990 session.**
    - The mechanics behind the mantra remain opaque — Dennett speculates it works through the power of thought on the astral plane — but results were consistent across at least four separate uses over six years.
    - The mantra can also function as a launch command: in a 1994 OBE he pronounced it mid-experience and was propelled at high velocity out of the dream state and into a genuine OBE over his father's horse corral.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-038"></span>**Two dreams recovered through the mantra in December 1990 were confirmed as precognitive and mutually shared: a red plastic helmet appeared as a gift that very day with identical details, and his sister Victoria independently reported a dream sharing at least seven elements with his own coastal cliff dream.**
    - The shared elements included: location along the Pacific Coast, a narrow strip or pathway, rain, a cliff, inching along, fear of falling, and a chain-link fence or cage — a level of specificity that rules out coincidence.
    - The helmet dream was particularly precise — even the little ridges on the edge matched the physical object exactly — demonstrating the mantra's capacity to surface veridical precognitive material.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-039"></span>**P. D. Ouspensky's claim that pronouncing one's own name during a dream causes immediate waking was tested by Dennett and Patricia Garfield with dramatic but inconsistent results — Garfield found the atmosphere 'vibrated and thundered' when she carved her name, while Dennett's own experiments mostly produced nothing until one explosion into a meteor-shower dimension.**
    - When Dennett stated 'My name is Preston Dennett' during a March 1997 OBE, he was instantly transported to a field where the night sky displayed massive crisscrossing fireballs, then shifted into a dimension of millions of tiny golden flakes.
    - Stephen LaBerge conducted the same experiment with minimal reaction, concluding that prior expectations may influence the drama of the result — suggesting the mantra and name experiments both work through the thought-responsive nature of the astral plane.

## OBEs and Healing

After being diagnosed with rheumatoid arthritis in 2001, Dennett used his OBE practice to attempt self-healing — receiving cryptic advice ('You must climb into your crown'), experiencing hands lifted by an electrical healing force, and exercising his astral body freely to model the health he sought — reporting that his disease has been largely in remission, though he cannot isolate the OBE contribution from medication.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-040"></span>**Multiple advanced OBE practitioners — including Albert Taylor (multiple sclerosis), Robert Bruce (hip problem), Bruce Moen (rare liver disease), and others — have reported healings attributable to their astral travel, establishing a documented pattern that Dennett sought to apply to his own rheumatoid arthritis.**
    - A personal friend of Dennett's had a near-death experience during which she was cured of cancer, providing a close-range example of the healing potential of OBE-adjacent states.
    - Paul Twitchell cured himself of a serious childhood illness, and Terrill Wilson woke from a powerful OBE to find fever, nausea, sore throat, and congestion completely gone.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-041"></span>**During a May 2001 OBE, a beautiful woman on an astral path told Dennett 'You must climb into your crown' in response to his question about healing — a phrase he initially didn't understand but later recognized as instruction to activate the crown chakra.**
    - The cryptic nature of the instruction exemplifies a recurring pattern in Dennett's OBEs: information arrives symbolically or obliquely rather than as direct practical advice.
    - Shortly after, a separate OBE lifted both his hands upward involuntarily into a strong buzzing electrical healing force — notable because at that time only his left hand hurt, while both arms later developed arthritis.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-042"></span>**Dennett's most creative healing attempt involved exercising vigorously in the astral body — doing jumping jacks, shadow-boxing, and fully extending his arthritic elbow — on the theory that the superior astral body's demonstrated health could be downloaded into the physical body.**
    - During a January 2003 OBE he experienced total freedom from pain: 'The feeling is a huge relief and is absolutely exquisite. This is how I want to feel in the physical.'
    - He also observed his reflection in an astral mirror where his hair regrew and he lost fifteen years of age in seconds — interpreting this as the astral body reflecting ideal health rather than physical condition.

## Meetings with God and My Higher Self

Dennett's repeated attempts to contact God and his Higher Self produced consistently unexpected results — his Higher Self appeared variously as his brother and sister-in-law, a stranger named Diane, and a pleasant blond woman named Bonnie — suggesting either that his Higher Self is other people (pointing toward universal interconnection) or that expectation-free commands produce results too strange for the projector's framework to interpret.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-043"></span>**Researcher Fariba Bogzaran found that 80 percent of people who believe in a personal God encounter God as a person during OBEs, while 80 percent of those who believe in an impersonal deity encounter something non-personal — suggesting that prior belief, not objective reality, shapes the God-encounter.**
    - A computer-programmer friend of Dennett's who shouted 'Take me to see God!' found himself in a room of computers operated by a little old man at a central console — an experience shaped entirely by his professional conceptual framework.
    - Robert Peterson, during an OBE request to see God, was hit by a single bolt of raw energy so powerful it knocked his astral body to the ground — 'just raw power; there was no feeling behind it.'
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-044"></span>**Dennett's first attempt to shout 'I want to see my God!' resulted in his astral body being seized by an extremely powerful force and whisked upward through the ceiling at a speed that immediately overwhelmed his consciousness — he woke later feeling more energized and joyful than he could remember.**
    - The experience of being gripped and accelerated by a divine force was consistent with Peterson's energy-bolt encounter — both men described being overpowered rather than communicated with.
    - More than four years passed before Dennett made another attempt, reflecting both the rarity of successful high-control OBEs and the intimidating nature of the encounter.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-045"></span>**When Dennett commanded 'I want to see my Higher Self,' he was transported to a small paneled room where his brother Marco and sister-in-law Christy waited for him smiling — an outcome he found hilarious and confusing, as it implied his Higher Self is other people.**
    - Subsequent attempts produced Diane (his father's friend, who said 'I think you've got the wrong person') and an unknown blond woman named Bonnie or Susan who smiled knowingly but said nothing.
    - Dennett's tentative conclusion: 'Could it be that my Higher Self is other people? Maybe we are all connected' — a hypothesis of universal interconnection consistent with both mystical traditions and quantum theories of consciousness.

## High-Level Out-of-Body Experiences

Dennett's most advanced OBEs accessed higher dimensions — the heavenly pastoral realms, a crystal world, the Akashic Library, a soul-retrieval, and a past-life revisitation — constituting his strongest evidence for a structured multidimensional cosmos in which deceased loved ones thrive, personal history extends across lifetimes, and a repository of all knowledge genuinely exists.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-046"></span>**In a landmark March 1997 OBE, Dennett's deceased mother appeared when called, took him hand-in-hand through three ascending dimensions — a rocky alien landscape, a vivid pastoral heaven, and a world of pure white crystal — providing the most extensive tour of the afterlife he ever experienced.**
    - In the crystal dimension, his mother turned a patch of crystals orange and invited him to taste them — they exploded with an electrically intense orange flavor unlike anything in the physical world.
    - His mother appeared to be about twenty-five years old, younger than Dennett himself, laughed playfully, and communicated largely through a telepathic link — the sky displayed the words 'Love you! Love you! Love you!' written in clouds.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-047"></span>**Dennett entered the NDEer-described 'Light' during a December 1995 OBE — he saw it as a vast glowing white-yellow cloud, flew into it, and experienced overwhelming warmth and well-being before a dimensional drop pulled him back into lower astral levels.**
    - The experience confirmed the near-death experience literature's consistent description of the Light as warm, loving, and beautiful — and demonstrated that living OBEers can access the same state as those who encounter it in clinical death.
    - Each downward 'shift' during high-level OBEs represents a change of energy body — moving from a finer vibrational vehicle to a coarser one — requiring memory to be 'downloaded' at each transition.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-048"></span>**Dennett performed a soul retrieval experiment in July 2002, being propelled to total darkness where he found a confused five-year-old blond girl, hugged her, took her hand, and flew upward until she released his hand — disappearing, presumably to meet her mother — exactly as Monroe and Moen had described such rescues.**
    - A vague impression of suffocation and being stuck under a house accompanied the encounter, suggesting a traumatic death scenario for the child.
    - Upon releasing the girl's hand, Dennett found himself in a small underground space containing a huge pile of gold coins representing his memories of the event — he grabbed as many large coins as he could carry back.
* <span class="pull-quote">I have never wanted anything so badly in my entire life. Still in total command of my waking consciousness, I calmly sit down and make a strong, fervent wish. 'Please let them come to me.'</span>
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-049"></span>**Dennett's visits to the Akashic Library — a Roman-pillared marble structure in a brilliantly lit dimensional space — allowed him to view his past lives flashing by in rapid succession and glimpse a single future-life image: a bald, silver-eyed ageless man of extraordinary wisdom, possibly not from this planet.**
    - His mother guided him to the library entrance, then told him he must go in alone; inside, crystal-and-marble walls held screens displaying his past lives too rapidly for detail but communicating their multiplicity.
    - A second visit required him to physically tear through a barrier — like 'Knocking on Heaven's Door' — ripping open a hole that flooded with divine light, beyond which lay the library overlooking a diamond-glinting lake.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-050"></span>**A past-life OBE in September 1997 placed Dennett inside the experience of a young woman walking alone on a rural road at night as a car stopped ahead of her — flooded with the certainty that she was about to be raped and killed — demonstrating that past-life revisitation can be traumatic enough to require aborting the experience.**
    - He was not merely observing but fully embodied as the woman, feeling her vulnerability and terror while simultaneously trying to identify the car's make and model to date the memory.
    - Robert Monroe, Bruce Moen, and Marilyn Hughes have all documented past-life discovery via OBE; Dennett's preliminary past-life hints appeared first as powerful recurring dreams of slavery, the Holocaust, and Native American boyhood.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-051"></span>**Dennett's spirit guide revealed itself not as a person but as a coyote — a classic Native American trickster figure — that darted across the road behind him, stalking low and emanating mischief, love, and distant pride; he also encountered it on subsequent attempts where it easily outran him.**
    - The instruction to look behind oneself during an OBE to find one's guide came from Bruce Moen's books; Monroe, Moen, and Wilson had all reported arms, faces, or figures guiding their journeys from behind.
    - Immediately after the coyote sighting, a bird flew directly into Dennett's face and through his head — ending the OBE — which he interpreted as the trickster guide's humor at work.

## OBEs and Psychic Powers

Regular OBE practice produces an involuntary awakening of psychic abilities — especially precognition — as the boundary between dream and waking reality becomes permeable; Dennett's most dramatic example was waking twice before a double earthquake struck near Big Bear, California, and then dreaming the exact boulder-blocked road scene shown minutes later on television news.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-052"></span>**Virtually every major OBE practitioner — Garfield, Monroe, Peterson, Moen, Bruce, Taylor, Muldoon — reports that developing astral travel ability triggers a flood of psychic experiences, particularly precognition, as a consistent side effect rather than an exception.**
    - "I had asked for out-of-body experiences, not psychic experiences. Somehow I had gotten them both. Somehow I was becoming psychic, whether I wanted it or not." —Robert Peterson
    - Muldoon wrote: 'Scarcely a week goes by without some future-contemplating dream' — suggesting precognition becomes essentially continuous once the astral faculty is activated.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-053"></span>**Dennett woke spontaneously and walked onto his balcony seconds before the first of two earthquakes struck near Big Bear, California (magnitude 6.5), and then dreamed the exact image of boulders blocking a mountain road that appeared on television news shortly afterward.**
    - A coworker who was physically in Big Bear that night reported unaccountable anxiety preventing sleep all night — and then spent hours in one of the cars picking through the boulder-strewn road exactly as Dennett had dreamed.
    - This sequence — precognitive wakening, earthquake, precognitive dream, televised confirmation — convinced Dennett that his dream state was accessing real future events, not generating random imagery.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-054"></span>**Bleed-through between dream and waking states creates genuine perceptual confusion: Dennett once heard his sister's voice calling 'Preston, wake up!' both in a dream and immediately upon waking, only to realize it was a neighbor's dog barking — his dreaming mind had translated the sound and the translation persisted into waking consciousness.**
    - On another occasion, erotic sensations from a dream continued for several seconds after genuine waking — the feeling of being caressed by an invisible presence exactly replicating what would conventionally be called ghostly molestation.
    - These bleed-through experiences show that the membrane between states is genuinely permeable rather than absolute, and that dream-state perceptions carry physical-level realism.

## How to Have OBEs and What to Do When You Succeed

Dennett presents a structured practical guide to inducing, maintaining, and directing OBEs — covering nine launch techniques, four control strategies, a developmental taxonomy from novice to expert, and a menu of experiments — arguing that conscious astral projection follows universal patterns and is an inevitable phase of human spiritual evolution.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-055"></span>**The three foundational steps to induce an OBE are: (1) complete physical relaxation until the body feels heavy, numb, or vibrating; (2) quieting mental chatter until thoughts become hypnagogic images; and (3) strongly intending, visualizing, and desiring to leave the body — all three must be simultaneous.**
    - Most people will experience pre-OBE sensations (vertigo, heaviness, lightness, or vibrations) during their first three attempts; it is not unusual to have an OBE on the first try.
    - Memory is the real key — the fact that we all project nightly but forget means that improving dream recall is as important as any induction technique.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-056"></span>**Nine specific launch methods are presented — vibrations, running ('The Flash'), spinning, boat-rocking, rope-climbing, rolling out, elevator visualization, cliff-jumping, and lucid dream projection — each exploiting the astral body's responsiveness to imagination and movement visualization.**
    - The running/'Flash' method — imagining running at superhero speed while fully relaxed — worked for Dennett by propelling him directly out and running down an astral street.
    - Lucid dream projection (asking 'Am I dreaming?' throughout the waking day until the habit carries into dreams) is presented as the most reliable route for people who struggle with direct induction.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-057"></span>**Once out of body, four control challenges must be managed: emotional overwhelm (meditate, breathe, analyze); collapsing awareness (spin, shout 'More awareness!' or use Robert Bruce's running commentary); blindness (shout 'Clarity!', shake the head, slowly open/close eyes); and movement (look at destination and will yourself there rather than trying to physically walk).**
    - To move through objects, simply gather courage and walk through a wall; if you bounce off, remind yourself that nothing can hurt you and try again.
    - To visit a specific person, shout their name, visualize them, or simply fly toward them — thought-directed travel is faster and more reliable than spatial navigation.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-058"></span>**Dennett proposes a four-stage developmental taxonomy: Novice (1–10 OBEs, occasional pre-OBE sensations, difficulties with all basic skills); Intermediate (10–100 OBEs, regular induction, one-minute sustained OBE); Advanced (100–1,000+ OBEs, full skill control, one-hour OBEs, verified evidence and higher dimensions); Expert (thousands of OBEs, waking projection capability, supernormal powers, possible immortality).**
    - Male projectors appear to outnumber female projectors in published accounts, but this may reflect publication bias rather than actual gender difference in ability.
    - About half of all projectors began spontaneously; half actively pursued OBEs. About 70 percent report sleep paralysis. Nearly all report psychic events.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-059"></span>**Conscious astral travel is argued to be evolutionarily inevitable: as humanity evolves spiritually, more people will spontaneously begin projecting, because death itself is a permanent OBE that everyone will eventually experience, making learning the skill in advance profoundly practical.**
    - "OBE is way too powerful and meaningful to be accidental. So it seems logical to suggest that conscious OBE may be an important part of the spiritual evolutionary process." —Robert Bruce
    - The fact that we die means that one day, we will all have a permanent out-of-body experience — making conscious preparation for that state both logical and urgent.

## Questions and Answers

In a FAQ format, Dennett addresses the most common concerns about OBEs — including safety, universality, verification, and time investment — consistently arguing that OBEs are natural, harmless, and accessible to anyone, with the only real barrier being the effort required to develop memory and awareness.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-060"></span>**OBEs are definitively not dangerous — no reported case exists of anyone being physically harmed — and cannot result in getting locked out of one's body, possessed, or stranded too far away; on the contrary, they promote physical and spiritual health.**
    - It is impossible to be hurt while out of body because you are nonphysical. Nor can you get too far away from your body, or locked out, or possessed.
    - While some people have reported scary experiences, there is very little evidence of any actual danger; the evidence supports the opposite — that OBEs promote health, both spiritual and physical.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-061"></span>**Any person regardless of culture, age, sex, race, education, health, or belief can have OBEs — the sole requirement is desire combined with time and effort — and most people will experience pre-OBE sensations within their first three attempts.**
    - Interestingly, many reported projectors are strongly left-brained — businessmen, computer programmers, engineers, analysts, accountants — suggesting the practice is not limited to spiritually inclined or mystically oriented individuals.
    - The development of astral projection follows a recognizable pattern across virtually all practitioners, suggesting it is a natural human faculty rather than a rare gift.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-062"></span>**Proof that OBEs are not merely dreams accumulates as the projector gains control: verifiable information — details observed out of body and confirmed physically — distinguishes genuine OBEs from lucid dreams, and virtually every advanced projector has obtained such personal proof.**
    - The five-step pattern of a fully conscious projection — relaxation, exit sensations, separation, consciousness perceived as external to body, return — differs structurally from the dream cycle.
    - Projectors who wake up after OBE travel feel tremendously energized and refreshed rather than tired, the opposite of what would be expected if the experience were merely vivid dreaming.

## Epilogue

Looking back across nearly twenty years of OBE practice, Dennett identifies the core lessons — life after death is real, privacy is an illusion, we are all interconnected, and thought has far-reaching effects — while reporting ongoing practice at roughly four OBEs per month and expressing confidence that conscious astral travel will become increasingly common as human spiritual evolution progresses.

* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-063"></span>**Two decades of OBE practice transformed Dennett from a hard skeptic who dismissed life after death as impossible to someone who considers it empirically verified through repeated visitation of his deceased mother and pets, leaving him with absolute certainty that consciousness survives death.**
    - A psychic consulted by Dennett's sister-in-law Christy — before Dennett had any OBE ability — spontaneously said he should try OBEs because he would be very good at them, a prediction that proved accurate.
    - Core lessons learned: privacy is an illusion; the universe is far vaster than imaginable; beliefs unsupported by experience lead to delusion; thoughts and emotions have far-reaching effects.
* <span id="out-of-body-exploring-a-beginners-approach-064"></span>**Dennett argues that conscious astral travel will become increasingly common in the general population because it is a naturally occurring human condition that activates as people follow their paths of spiritual evolution — not a rare talent but an inevitable developmental milestone.**
    - "OBE is way too powerful and meaningful to be accidental... As mankind evolves, I believe the OBE factor will steadily become a more profound and meaningful part of life, on the individual as well as on the species-growth level." —Robert Bruce
    - Dennett reports continued progress in contacting his coyote spirit guide and was recently pulled out of body by two small hands belonging to a young female voice saying 'Come' — suggesting his practice continues to develop.
