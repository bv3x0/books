## Introduction

Preston Dennett recounts how his investigation of UFO reports in southern California beginning in 1986 revealed a remarkable concentration of unidentified submersible object (USO) cases centered in the Santa Catalina Channel, eventually leading to a Fate Magazine article, multiple History Channel appearances, and a decades-long accumulation of evidence pointing toward a possible alien undersea base.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-001"></span>**The Santa Catalina Channel, stretching from the Channel Islands near Santa Barbara south to San Clemente, is one of the highest-density USO reporting areas in the world, with accounts dating back to at least the 1920s–1930s.**
    - Wishar Cerve documented reports of peculiarly shaped boats that flew out of this region and then returned to the Pacific in the 1920s–1930s.
    - Ann Druffel, investigating since 1945, documented recurring cloud-cigars, hovering spheres, and at least one underwater UFO with uniformed occupants in the channel over a thirty-year period.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-002"></span>**Dennett's 2006 Fate Magazine cover article 'Is There an Undersea UFO Base off the California Coast?' triggered a massive public response, went viral internationally, and catalyzed additional witness reports that deepened the investigation.**
    - The article was lifted from the magazine and republished across the internet in multiple languages, commented on in mainstream newspapers, and prompted radio, television, and convention speaking requests.
    - The History Channel's Deep Sea UFOs episode, inspired by the same research, earned record ratings beating Ice Road Truckers, then their highest-rated show.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-003"></span>**In May 2014, Google Earth images of what appeared to be a massive artificial undersea structure directly off the Malibu coast—at the center of the USO activity zone—reignited global attention and seemed to confirm the undersea base hypothesis.**
    - Radio host Jimmy Church alerted Dennett to the images, which showed what appeared to be an undersea platform with pillar-like structures and an apparent entrance.
    - Dennett's subsequent MUFON lecture about the activity earned more than 80,000 views on UFO-TV.

## UFOs over the Santa Catalina Channel

Dennett catalogs decades of credible UFO sightings over the waters of the Santa Catalina Channel—from the 1942 Battle of Los Angeles through the 1980s—demonstrating that the area has been a persistent, high-volume UFO zone with military, law enforcement, and civilian witnesses, and that these aerial objects are inextricably linked to the USO activity below.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-004"></span>**The 1942 Battle of Los Angeles established the channel as a UFO zone when a large circular object hovered over Culver City for five hours while the Army fired 1,430 rounds at it without effect, before it slowly moved back out to sea.**
    - Researcher Robert Wood claims new documents show the military successfully shot down at least one object and that it was extraterrestrial, not Japanese, in origin.
    - The object was illuminated by searchlights and photographed over Culver City at approximately 2:00 a.m. on February 25, 1942.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-005"></span>**Multiple military pilots and personnel made close-range UFO observations over the channel in the 1940s and 1950s, with craft consistently outperforming known aircraft in speed and maneuverability.**
    - Army Air Force pilot Gerry Casey observed an orange, circular, fifty-foot object fly in perfect formation alongside his BT-13 trainer on April 5, 1943, before it accelerated away over the ocean at impossible speed.
    - In January 1953, retired Navy Lt. Commander Rex Hardy Jr. and two crewmen sighted four circular, aluminum-colored objects the size of a B-36 moving at approximately 1,200 mph over Malibu.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-006"></span>**Mass sightings involving hundreds of civilian witnesses, including 300 boy scouts on Catalina Island in 1952, demonstrate that this is not a marginal phenomenon limited to isolated individuals.**
    - A metallic, lens-shaped object with portholes hovered 500–800 feet above Camp Fox and darkened the surrounding sky in a purple-blue halo effect, observed by 350 people and acknowledged by Colonel Charles W. Bicking at March Air Force Base.
    - Researcher Marc Davenport cited the strange optical darkening effect as evidence that UFOs may be surrounded by anomalous energy fields.
* <span class="pull-quote">As can be seen from the above cases, the southern California coast is thick with UFOs. A surprisingly high number of cases come from military officers including a Navy commander, pilots, radar operators, an infantryman, not to mention several police officers. Testimony from so many credible sources becomes hard to ignore.</span>
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-007"></span>**Radar confirmation of unidentified objects in the channel area—tracked at speeds up to 3,600 mph—rules out conventional aircraft and establishes the phenomenon as physically real rather than perceptual.**
    - On March 23, 1957, four large round objects flashing red lights were tracked by CAA radar over Long Beach; an F-86 pursuer watched them travel thirty miles in thirty seconds before a security lid was clamped on the case.
    - Navy radar operator Austin Roberts observed five objects form a V-formation off Catalina in 1957, simultaneously confirmed by surface and air radar at 5,000 feet altitude, with no approach visible on radar prior to their appearance.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-008"></span>**Anomalous clouds—stationary, shape-retaining masses that emit light, expand, and maneuver unlike any natural weather phenomenon—constitute a recurring and distinctive sub-category of channel UFO activity investigated by Ann Druffel beginning in 1962.**
    - In August 1962, Druffel observed a rectangular cloud remain stationary for 45 minutes, then rapidly expand to 20–30 times its original size while emitting strong metallic reflections and appearing 'solid-appearing, unlike a normal cloud.'
    - In July 1968, multiple independent witnesses across Long Beach reported a diamond-shaped glowing mass accompanied by smaller maneuvering oval objects with 'precise edges,' with witness Castano describing the scene as 'some kind of war.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-009"></span>**Boaters are disproportionately targeted by UFOs in the channel, with objects repeatedly hovering directly over individual vessels—a behavioral pattern suggesting intelligent interest in maritime activity.**
    - In July 1955, Mr. and Mrs. George Washington watched a gray-white rotating craft hover directly over their yacht for six minutes, then maintain position above them as they restarted and continued toward Catalina Island.
    - In July 1987, a sailor witnessed an object hover silently over his sailboat before moving off toward Catalina; in 1970 a similar object flew only hundreds of feet above another vessel.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-010"></span>**The visual variety of objects reported—saucers, cigars, triangles, glowing orbs, V-shapes, bar-lights, and anomalous clouds—suggests either multiple types of craft or highly adaptable technology rather than a single secret program.**
    - Engineer Frederic Hehr witnessed an entire 'squadron' of brilliant white bars form a diamond pattern around a diffuse central object over Santa Monica in August 1953, lasting ten minutes.
    - In 1986, a witness near the Palos Verdes Peninsula described a dull gray, highly angular object with rectangular opaque panels that appeared incapable of flight yet moved silently at 30 mph—matching no known aircraft before or since.

Dennett documents over two dozen additional high-quality UFO sightings above the Santa Catalina Channel from 1990 to 2017, showing that the phenomenon did not diminish but intensified, involving pilots, military veterans, photographers, and mass witnesses, while photographic and video evidence accumulated alongside consistent behavioral patterns including boater-targeting and unexplained electromagnetic effects.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-011"></span>**Pilot and flight instructor Toshi Inouye's 1990 sighting of a glowing red cigar-shaped object two to three times the size of a bus over Santa Monica—which vanished while under observation by accelerating away at extreme speed—adds trained aeronautical testimony to the coastal record.**

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-015"></span>**The 2010 'mystery missile' incident—in which a glowing contrail rising from the Santa Catalina Channel was filmed by news cameras but officially explained away as a jet contrail despite expert military opinion to the contrary—demonstrates active cover-up of anomalous activity in the channel.**

## Into the Ocean

Dennett documents over thirty USO incidents in the Santa Catalina Channel from the 1920s through the late 1980s, establishing that unidentified submersible objects have been entering, exiting, and operating within the channel's waters for nearly a century, with cases involving military vessels, commercial ships, civilian boaters, scuba divers, and beachgoers.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-017"></span>**The February 1956 Redondo Beach USO—a glowing 'ball of fire' that glided into the ocean and remained visible while submerged until lifeguards rowed out to investigate—represents one of the best-documented early USO cases, with dozens of witnesses including police officers, lifeguards, and residents whose testimony was subsequently covered up by a ludicrous official explanation.**
    - Lifeguard Lieutenant Frank Rodecker rowed out and observed the object still glowing 'about fifteen feet across' beneath the surface before it winked out, finding no radiation when tested.
    - UFO researcher Idabelle Epperson noted the official 'flashlight' explanation failed to account for the gliding motion, the 20+ minutes of floating before sinking, or the object's large size as described by multiple witnesses.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-018"></span>**The 1947 Navy survey ship Maury's encounter with a 'phantom reef' that moved between locations in the Pacific—tracked by echo sounders that found the mass only to have it disappear and reappear elsewhere—is the first documented case of the military tracking what may have been a submerged USO.**
    - "Captain Hambling reported that their echo sounders detected a mass approximately 1,600 yards away; when they changed course toward it, 'four hundred yards away from it, we found it had vanished.'" —Captain Hambling
    - The mysterious mass had been reported by multiple ships as a 'reef' or 'submarine mountain' that appeared overnight, and the incident preceded a dramatic rise in USO reports off the southern California coast.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-019"></span>**Multiple witnesses in the 1960s and 1970s observed USOs entering or exiting the water at locations throughout the channel, with the Topanga Beach case of 1972 being particularly compelling because witnesses used binoculars and a telescope to track a disk that hovered, submerged, glowed underwater, then shot toward Catalina Island at extreme speed from a dead stop.**
    - Robert Snyder observed a thick, multi-colored disk hover silently 200 feet above the water near Topanga Canyon Beach, descend into the ocean where its glow was visible through telescope, then 'in a split second shot off at an incredible speed from a standstill.'
    - Snyder, who later flew helicopters and fixed-wing aircraft, concluded: 'There isn't anything that could even remotely function like that.'
* <span class="pull-quote">Something is lurking around in our oceans and people want to know what it is!</span>
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-020"></span>**A senior electronics engineer's encounter with a 300-foot-diameter fluorescent green underwater object that passed directly beneath his 38-foot sailboat between Santa Barbara and Santa Cruz Islands—disabling his compasses, depth sounders, and radio as it passed—demonstrates that USOs can cause measurable electromagnetic effects on nearby vessels.**
    - The witness determined the object was approximately 100 feet deep using depth soundings, then found all three of his compasses slowly rotating and unable to contact the Coast Guard due to radio failure.
    - Post-incident inspection revealed that all compass mountings were physically broken—material evidence of an electromagnetic or physical force.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-021"></span>**Navy radar operator Gary Wagoner's 1983 sighting of a half-moon-shaped light changing size and intensity off Catalina from the U.S.S. Long Beach—confirmed visually by 15–20 shipmates but absent from radar—demonstrates that USOs can be simultaneously visible and radar-invisible, suggesting advanced stealth capability.**
    - When Wagoner asked the radar operators what they saw, they reported 'no surface contact'; when he asked the bridge, the sailor replied the bridge officers were 'too busy looking at the object themselves.'
    - Wagoner reported a second incident in the Indian Ocean where fast-moving lights crossing the horizon in eight seconds prompted an officer to respond: 'You did not see anything.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-022"></span>**The 'mothership with baby USOs' pattern observed in Marina Del Rey in 1989–1990—where a large 100-foot light spawned smaller 10–12-foot objects that moved swiftly under the ocean surface—suggests an operational hierarchy among USOs consistent with a local base.**
    - Investigator Bill Hamilton reported that witnesses observed as many as twenty events per hour, with the larger light generating smaller ones that moved swiftly 500–1000 feet from the coastline at Abalone Cove.
    - At least one of the smaller lights was reported to have emerged from the water.

## A Deepening Mystery

The 1990s and 2000s brought an upsurge of USO encounters in the Santa Catalina Channel, including increasingly dramatic cases involving USOs targeting boats, ejecting material, affecting nearby instruments, and responding to official investigation, while Dennett's Fate Magazine article and History Channel appearances generated a cascade of new reports confirming the area as an extraordinary USO hotspot.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-023"></span>**Ex-Navy submariner Paul Castardo's encounter off San Simeon with a tanker-sized object displaying banks of stadium lights—which moved to stay ahead of his vessel, appeared on radar while visually defying identification, and caused erroneous depth gauge readings of 15 feet in deep water—demonstrates that USOs can actively engage and deceive nearby vessels.**
    - Castardo, a trained submarine navigator with 8,000 miles of submarine experience, stated the vessel had no port or starboard running lights, no hull shape discernible, approximately 30–40 pulsating orange lights, and appeared 400–600 feet long.
    - When Castardo tried to skirt around it, the object appeared one mile ahead on radar within a minute or two, impossible given the distances involved.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-024"></span>**UFOs ejecting 'molten metal'-like material over the channel—reported independently by multiple witnesses in 2004 and 2009—represent a distinctive behavioral sub-category with no conventional aerospace explanation.**
    - On October 2, 2004, a witness off Huntington Beach watched an orange light eject what looked like molten metal five or six times as it moved across the sky.
    - Matthew of Huntington Beach witnessed a silver, rounded 'Stealth Bomber'-shaped craft on Christmas night 2009 discharge three burning objects that fell into the ocean before zipping toward Long Beach in three seconds.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-025"></span>**David Russo's 2006 encounter in Long Beach—in which a blue triangle hovered 15–25 feet above the water, illuminated the ocean with blue glow while rotating, then accelerated toward Catalina at extraordinary speed—is paradigmatic of the channel's USO pattern and was compelling enough to lead Russo to obsessively monitor the area afterward.**

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-026"></span>**Nancy Bradley's 2009 photographic capture of a white object hovering then diving into the sea near Catalina—accompanied by hundreds of panicked dolphins and later confirmed by local island residents as a recurring phenomenon—constitutes multiple-witness, photographic, and behavioral evidence for USO activity.**

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-027"></span>**A 2012 observation in which a Scripps Institute of Oceanography research vessel positioned itself for days at the exact ocean coordinates where a witness had seen a sparkling light enter the water suggests the U.S. government is actively and covertly investigating USO activity in the channel.**
    - Witness 'Mike' observed a sparkling light enter the ocean between Catalina and Palos Verdes around 11:00 p.m. on September 3, 2012; approximately five hours later a 300-foot Scripps vessel appeared at that exact location and remained for days.

## The Mass Sightings

The Santa Catalina Channel area has produced several extraordinary mass sighting events involving hundreds of craft simultaneously—most notably the June 14, 1992 Topanga Canyon wave and the June 26, 2013 Catalina Island boy scout encounter—events so extreme in scale that Dennett argues only an undersea base can explain where so many objects are originating.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-028"></span>**On June 14, 1992, more than twenty witnesses in Topanga Canyon observed waves of 40–200 UFOs over more than two hours, with screen-writers Daniel and Katherine counting approximately 200 objects emerging from below a nearby ridge in rhythmic pulses—and at least one witness at Venice Beach simultaneously observed a stadium-sized green glow emerge from the ocean and launch objects inland.**
    - Katherine counted approximately 40 objects in each of at least five waves and said: 'There were so many of them...it was not good news. I didn't sleep that night and I was upset for weeks after that.'
    - Venice Beach witness Allen, who had national security connections, heard volumes of water splashing back into the ocean as the craft emerged, saw 'a bright large vessel, plus smaller vessels,' and confirmed via his security connections that what he observed was a 'USO.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-029"></span>**On June 26, 2013, boy scout Alan Bergholdt and at least twenty other scouts at Catalina Island's Parsons Landing witnessed approximately 200 fast-moving craft over two hours, launched in pulses from four large stationary pyramid-sphere objects—with one craft semi-submerged in the ocean communicating in synchronized light pulses with another hovering above—and cameras drained of power during the event.**
    - Bergholdt described a dozen variants of craft including cigar-shaped, pyramidal, U-shaped, stingray-shaped, and triangular, some breaking apart into smaller triangles; all were completely silent and moved at roughly ten times the speed of a supersonic jet.
    - A bizarre optical effect accompanied the display: witnesses could see the Pacific Coast Highway with individual cars on it from 90 miles away, as if looking through a telescope, an effect that ceased when the objects departed.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-030"></span>**Less than one month after the Catalina Island mass sighting, a Santa Monica resident observed 'a massive formation of bright, ascending, glittering silent lights' rising from the ocean—up to thirty at a time—confirming that the June 2013 activity was not isolated.**
    - The witness described 'a phalanx of spectacular bright, golden lights, sometimes as many as thirty, ascending slowly over the ocean. They shot up silent, three or four at a time, in a wide formation, and disappeared into the sky.'

    - General Telephone employee Richard Keyhoe and two other drivers observed an 'egg-shaped' object in a 'blue haze' resting on the beach; the entities returned to the craft which took off and disappeared, after which all car engines restarted spontaneously.

    - Consciously, she only remembered seeing a huge oval craft reflecting both ocean and sky, human-appearing beings calling her by the name 'Oora,' and waking alone on the beach hours later with her ring missing and her body extremely sore.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-033"></span>**Noah Felice's January 1980 encounter in which his plane was struck by a beam from a USO off Catalina, pulled into the craft, and crashed into the ocean—killing his cousin while Felice survived 20 minutes underwater without cuts or scratches—represents a direct fatality connected to USO activity in the channel.**

## The Undersea UFO Base

Dennett presents the cumulative case for an extraterrestrial undersea base in the Santa Catalina Channel, examining the Google Earth 'Malibu anomaly' (Sycamore Knoll) as a potential candidate, weighing competing geological and extraterrestrial interpretations, and concluding that while the anomaly's artificial nature remains unproven, the totality of USO evidence—including abductee testimony of underground facilities—strongly implies a base exists somewhere in the region.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-036"></span>**Two abductee accounts describing underground facilities rather than craft interiors—Paul Nelson's 1967 Catalina abduction to a 'round-walled room' with rock-like walls, and Kim Carlsberg's 1992 recall of a vast lobby filled with milling human abductees—constitute the strongest internal evidence for an actual undersea or underground base in the region.**
    - Nelson recalled being placed on a table in 'a round-walled room...more underground than onboard a ship,' with rock-like faceted walls, examined by praying mantis-type beings in tight jumpsuits.
    - Carlsberg recalled 'waking up in a lobby where many humans were milling around' and later sitting in a large auditorium with other abductees being 'prepared for something,' in what appeared to be a vast underground complex.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-037"></span>**The 'Malibu anomaly' (Sycamore Knoll), a roughly 2–3-mile-wide flat-topped formation approximately 2,000 feet below the surface off Point Dume, sits at GPS coordinates 34°1'23.31"N, 118°59'45.64"W—almost exactly in the center of the highest-density USO activity zone, making it the leading candidate for a physical base location.**
    - Robert Stanley first identified the formation in 2009 on Google Maps as 'an ancient artificial platform,' noting its perfectly flat top and what appeared to be columns at its base.
    - On May 15, 2014, Mark D. Kovar's press release announced the discovery as 'a massive underwater entrance...1.35 miles by 2.45 miles wide, 6.66 miles from land, with an entrance 2,745 feet wide and 630 feet tall' with a '500-foot-thick ceiling.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-038"></span>**Geological experts, former FBI agent Ben Hansen, and MUFON investigators who examined the Malibu anomaly from multiple angles—including a physical submersible dive by the Lowe Files team in 2017—concluded it is a natural thrust fault, with the apparent pillars and tunnel entrance being artifacts of Google Earth's image-patchworking process.**
    - USGS earthquake geologist David Schwartz identified the formation as Sycamore Knoll resulting from a thrust fault, saying 'There's nothing unnatural-looking about it—it's just showing some sort of variation in the offshore coastal morphology.'
    - MUFON investigator Chase Kloetzke, after physical exploration via submersible, wrote: 'No evidence of an opening nor pillars was visible...nothing was found that would indicate or allow suspicions of man-made artifacts or construction.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-039"></span>**Strange radio signals in the 54-kilocycle range, detected by a Navy-trained ham radio operator in Malibu and traceable by range-finder to the exact location of the underwater anomaly, add an independent electronic anomaly to the base evidence—and when incorporated into music by Merrell Fankhauser, caused four radio stations to shut down.**
    - Fankhauser reported that a Point Mugu Naval Base contact, when asked about the anomaly and strange signals on live radio, said 'I can't talk about that' and immediately hung up.
    - Fankhauser compared the signals to recordings made by UFO contactee Billy Meier in the 1970s and found them 'almost the same.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-040"></span>**An insider report from retired Hughes Aircraft classified contractor 'Martha,' who matter-of-factly stated that the military works with Grays in 'an underwater base in the San Pedro Basin near Catalina Island,' provides rare alleged government-insider confirmation of the base's existence and its extraterrestrial connection.**
    - Martha told her contact that much of the information about ET contact was already available on the internet, suggesting disclosure was incremental and deliberate.
    - Researcher Agent K of The Object Report described Martha as 'a highly intelligent professional who was employed in a classified position with a military contractor who supports the theory of a possible USO base near Catalina Island in a very matter-of-fact manner.'

Dennett presents a selection of previously unpublished USO cases from outside the Santa Catalina Channel—including encounters in Puget Sound, the Bahamas, the Atlantic off Halifax, the Dominican Republic, Lake Miccosukee in Florida, and Lake Hamilton in Arkansas—demonstrating that USOs are a global phenomenon and that the behaviors observed in the Santa Catalina Channel (vessel-targeting, light displays, missing time, sonar invisibility) recur worldwide.

Boyne.**
    - "The deck officer asked how to write up the contact; Commander Boyne replied: 'Officers who write up contacts like this do not get to advance in rank.'" —Commander Peter B. Boyne

    - Sanders noted the statistical improbability: 'By the laws of chance, for it to go directly under my boat—it's pretty thin. I feel like it came by to take a look.'
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-043"></span>**Mark Halverson's 1952 Lake Hamilton, Arkansas encounter—in which a 25–30-foot black, tapered saucer rose from the lake beside his boat, held the boat immobile despite a wide-open engine, and caused approximately four hours of missing time for all four witnesses—demonstrates that USO and missing-time abductions occur in freshwater lakes, not only coastal saltwater zones.**
    - When Mark jammed the accelerator to full speed, the boat 'just sat there and it wouldn't go, like something was holding it'—the next memory was being 200–300 yards away at 50 mph with everyone still screaming.
    - Twenty years later, when Mark ran into witness Randy, their exchange about the incident was brief and emotionally loaded; Randy died of cancer before any formal investigation could occur, taking his testimony with him.

## Conclusions

Dennett synthesizes more than 140 documented cases to argue that the Santa Catalina Channel UFO/USO phenomenon cannot be attributed to military technology, hoax, or misperception, and that the totality of evidence—the longevity, scale, witness diversity, humanoid contact cases, and behavioral consistency of the objects—points overwhelmingly to extraterrestrial origin and strongly suggests the existence of an undersea base.

* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-044"></span>**The witness roster for Santa Catalina Channel encounters—spanning Navy commanders, radar operators, submarine navigators, Air Force pilots, Coast Guard captains, city police officers, lifeguards, lawyers, doctors, musicians, boy scouts, and ordinary residents—constitutes a cross-section too diverse and credible to dismiss as hoax or collective misperception.**
    - Many of the most credible witnesses—trained military observers familiar with advanced aircraft—consistently stated they had never seen anything like what they observed, ruling out misidentification of known craft.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-045"></span>**Dennett presents five reasons the activity cannot be attributed to secret military technology: witnesses report non-human occupants; the craft far exceed known aircraft performance; military craft are not tested over dense populations; military jets are seen pursuing these objects; and the activity dates to the 1920s when U.S. military technology was still in its infancy.**
    - The military tests secret aircraft in remote areas like Area 51, not over Long Beach, Malibu, and Newport Beach; the pattern of civilians being buzzed, cars chased, and beams of light directed at individuals is incompatible with secret test operations.
    - The 1942 Battle of Los Angeles predates any conceivable American secret aerospace program capable of producing hovering circular craft impervious to 1,430 rounds of anti-aircraft ammunition.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-046"></span>**The Navy's documented 1965 contract with Convair/Electric Boat for a flying submarine capable of 225-knot airspeed and 10-knot underwater travel—with a 75-foot depth capability—shows the military has long pursued USO-like technology, but even this advanced concept falls far short of the performance parameters witnessed in the channel, ruling it out as a complete explanation.**
    - Eugene Handler's 1965 paper in the U.S. Naval Institute Proceedings confirmed that 'the Bureau of Naval Weapons has recently awarded a contract' for flying submarine development, with $36,000 then equivalent to over $250,000 today.
    - After more than 50 years of development, any operational flying submarine would still not account for the reported speeds of thousands of MPH, right-angle turns, and the hundreds of simultaneous objects seen in mass sightings.
* <span id="undersea-ufo-base-047"></span>**The surrounding military infrastructure—Seal Beach Naval Weapons Station, Point Mugu Naval Base, Vandenburg AFB, and San Clemente Island Naval Reservation—while suggesting heightened military awareness and interest in the channel, actually strengthens the ET hypothesis because so many military witnesses report genuine puzzlement at what they observe.**
    - San Clemente Island's navigational chart contains a warning of 'local magnetic disturbance' with variations 'as much as five degrees from normal variation' observed up to three miles offshore—a physical anomaly consistent with USO activity.
    - Catalina Island was used as a classified military base 1943–1945 for secret underwater operations and amphibious commando training, establishing a precedent for covert activity in the channel that continues.
