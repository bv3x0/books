# Revolt Against the Modern World

## Metadata
- Thesis: Modern civilization represents the complete inversion and systematic destruction of traditional principles that once organized human societies around transcendent spiritual realities, leading to an unprecedented dark age of materialism, egalitarianism, and spiritual darkness.
- Topics: traditional hierarchy and caste systems, spiritual authority versus temporal power, cyclical decline through four ages, masculine solar spirituality versus feminine lunar materialism, counter-initiation and organized antitradition
- Categories: philosophy, religion, political science
- Collections: Magic
- Author: Julius Evola
- Year: 1934
- Mode: quick

## The Beginning

The traditional worldview rests on the fundamental doctrine of the "two natures" - a distinction between the physical/mortal realm and a metaphysical/immortal realm of pure being.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-001"></span>**Traditional man's expanded reality**: Unlike modern materialism, traditional man directly experienced invisible, spiritual dimensions as more real than physical phenomena
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-002"></span>**The sacred vs. the supernatural**: Traditional "nature" included invisible forces and spiritual realities; the supernatural was simply a higher degree of nature
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-003"></span>**Doctrine of being vs. becoming**: The superior realm represents stable being; the inferior realm represents flux, change, and impermanence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-004"></span>**Liberation through asceticism**: Traditional path involved mastery over desire and identification with temporal forms to achieve transcendence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-005"></span>**Symbolic geography of transcendence**: Solar symbols, heights, islands, and mountain peaks represented the realm of being
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-006"></span>**Two births and two deaths**: Possibility of spiritual rebirth and achieving immortality, versus dissolution into mere shadows

## Regality

True traditional kingship embodied a supernatural, non-human quality that served as a bridge between the temporal and transcendent realms.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-007"></span>**The pontifex as bridge-builder**: Kings served as living connections between natural and supernatural dimensions
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-008"></span>**Divine origin of authority**: Royal power derived not from human consent or force, but from metaphysical sources - kings were manifestations of divine beings
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-009"></span>**Solar symbolism of kingship**: Kings represented the same "glory" and "victory" as the sun's daily triumph over darkness
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-010"></span>**The hvareno (royal glory)**: Persian concept of supernatural fire that allowed kings to partake of immortality and granted victory
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-011"></span>**Fighting Horus**: Egyptian pharaohs embodied the victorious solar principle through ritual reenactment of divine victories
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-012"></span>**Chinese mandate of heaven**: Emperors received supernatural force that acts without acting (wei wu wei) and affects subjects through mere presence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-013"></span>**Ritual foundation**: Kings' primary function was performing sacrificial actions that maintained cosmic order and community blessing
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-014"></span>**Beyond moralistic limitations**: Traditional royal dignity transcended conventional morality, operating according to metaphysical rather than ethical principles

## Polar Symbolism; the Lord of Peace and Justice

The universal king (cakravartin) represents the archetypal center around which traditional civilization organizes itself, embodying both cosmic order and spiritual transcendence.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-015"></span>**The unmoved mover**: Like the polestar around which other stars revolve, the true king provides stability through spiritual centrality rather than material force
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-016"></span>**Cakravartin as dharmarāja**: The universal king serves as "Lord of the Law" who establishes cosmic order and divine justice
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-017"></span>**Sacred geography of the center**: Traditional centers (ziggurats, palaces, temples) architecturally expressed hierarchical cosmic order with the throne at the apex
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-018"></span>**Initiation and enthronement**: Ritual coronation involved ascending through planetary spheres to reach the realm of fixed stars, symbolizing transcendence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-019"></span>**Stability as divine attribute**: Royal stability (djed in Egyptian) represents both inner spiritual equilibrium and cosmic order
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-020"></span>**Peace as transcendent activity**: Royal peace differs from mere absence of conflict - it represents the highest perfection of withdrawn, pure activity
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-021"></span>**Melchizedek principle**: The mysterious king-priest of Salem embodies the synthesis of temporal and spiritual authority that transcends ordinary religious limitations
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-022"></span>**Justice as cosmic principle**: Traditional justice (dharma) means each being fulfilling its proper nature within the hierarchical order, not abstract equality

## The Law, the State, the Empire

Traditional law and governance derive their legitimacy from transcendent rather than human sources, creating organic rather than mechanistic social order.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-023"></span>**Transcendent realism**: Law must have divine character to be legitimate - purely human legislation lacks true authority and binding power
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-024"></span>**Ṛta as cosmic law**: In Vedic tradition, law (ṛta) means truth, reality, and the ordered cosmos itself - legal transgression violates cosmic order
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-025"></span>**Sacred stones and centers**: Traditional states organized around ritual centers (omphalos, sacred stones) that connected earthly order to cosmic principles  
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-026"></span>**State as spiritual organism**: The state should transfigure rather than merely organize human collectivity, elevating it toward transcendent purposes
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-027"></span>**Universal vs. particular**: True empire transcends nationalism by embodying universal principles that can unite diverse peoples through spiritual rather than material bonds
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-028"></span>**Divine vs. demonic elements**: Traditional hierarchy distinguished between divine castes (dvīja) oriented toward transcendence and natural/demonic elements needing formation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-029"></span>**Empire as cosmic order**: Persian tradition saw empire as territory snatched from darkness and organized by the principle of light
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-030"></span>**Roman aeternitas**: The eternal quality of empire served as a bulwark against chaotic forces, maintaining cosmic order until the end of the cycle
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-031"></span>**Medieval ideal**: Dante's vision of empire as supernatural institution providing remedy for fallen human nature through divine rather than merely political authority

## The Mystery of the Rite

Ritual action serves as the fundamental technology for establishing contact with transcendent forces and maintaining cosmic order.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-032"></span>**Primordial sacrifice and divine creation**: Creation myths describe primordial powers transcending themselves through sacrificial action, generating divine principles of order
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-033"></span>**Osiris as archetypal sacrifice**: The Egyptian god who first experienced death became the model for all transformative ritual action leading to divine realization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-034"></span>**Three phases of ritual**: Purification of the officiant, evocation of spiritual forces, and crisis/actualization that manifests divine presence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-035"></span>**Dangerous sacred technology**: Improperly performed rites unleash chaotic forces; correctly performed rites maintain cosmic stability and human connection to transcendence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-036"></span>**Hereditary transmission**: Ritual knowledge passes through bloodlines as both spiritual legacy and biological predisposition, creating sacred races
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-037"></span>**Chinese ritual as cosmic law**: Rites serve as "channels by which we can apprehend the ways of Heaven" - fundamental to imperial governance
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-038"></span>**Brahmanical supremacy**: In Aryan India, the brahmaṇa caste's monopoly on ritual granted authority over even kings and gods
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-039"></span>**Foundation of hierarchy**: Access to effective ritual performance determines social rank more than wealth, birth, or political power
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-040"></span>**Connection to invisible world**: Rites establish causal relationships in the metaphysical realm that then manifest in physical events and human destiny

## On the Primordial Nature of the Patriciate

Traditional aristocracy derives its legitimacy from spiritual rather than material factors, creating a fundamental distinction between patricians and plebeians.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-041"></span>**Ritual monopoly defines nobility**: Patricians possessed rites connecting them to divine ancestors; plebeians lacked such supernatural connections ("gentem non habent")
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-042"></span>**Second birth through initiation**: Even Aryan birth required ritual activation through initiation to actualize aristocratic potential - blood alone insufficient
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-043"></span>**Divine vs. earthly ancestors**: Patrician families traced descent to gods (divi parentes) while plebeian ancestors remained merely human
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-044"></span>**Sacred fire as family essence**: Each patrician family maintained perpetual fire representing their divine legacy and connection to transcendent forces
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-045"></span>**Pater as priest-king**: Family heads served as priests of their household cult, wielding authority through spiritual rather than merely paternal power
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-046"></span>**Plebeian religion as collective/chthonic**: Lower castes worshipped earth deities and collective forces, lacking individual connection to transcendent principles  
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-047"></span>**Adoption as spiritual rebirth**: Roman adoption created supernatural filiation more significant than biological kinship
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-048"></span>**Germanic sacred kingship**: Northern European kings derived authority directly from divine ancestry rather than priestly mediation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-049"></span>**Unity of blood and spirit**: Highest aristocracy combined racial heredity with spiritual transmission, creating both biological and metaphysical continuity

## Spiritual Virility

Traditional spirituality emphasized direct action and mastery rather than devotional submission, representing a fundamentally masculine approach to transcendence.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-050"></span>**Numen vs. personal deity**: Original religious experience focused on impersonal spiritual forces (numina) rather than anthropomorphic gods requiring worship
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-051"></span>**Divine technique not devotion**: Ritual functioned as precise spiritual technology producing necessary results, not emotional communion with personal beings
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-052"></span>**Brāhmaṇa superiority over gods**: Vedic priests commanded spiritual forces that even deities must obey - representing triumph of knowledge over belief
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-053"></span>**Egyptian royal formulas**: Pharaohs declared their safety essential to divine welfare - "O gods, you are safe if I am safe"
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-054"></span>**Orthodoxy through practice**: Traditional spirituality required correct ritual performance rather than doctrinal belief - heresy meant ritual neglect
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-055"></span>**Do ut des relationship**: Classical religion operated on reciprocal exchange with divine forces rather than submissive worship
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-056"></span>**Autonomous sacred action**: Highest spirituality involves beings who "act without being acted upon" - the "holy race of people without kings"
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-057"></span>**Magic as royal science**: True magic represents spiritual virility and centrality, not primitive superstition - the path of those who command rather than supplicate
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-058"></span>**Transcendence of moral limitations**: Spiritual realization operates beyond conventional ethics, as power transcends human virtues and sins

## The Two Paths in the Afterlife

Traditional teaching distinguishes between mere survival and true immortality, with most humans dissolving after death while heroes achieve transcendent continuity.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-059"></span>**Three components of man**: Beyond body lies ordinary personality (destined for dissolution), the daemon/double (connection to ancestral forces), and potential for immortal realization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-060"></span>**The daemon as life-force**: Deep spiritual power that connects individual to family/tribal essence but transcends personal identity
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-061"></span>**Two destinies after death**: Path of the gods (deva-yāna) leading to immortal states vs. path of ancestors (pitṛ-yāna) resulting in reincarnation within natural cycles
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-062"></span>**Heroic immortality**: Warriors and nobles who die gloriously achieve true survival through spiritual transformation rather than mere ghostly persistence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-063"></span>**Egyptian sahu body**: Ritual creation of incorruptible spiritual body replacing physical form - "resurrection body" of solar immortality
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-064"></span>**Aristocratic afterlife**: Valhalla, House of the Sun, and similar heavenly realms reserved for heroes, nobles, and initiated ones
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-065"></span>**The totemic fate**: Ordinary people dissolve back into collective ancestral forces, losing individual consciousness in endless cycles of rebirth
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-066"></span>**Solar vs. lunar paths**: Solar path leads beyond cosmic cycles to unconditional states; lunar path remains trapped within natural becoming
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-067"></span>**Victory over the second death**: True spiritual realization prevents final dissolution, achieving stable participation in transcendent being

## Life and Death of Civilizations

Civilizations decline when their spiritual center weakens, not due to external material factors, but through loss of connection to transcendent principles.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-068"></span>**Insufficiency of material explanations**: Political collapse, moral corruption, racial mixing, and external invasion cannot fully explain civilizational death
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-069"></span>**Spiritual race vs. biological race**: The "inner race" and spiritual essence matter more than mere biological heredity for civilizational vitality
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-070"></span>**Central principle requirement**: Living civilizations need individuals who actually embody rather than merely represent transcendent principles
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-071"></span>**Gog and Magog legend**: Chaotic forces attack when no one maintains the spiritual defenses - when trumpet calls become mere wind sounds
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-072"></span>**Ritual forms without essence**: When spiritual realization dies, traditional institutions become empty shells that eventually crumble
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-073"></span>**Blood as spiritual register**: Racial heredity preserves effects of spiritual action across generations but cannot substitute for living realization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-074"></span>**Necessity of inner awakening**: Material measures alone cannot prevent civilizational decline without spiritual regeneration from above
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-075"></span>**Hierarchical emanation**: Traditional authority flows from authentic spiritual realization at the apex down through various degrees of participation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-076"></span>**The walking corpse**: De Gobineau's image of civilizations that continue functioning after their spiritual essence has departed

## Initiation and Consecration

When natural divinity no longer incarnates directly, special ritual actions become necessary to establish connection between human individuals and transcendent functions.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-077"></span>**Deus vs. divus distinction**: Original gods were naturally divine beings; later heroes achieved divinity through initiation or transformative action
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-078"></span>**Egyptian royal initiation**: Pharaohs reenacted Osiris's sacrificial death and resurrection to renew divine nature through ritual rather than birth
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-079"></span>**Eleusinian rite sequence**: Crossing death-waters, ascending through planetary spheres, receiving divine embrace - systematic transformation of consciousness
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-080"></span>**Rex as initiatory title**: At Eleusis, "king" designated achieved supernatural dimension qualifying for leadership function
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-081"></span>**Priestly mediation vs. direct realization**: Later civilizations required clerical intermediaries to transmit spiritual influences to temporal rulers
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-082"></span>**Anointing and consecration**: Sacred oils and ritual investiture created "deus-homo" status, making kings as holy as priests before God
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-083"></span>**Independence of spiritual dignity**: Initiatory attainment transcends human virtue or sin, creating "indelible mark" unaffected by moral behavior
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-084"></span>**Chinese distinction**: Natural sages vs. those who achieve transcendence through discipline and return to ritual order
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-085"></span>**Maintenance of synthesis**: Even mediated consecration aimed to reconstitute unity of spiritual and temporal authority in single person

## On the Hierarchical Relationship Between Royalty and Priesthood

When spiritual and temporal powers separate, kingship maintains primacy over priesthood as the active principle over the passive matrix.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-086"></span>**Melchizedek precedent**: The mysterious priest-king's superiority over Abraham establishes royal priesthood as higher than ordinary sacerdotal function
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-087"></span>**Brāhmaṇa as maternal matrix**: Vedic texts describe priestly caste as "mother" providing spiritual substance that warrior principle shapes and transcends
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-088"></span>**Warrior gods from Brahman**: Higher divine forms arise from primordial spiritual principle, suggesting temporal authority represents evolved spiritual power
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-089"></span>**Consecration not subordination**: King assumes rather than receives power through priestly mediation - priest must worship the consecrated ruler
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-090"></span>**Fire and water symbolism**: Kṣatriya compared to fire and iron, brāhmaṇa to water and stone - active vs. passive spiritual principles
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-091"></span>**Emperor as caput ecclesiae**: In traditional view, imperial function represents effective dominion of spiritual force rather than clerical organization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-092"></span>**Solar vs. lunar symbolism**: Empire compared to sun, church to moon - primary light source vs. reflected illumination
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-093"></span>**Papal usurpations**: Pope's adoption of pontifex maximus title and imperial symbols represents illegitimate appropriation of originally royal functions
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-094"></span>**Gregorian heresy**: Church's claim to supremacy over consecrated emperor subverts traditional hierarchy and spiritual truth

## Universality and Centralism

True imperial unity operates through spiritual attraction and inner adhesion rather than material force and administrative centralization.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-095"></span>**Fides as sacred bond**: Medieval feudal faithfulness represented sacramental relationship elevating political loyalty to spiritual dimension
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-096"></span>**Ideal vs. material unity**: Authentic empire unifies through transcendent principle rather than bureaucratic control or military domination
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-097"></span>**Frankish model**: Early medieval organization based on scattered noble cells maintaining spiritual connection to center while enjoying local autonomy
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-098"></span>**Wei wu wei imperial action**: Far Eastern teaching that true authority acts by not-acting, allowing natural order to emerge through spiritual influence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-099"></span>**Organic vs. mechanical unity**: Traditional empire resembles living organism with autonomous parts; modern state operates as uniform mechanism
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-100"></span>**Absolutism as decay**: When monarchs resort to material centralization and political control, they undermine authentic imperial authority
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-101"></span>**Totalitarian terminus**: Enforced uniformity leads inevitably to democratic leveling and eventual collectivist tyranny
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-102"></span>**Sacred vs. profane imperialism**: True empire requires spiritual transcendence of nationalism; mere military conquest produces temporary violence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-103"></span>**Church contribution to secularization**: Gregorian opposition to imperial authority inadvertently promoted secular, materialistic concept of state
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-104"></span>**Die and become principle**: Authentic imperial mission requires peoples to transcend particularistic identity through spiritual transformation

## The Soul of Chivalry

Medieval knighthood represented a synthesis of warrior action and spiritual realization, creating a supernational aristocracy dedicated to transcendent principles.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-105"></span>**Feudal vs. knightly distinction**: Feudal nobility tied to land and local lord; knights formed universal community bound by honor and spiritual loyalty
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-106"></span>**Military priesthood**: Knights combined warrior function with sacred dedication, serving spiritual authority through heroic action
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-107"></span>**Esoteric feminine symbolism**: Knight's devotion to "woman" represented relationship to Holy Wisdom and transcendent spiritual power, not ordinary romance
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-108"></span>**Initiation through ordeal**: Knightly consecration involved fasting, vigil, symbolic death/rebirth, and reception of spiritually charged weapons
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-109"></span>**Templar secret doctrine**: Knights Templar possessed inner teaching that transcended exoteric Christianity, recognizing limitations of devotional religion
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-110"></span>**Grail tradition**: Sacred vessel legends embodied Ghibelline ideal of regal spirituality independent from ecclesiastical authority
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-111"></span>**Weapon symbolism**: Sword, lance, and other knightly arms served as visible reminders of spiritual virtues and inner transformation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-112"></span>**Courts of Love**: Chivalrous literature concealed initiatory teachings about relationship between warrior consciousness and divine feminine principle
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-113"></span>**Heretical connections**: Strongest chivalrous movements (Albigensians, Templars) maintained suspect relationships with Church orthodoxy
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-114"></span>**Imperial vs. ecclesiastical**: Chivalry served Empire rather than Church, representing active spiritual path vs. passive religious devotion

## The Doctrine of the Castes

The traditional caste system reflects natural spiritual hierarchies and provides organic framework for human development according to innate capacities.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-115"></span>**Organic correspondence**: Four castes mirror hierarchy of organism - spiritual authority (head), warrior nobility (arms), producers (belly), workers (feet)
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-116"></span>**Functional not social**: Castes define spiritual types and ways of being rather than mere economic or political categories
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-117"></span>**Pre-existence determines birth**: Traditional teaching holds that spiritual nature determines caste birth, not vice versa - birth reflects transcendent will
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-118"></span>**Dharma as natural law**: Each caste has distinctive duty (svadharma) corresponding to inherent spiritual nature and cosmic function
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-119"></span>**Hereditary transmission**: Closed caste system preserves favorable organic and psychic conditions for developing prenatal spiritual dispositions
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-120"></span>**Bhakti as universal path**: Pure action without personal attachment allows any caste member to achieve spiritual realization within their station
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-121"></span>**Mixture as contamination**: Crossing caste boundaries violates cosmic order and unleashes infernal forces - the only true "untouchables"
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-122"></span>**Vertical not horizontal advancement**: Spiritual progress occurs through perfecting one's nature rather than changing social position
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-123"></span>**Traditional vs. modern morality**: Caste ethics based on nature and function, not abstract universal principles applying equally to all
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-124"></span>**Pariah as the casteless**: Only those who abandon their proper dharma become truly impure and socially ostracized

## Professional Associations and the Arts; Slavery

Traditional arts and crafts possessed sacred dimension and initiatory potential, while slavery institutionalized the spiritual reality of those bound to material existence.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-125"></span>**Sacred sciences**: Traditional knowledge systems (alchemy, astrology, medicine) considered spiritual realities symbolically expressed through material phenomena
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-126"></span>**Anagogic element**: Traditional sciences valued capacity to "lead upward" to transcendent understanding over mere practical effectiveness
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-127"></span>**Divine origin of arts**: Crafts traced to divine or heroic founders (Vulcan, Hermes, Prometheus), indicating non-human source of technical knowledge
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-128"></span>**Arcanum magisterium**: Each trade possessed secret inner tradition transmitted alongside practical techniques through guild initiation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-129"></span>**Corporate religious structure**: Professional collegia had own patron deities, sacred fires, hierarchical organization modeled on military and civic order
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-130"></span>**Work as spiritual discipline**: Guild system transformed material labor into ritual action oriented toward transcendent goals
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-131"></span>**Slavery as dharma**: Traditional world assigned "work" (ponos) to slaves because pure material labor represents spiritual condition of bondage
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-132"></span>**Contempt for labor**: Free men avoided work not from arrogance but because mechanical activity contradicts spiritual freedom and self-sufficiency
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-133"></span>**Victory and defeat as spiritual signs**: Military conquest revealed metaphysical superiority - vanquished assigned servile status reflecting spiritual defeat
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-134"></span>**Modern industrial slavery**: Contemporary mechanized labor creates more dehumanizing conditions than ancient slavery while lacking traditional spiritual framework

## Bipartition of the Traditional Spirit; Asceticism

Traditional spirituality manifests through two complementary paths - contemplative detachment and heroic action - both leading beyond human limitations.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-135"></span>**Contemplative asceticism**: Integration of knowing faculty through detachment from sensible reality and progressive stripping of conditioned consciousness  
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-136"></span>**Buddhist example**: Early Buddhism exemplified pure contemplative path - systematic destruction of identification with conditioned existence through knowledge
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-137"></span>**Aristocratic renunciation**: True ascetic renunciation proceeds from spiritual nobility and direct desire for transcendence, not moral mortification
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-138"></span>**Neoplatonic method**: Plotinus's "simplification" (aplōsis) removes all conditioning to achieve metaphysical participation in intelligible reality
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-139"></span>**Eckhart's detachment**: Rhineland mysticism transcended Christian theism through radical detachment (Abgescheidenheit) superior to conventional virtues  
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-140"></span>**Action path**: Heroic asceticism awakens deepest human forces through absolute intensity, causing superlife to spring from life itself
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-141"></span>**Unified vs. separated poles**: Complete traditional forms integrate both contemplative and active dimensions rather than specializing in one approach
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-142"></span>**Feminine spirituality warning**: When traditional synthesis breaks down, separated spiritual authority tends toward lunar/feminine character incompatible with solar regality
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-143"></span>**Transcendent vs. moral**: Both paths operate beyond conventional ethics, seeking ontological transformation rather than behavioral improvement

## The Greater and the Lesser Holy War

Warfare can serve as means for spiritual realization when external combat becomes the method for conquering inner enemies and achieving transcendence.

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-144"></span>**Islamic distinction**: "Greater holy war" against inner enemies vs. "lesser holy war" against external foes - inner battle is primary and essential
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-145"></span>**Inner enemies**: Animalistic instincts, fear, attachment, false identification with temporary personality - obstacles to spiritual realization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-146"></span>**Right intention (niya)**: Transcendent orientation transforms combat into ritual sacrifice capable of producing liberation rather than mere violence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-147"></span>**Koranic teachings**: "Life of this world is but sport and pastime" - temporal existence has no ultimate significance compared to eternal realities
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-148"></span>**Bhagavad Gītā doctrine**: Krishna teaches Arjuna that true reality cannot be killed or destroyed - physical combat reveals metaphysical principles
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-149"></span>**Divine theophany**: Transcendent power manifests through destructive action that negates finite limitations and reveals immortal essence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-150"></span>**Mors triumphalis**: Heroic death in battle represents victory over death itself - passage to states transcending mortal existence
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-151"></span>**Crusade spirituality**: Medieval holy war combined military action with monastic discipline - knights as "armed priests" seeking absolute glory

* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-315"></span>**Renovatio Romani Imperii**: Formula spoken at Frankish king coronation; Germanic princes identified Rome as symbolic source of imperium and right

## Decline of the Medieval World and the Birth of Nations

The Holy Roman Empire's decadence was determined by causes from above and below, with gradual secularization and materialization of the political idea.

**Imperial Decadence:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-332"></span>**Frederick II's Upheaval**: While defending supernatural character against Church, associated with incipient humanism, rationalism, and antifeudal centralization at Sicilian court
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-333"></span>**Consecration Loss**: By 1338, Ludwig IV declared imperial consecration no longer necessary; Charles IV's "Golden Bull" completed emancipation; emperors compromised transcendent dignitas
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-334"></span>**Sacred vs Secular**: When empire ceases to be sacred, it ceases to be empire; inner vision and authority decline; mere "politics" excludes universalism and higher unity
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-335"></span>**Last Crowned Emperor**: Frederick III of Austria (1452) as last emperor crowned in Rome after rite reduced to empty ceremony

**Feudal System Breakdown:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-336"></span>**Normal Traditional Structure**: Feudal system characterizing great traditional eras; principle of plurality and political autonomy with universal element presiding over parts
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-337"></span>**Centralization vs Particularism**: When dignitas falls and spiritual acknowledgment fails, either centralizing absolutism or dissociative processes destroy medieval civilization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-338"></span>**National State Emergence**: Kings claiming absolute authority for own fiefs; spreading subversive idea of national state; European ecumene beginning to fall apart
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-339"></span>**Chivalry Decline**: Knights defending temporal ambitions of lords and national states rather than superpolitical ideals; great forces replaced by diplomatic combinations

**Church's Failure:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-340"></span>**Inability to Lead**: After Empire declined, Church demonstrated inability to organize Western world according to Guelph ideal; replaced by multiplicity of national states
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-341"></span>**Hierarchical Law**: When caste rebels against higher caste claiming independence, unavoidably loses character and reflects lower caste characteristics
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-342"></span>**French Example**: Philip the Fair destroyed Templar order, started lay emancipation, favored bourgeoisie against nobility; prepared body for incarnation of new principle

**Cultural Consequences:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-343"></span>**Neutralization Phenomenon**: Civilization ceased having unitary axis; no common organizing force animating culture; art, philosophy, science developing in isolation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-344"></span>**Renaissance Misunderstanding**: Not revival of classical civilization but borrowing decadent forms; employing ancient legacy in radically new fashion emphasizing human affirmation
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-345"></span>**Horizontal vs Vertical**: Medieval tension with metaphysical orientation changed polarity; potential flowed outward into horizontal dimension producing external creativity without traditional elements

## The Regression of the Castes

Progressive shift of power and civilization type has occurred from one caste to next since prehistoric times, representing objective law of decadence process.

**Four Stages of Decline:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-364"></span>**First Caste (Divine Royalty)**: Representatives of spiritual virility and Olympian sovereignty belong to distant mythical past; Ghibelline Holy Roman Empire as last echo of highest tradition
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-365"></span>**Second Caste (Warriors)**: Authority descended to warrior caste; monarchs as military leaders and temporal justice rulers; regality of blood replacing regality of spirit; age of great European monarchies
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-366"></span>**Third Caste (Merchants)**: Aristocracies decaying and monarchies shaking; capitalist oligarchies revealing power shift from warriors to mercantile class; "kings of coal, oil, iron" replacing kings of blood and spirit
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-367"></span>**Fourth Caste (Serfs)**: Crisis of bourgeois society and proletarian revolt; power passing to lowest traditional caste; reduction to plane of matter, machine, and quantity; Russian Revolution as prelude

**Economic Dominance:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-368"></span>**Gold as Tool**: Those acquiring and multiplying gold controlling political power behind democratic appearances; trafficking with money invading new civilization
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-369"></span>**Jewish Emancipation**: Secularized Judaism seeing ways to world domination; Marx noting money becoming world power and practical Jewish spirit becoming Christian spirit
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-370"></span>**Social Contract Theory**: Social bond no longer warrior fides based on faithfulness/honor but utilitarian economic character; agreement based on convenience and material interest
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-371"></span>**Capitalist Foundation**: Codification of traffic with gold as loan charged with interest; aberrant development of banking, finance, pure economy spreading like cancer

**Cultural Regression:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-372"></span>**Architecture**: From temple (first caste) to fortress/castle (warriors) to city-state with walls (merchants) to factory and rational mass-housing (serfs)
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-373"></span>**Family Evolution**: From sacred foundation to authoritarian model to bourgeois convention to dissolution when party/society supersede in importance
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-374"></span>**War Concepts**: From "sacred war" and mors triumphalis to wars of honor to national/economic conflicts to world revolution of proletarian class against capitalism
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-375"></span>**Art Development**: From symbolic sacred art to epic/heroic to romantic/psychological bourgeois consumption to "social" art for masses

**Ethical Degradation:**
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-376"></span>**Action to Work**: Ancient world despised work because it knew action; opposition between spiritual pure free pole and material impure pole; loss of this opposition characterizes last ages
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-377"></span>**Work Religion**: Slave's principle elevated to religion; "Work ennobles man" and "religion of work"; ancient ascetic regarded as time-waster, hero as fanatic
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-378"></span>**Sports Phenomenon**: Modern sublimated work version becoming disinterested activity; "blue collar religion" as typical counterfeit of traditional action
* <span id="revolt-against-the-modern-world-379"></span>**Evolutionary Myth**: Profession of faith of upstart; civilization from barbarism, religion from superstition, man from animal; alibi for civilization created by revolution of serfs against aristocracy
