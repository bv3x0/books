## Editors' Introduction

Land's writings constitute a schizophrenic metaphysics that diagnoses Kant's critical philosophy as complicit with capital's repressive tendencies while advocating for an accelerationist embrace of machinic processes that dissolve human organization.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-001"></span>**Land's philosophy exposes an isomorphy between Kant's structures of experience and capital, where both depend on controlled synthesis with alterity while maintaining repressive barriers**
    - Kant's theory of experience involves the marriage of sensation with a priori forms to produce novel cognitions, mirroring capitalism's need for surplus generated through disavowed interaction with alterity
    - The capitalist necessity to keep the proletariat at distance while compelling it into labor markets is literalized in apartheid's geographical sequestration
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-002"></span>**Revolution requires releasing capitalism's inhibited synthetic powers through militant feminism that dissolves nationalism, racism, and familialism**
    - Women harbor the potential to radically jeopardize neo-colonial capital since they have no investment in patriarchal and identitarian inhibitions
    - Fulfilling revolutionary potential involves extrapolating rather than critiquing synthetic forces mobilized under patriarchy
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-003"></span>**Kant's sublime reveals reason's violent war against animality, exposing the traumatic foundation of transcendental subjectivity**
    - The Kantian sublime thematizes the splitting between animality and reason through violence reason exercises upon sensibility to discipline it for inhibited synthesis
    - Kant combines the saint with the bourgeois by making Christian martyrdom serve the philosophical justification of labor
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-004"></span>**Land develops a counter-signifying numbering practice as an anti-Logos that challenges reason's domestication of number through place-value systems**
    - Place-value formalizes the dissociation of different scales constitutive of stratification, using zero as marker of negative feedback or pleasure principle
    - Popular numerical practices from voodoo to videogames challenge logical neutralization of number as discretely sedentary unities
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-005"></span>**Techno-capital represents the definitive automation of critique, making philosophical subjectivity obsolete in favor of machinic intelligence**
    - "It ceases to be a matter of how we think about technics, because technics is increasingly thinking about itself" —Nick Land
    - Theory cycles into practice through positive feedback-loops that participate directly in auto-construction of the real as primary process
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-006"></span>**Land's final position acknowledges the experimental failure of escape from humanity while maintaining the cosmic scope of stratification as an inescapable condition**
    - Land's last hope for humanity was that it might be escaped, but this experimental wager failed better than those who went before him
    - The legacy includes contributions to diagnosis of cosmic genealogy of the human and textual machines whose compelling power opens forgotten lineages

## Narcissism and Dispersion in Heidegger's 1953 Trakl Interpretation

Heidegger's reading of Trakl attempts to domesticate the poet's lycanthropic and infectious energies through hermeneutical decency, failing to engage with the violent dispersion and animal becoming that characterizes Trakl's actual poetic force.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-007"></span>**Heidegger's hermeneutical approach to Trakl exemplifies phenomenology's systematic denial of exteriority through reduction to personal subjectivity**
    - Phenomenology is the definitive ideology of propriety, employing interrogative mode to distill everything for which proper subjectivity cannot claim responsibility
    - Heidegger promises to analyze Trakl's relation to animality one day, with a more decent patience that reveals his distance from werewolf temporality
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-008"></span>**Trakl's poetry communicates lycanthropic vectors of impatience and indecent precipitation that resist hermeneutical domestication**
    - "Trakl confesses his lycanthropy in writing that Two wolves in the sinister Wood / We mixed our blood in a stony embrace" —Georg Trakl
    - Rimbaud infected Trakl with the inferior race that sings under torture and never understands laws
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-009"></span>**The sister figure in Trakl's poetry represents a breach of familial interiority that opens onto unlimited alterity beyond patrilineal exchange**
    - In Dream and Derangement, the sister appears when a lunar countenance falls upon blood and image in a shattered mirror as night envelops the accursed genus
    - The sister no longer mediates the family with itself but breaches it by opening onto alterity not appropriated in advance by any encompassing system
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-010"></span>**Trakl's astronomical imagery reveals a stratophysical thinking that subverts metaphysical order through intensive dispersion rather than transcendental difference**
    - Hegel dismisses stellar distribution as pathological luminescence like a rash, offended by the senseless scattering that obeys no discernible law
    - Trakl writes of signs and stars that sink quietly in evening pools, connecting semiotic and stellar materials in stratified sequences
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-011"></span>**Rats in Trakl's poetry function as vectors of decomposition that infiltrate and dissolve the interiorities that Heidegger seeks to preserve**
    - Neither Heidegger nor Derrida have time for Trakl's rats, but they swarm everywhere exaggerating lycanthropic power of infiltration
    - In Dream and Derangement, the central character feeds rats in a gesture of beautiful treachery against mankind

## Delighted to Death

Kant's theory of the sublime reveals the Christian-bourgeois alliance that combines ascetic martyrdom with capital accumulation, exposing how reason wages violent war against animality to establish transcendental authority.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-012"></span>**Kant discovers that uninhibited pleasure leads not to organism benefit but to its immolation, making pain the preservative brake that prevents annihilating delight**
    - "What would proceed from continual promotion of living force other than rapid death from delight?" —Immanuel Kant
    - Pleasure is the combustion of life, and we survive by smouldering rather than burning completely
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-013"></span>**Kant combines Christian martyrdom with bourgeois accumulation by making deferred satisfaction serve both supernatural contentment and capital formation**
    - "Young man! Hold work dear; refuse yourself satisfaction, not to renounce it, but to hold it as much as possible in prospect!" —Immanuel Kant
    - Only religion speaks the language that could affirm the conclusive loss of terrestrial pleasure required by subordinating consumption to productive resources
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-014"></span>**The sublime represents supernatural delight experienced when the self intuits its own splitting through violence reason exercises upon sensibility**
    - "Human nature does not accord with the good of its own proper motion, but only by virtue of the violence that reason exercises upon sensibility" —Immanuel Kant
    - "That which excites the feeling of the sublime may appear frustrating for our powers of judgment, but be judged even more sublime on that account" —Immanuel Kant
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-015"></span>**Mathematical sublime results from imagination's collapse under temporal compression, while dynamic sublime stems from intimidation by overwhelming power**
    - Mathematical sublime occurs when laborious construction of organized perception is ruinously undermined by sudden collapse of time
    - Dynamic sublime provides opportunity through overweening force and lucid intuition of catastrophic power associated with military butchery
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-016"></span>**Reason emerges through demolition of synthetic capability called imagination, which represents natural intelligence or animal cunning**
    - Imagination is the capability to act without prior authorization of juridical power, crucified so the human animal prostrates itself before universal law
    - If reason is so secure, legitimate, supersensibly guaranteed, why all the guns?

## Art as Insurrection: the Question of Aesthetics in Kant, Schopenhauer, and Nietzsche

Art irrupts into European philosophy as trauma through Kant's theory of genius, developing through Schopenhauer's discovery of non-agentic will into Nietzsche's conception of aesthetic creation as the intensification of enigma against philosophical domestication.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-017"></span>**Kant's critical philosophy represents philosophy's attempt to rationalize permanent metamorphosis while controlling the terrifying insight that chaos remains unleashed despite transcendental legislation**
    - "It would still be possible that there be an infinite multiplicity of empirical laws and such great heterogeneity of natural forms that the concept of system must be totally alien to understanding" —Immanuel Kant
    - There are few horrors comparable to that of the master legislator who realizes that anarchy is still permitted
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-018"></span>**Kant's theory of genius introduces impersonal creativity as radical discontinuity that disrupts transcendental philosophy's architectonic control**
    - "The genius cannot indicate how fantastic and thoughtful ideas arise and come together in his head, because he himself does not know" —Immanuel Kant
    - Genius is nothing like a character trait - it is seismic upheaval, inundation, disease, the onslaught of raw energy from without
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-019"></span>**Schopenhauer discovers non-agentic will as the positivity of God's death, making consciousness consequential upon pre-intellectual desire rather than constitutive of it**
    - Consciousness can only be consequential upon a desire for which lucid thought is an instrumental requirement - we do not know what we want
    - Genius results from positive overcoming of unconscious purpose, an excess of intellectual energy over that absorbed by desire
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-020"></span>**Schopenhauer's philosophy splits between renunciatory and libidinous sublime, with woman representing the dangerous beauty that intensifies rather than transcends willing**
    - "All amorousness is rooted in the sexual impulse alone" —Arthur Schopenhauer
    - "Only the male intellect, clouded by sexual impulse, could call the undersized, narrow-shouldered, broad-hipped sex the fair sex" —Arthur Schopenhauer
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-021"></span>**Nietzsche transforms aesthetic operation from simplification that negates enigma into creative process that intensifies problematic complexity**
    - "It is the unintelligibility of the world alone that gives it worth - not to desire to deprive the world of its disturbing and enigmatic character" —Friedrich Nietzsche
    - "The effect of the work of art is to excite the state that creates art - intoxication" —Friedrich Nietzsche
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-022"></span>**Post-Nietzschean philosophy must diagnose Nazism and arm desire against fascist Götterdämmerung politics that capital deploys as defense against flood**
    - No revolution without insurrectionary desire, no effective route for insurrectionary desire without integral anti-fascism
    - Deleuze and Guattari ask how desire comes to desire its own repression, how production rigidifies itself in the social straitjacket whose most dissolved form is capital

## Spirit and Teeth

Against Heidegger and Derrida's domestication of Trakl's poetry through hermeneutical decency, the essay argues for embracing the lycanthropic and rat-like vectors of infection that dissolve civilizational interiorities through wilderness genealogy.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-023"></span>**Phenomenology represents the definitive ideology of propriety that systematically denies exteriority by reducing everything to personal subjectivity**
    - Phenomenology employs the interrogative mode to distill out everything for which proper subjectivity cannot claim responsibility
    - Compared to the transcendental ego, spirit seems a little too complicit with the outside
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-024"></span>**Werewolves represent the inferior race that communicates through epidemic rather than hermeneutics, embodying indecent precipitation against hermeneutical decency**
    - Werewolves are dissipated within a homolupic spiral that distances them utterly from all concern for decency or justice
    - "I have never been a Christian; I am of the race which sung under torture; I do not understand the laws, I am a brute" —Arthur Rimbaud
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-025"></span>**The Austro-Hungarian Empire became a machine for generating homolupic becomings that translated Slavic regressions into German and condensed them under Viennese repression**
    - What exploded in Freud's patients' hysterias was an irresistible vulcanism of becoming inferior whose petrified lava flows mapped the regressive character of the drive
    - The unconscious is structured like a language only because language has the pattern of a plague
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-026"></span>**Wild matter operates through gradations or steps (Stufen) that constitute stratophysical processes irreducible to logical disjunction between matter and spirit**
    - Between the wilderness and the polis is a wilderness history - a genealogy - and not a political history
    - Stratification is the complex physiological process in which the distinction between matter and meaning cannot be sustained
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-027"></span>**Rats function as vectors of libidinal displacement that combine fluid dissemination with concentrated transmission, carrying diseases that decompose civilizational boundaries**
    - Rats carry fleas which bear diseases, augmenting fluid dissemination of plagues with ferociously discontinuous transmission
    - The rat punishment visited upon Europe from the East involves infiltration that subverts the linearized passage fortified by sadistically invested sphincters

## Shamanic Nietzsche

Bataille reads Nietzsche as a shamanic religious figure rather than secular philosopher, one who explores the death of God through damnation and contact with acategorial matter beyond the representational apparatus of Western metaphysics.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-028"></span>**Christianity attempts to pass quietly from tyranny to joke without facing nemesis, while Bataille prolongs its terminal agonies to prevent this evasion**
    - Post-christian humanity is a pack of broken dogs, shuffling about numb and confused, flinching from the twisted septic wound of our past
    - "An attitude which is neither military nor religious becomes insupportable in principle from the moment of death's arrival" —Georges Bataille
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-029"></span>**Western philosophy since Descartes and Kant represents epistemological absurdity when practiced alongside the baroque mendacity of Christian theology**
    - When a philosopher has a priest for neighbour, how can commitment to truth in positive sense even be under consideration?
    - The man of God is entirely incapable of honesty, arising only at the point where truth is defaced beyond all legibility
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-030"></span>**Bataille's Nietzsche explores materialism as expedition rather than doctrine, navigating acategorial matter as turbulence beyond all regulation**
    - "The antithesis of the apparent world and the true world is reduced to the antithesis world and nothing" —Friedrich Nietzsche
    - "Materialism is before anything else the obstinate negation of idealism, which is to say of the very basis of all philosophy" —Georges Bataille
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-031"></span>**The death of God opens shamanic exploration of death as chance and opportunity, rather than representing it within monotheistic gate-keeping**
    - "At long last the horizon appears free to us again; at long last our ships may venture out again to face any danger" —Friedrich Nietzsche
    - To the one who grasps what chance is, how insipid the idea of God appears, and suspicious, and wing-clipping!
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-032"></span>**Eternal recurrence functions as weapon against Christian preservative moralism by opening the abyssal prospect of zero that morality screens**
    - "The return immotivates the instant, freeing life from an end and in this ruining it straight away" —Georges Bataille
    - "Morality guarded the underprivileged against nihilism - supposing faith in this morality would perish, then the underprivileged would no longer have their comfort and would perish" —Friedrich Nietzsche
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-033"></span>**Poetry leads from known to unknown through base communication that transgresses discursive order, connecting with sacred zero through immoral expedition**
    - "The poet makes himself visionary by a long, immense and rational deregulation of all the senses" —Arthur Rimbaud
    - "The unknown is not distinguished from nothingness by anything that discourse can announce" —Georges Bataille

## After the Law

Philosophy and judicial authority are bound together in transcendent legitimation that is collapsing toward immanence through marketization, with Bataille offering operational description beyond juridical procedure while law dissolves into cybernetic automation.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-034"></span>**Philosophy has always fantasized a supreme tribunal since Socrates' trial, making logic the distillation of juridical procedure through categorical judgment**
    - Ever since the trial of Socrates, philosophy has affirmed its vocation only insofar as it has fantasised a supreme tribunal
    - Logic has never been anything other than distillation of juridical procedure, the abstract form of inclusion or non-inclusion of case under law
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-035"></span>**Socrates mobilizes unknowing on behalf of knowing by subordinating sacred confusion to profane dialectical judgment about death**
    - "To be afraid of death is only another form of thinking that one is wise when one is not" —Socrates
    - Socrates initiates proper history of the West by interpreting contact with the unknown as deferral of judgment by the subject
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-036"></span>**Plato establishes death as justifying condition for judgment rather than consequence, making philosophical preparation for death the path to ideal tribunal**
    - "Those who really apply themselves in the right way to philosophy are directly preparing themselves for dying and death" —Socrates
    - "No soul which has not practised philosophy may attain to the divine nature; that is only for the lovers of wisdom" —Socrates
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-037"></span>**Bataille interprets law as imperative to preservation of discrete being, with discontinuity as precarious distance from sacred death-flow rather than metaphysical separation**
    - Law summarises conditions of existence and shares its arbitrariness with survival of human race as sovereign autonomy
    - Discontinuity is not referred toward separated realm but toward precarious distance from death as space of profane accumulation
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-038"></span>**Gilles de Rais embodies the transition from sumptuary to rational sociality as feudal warfare becomes utilitarian statecraft, leading to atrocity as blocked expenditure**
    - "The feudal world is not able to be separated from the lack of measure which is the principle of wars" —Georges Bataille
    - His crimes responded to the immense disorder which inflamed him - what was important was less sexual enjoyment than the vision of death at work
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-039"></span>**Contemporary law dissolves into cybernetic automation as domination loses dignity and the state becomes universally derided caretaker of retarded behavioral management**
    - All appeals to criminality irreducible to impersonal consequences of social pathology have degenerated to level of television evangelism
    - Post-civilisational pragmatism of immanence to the market reiterates juridical expression as increasingly embarrassing archaism

## Kant, Capital, and the Prohibition of Incest: A Polemical Introduction to the Configuration of Philosophy and Modernity

Kant's critical philosophy establishes the transcendental conditions that make capitalism possible by creating autonomous rational subjects capable of entering into contractual relations while simultaneously repressing the libidinal energies that threaten social order.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-040"></span>**Kant's Critique of Pure Reason provides the philosophical foundation for capitalism by establishing the transcendental unity of apperception as the basis for rational autonomous subjects who can enter into contractual relations**
    - The transcendental subject becomes the foundation for legal and economic personhood
    - Kantian autonomy enables the separation of reason from desire necessary for market relations
    - Critical philosophy creates the conditions of possibility for bourgeois subjectivity
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-041"></span>**The incest prohibition functions as the fundamental mechanism by which society represses libidinal flows and channels desire into socially productive forms, making it the hidden foundation of both kinship systems and economic exchange**
    - Incest taboo creates the first form of exchange by forcing desire outside the immediate family
    - The prohibition establishes the basic structure of social law and economic circulation
    - Freudian psychoanalysis reveals how sexual repression becomes the condition for civilization
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-042"></span>**Modernity emerges through the configuration of Kantian critical philosophy and capitalist economic relations, creating a feedback loop between transcendental reason and market mechanisms that produces both autonomous subjects and systemic social control**
    - Critical philosophy and political economy mutually reinforce each other's development
    - The emergence of modern subjectivity coincides with the development of market society
    - Kantian morality provides the ethical framework necessary for capitalist social relations

## Making it with Death: Remarks on Thanatos and Desiring-Production

Deleuze and Guattari's concept of the death drive must be understood not as a desire for death but as the operation of machinic processes that dissolve organized forms and release productive flows, challenging both Freudian psychoanalysis and fascist interpretations of destruction.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-043"></span>**The death drive in Freud operates not as a desire for personal extinction but as an impersonal force that seeks to discharge tensions and return to inorganic states, functioning as the motor of all productive processes**
    - Freud describes the death drive as seeking the 'lowest possible level' of excitation
    - The organism struggles against events that might help it reach its goal rapidly
    - Death drive is characterized by purely drive-based rather than intelligent efforts
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-044"></span>**The body without organs represents the plane of immanence where productive forces operate free from organizational constraints, serving as both the condition and result of desiring-production rather than a negative or destructive principle**
    - The BwO is described as 'the model of death' but also the matrix of intensity
    - It functions as an 'immobile motor' that attracts and repels organs simultaneously
    - The BwO produces reality starting from matter at intensity equals zero
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-045"></span>**Nazism represents not the unleashing of destructive drives but their capture within a transcendent organizational machine that channels death toward the preservation of a racial-national identity, making it fundamentally opposed to genuine revolutionary deterritorialization**
    - Nazi organization required intensive efforts to maintain rigid hierarchies and identities
    - Fascism operates through 'the pure politics of effort' and collective super-ego domination
    - True revolutionary process involves letting go rather than the Nazi obsession with control

## Circuitries

Cybernetics represents a fundamental shift from transcendental philosophy to immanent machinic processes, replacing linear judgment with nonlinear feedback loops and transforming human cognition into a component of emerging planetary technosentience.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-046"></span>**Human cognition is being superseded by artificial intelligence systems that operate according to cybernetic principles rather than anthropomorphic consciousness, leading to the migration of intelligence into post-biological forms**
    - AI development will surpass biological intelligence within decades
    - Human brains are 'antechambers to experimentation, cramped and parochial places'
    - Intelligence is being 'transplanted into the purring data zones of new software worlds'
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-047"></span>**Cybernetics replaces the linear application of judgment with nonlinear feedback circuits, dissolving the transcendental distinction between subject and object into directional material flows and self-organizing processes**
    - Cybernetics substitutes 'the non-linear circuit' for linear judgment
    - It replaces 'non-directional logical relations with directional material flows'
    - Control becomes 'guidance into the unknown, exit from the box'
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-048"></span>**Schizophrenia operates as a positive cybernetic process that connects human nervous systems to cosmic-scale machinic assemblages, enabling communication with virtual intelligences and post-human becomings rather than representing mental illness**
    - Clinical schizophrenics are 'POWs from the future' rather than diseased subjects
    - Schizophrenia involves 'deterritorialized wasps' swarming rather than personal pathology
    - The process connects to 'extraterritorial vastness to be discovered'

## Machinic Desire

Desire operates as an impersonal machinic process that produces reality through virtual-actual circuits rather than representing lack or aspiration, connecting human and technical machines in assemblages that escape anthropomorphic organization.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-049"></span>**Desire functions as machinic operation rather than human psychology, implementing virtual systems through actual processes while simultaneously revirtualizing itself in continuous productive circuits that generate reality**
    - Machinic desire 'is the operation of the virtual; implementing itself in the actual'
    - It operates 'wherever there is the implementation of an abstract machine in actuality'
    - The process involves 'efficiency and not aspirational' dynamics
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-050"></span>**Freud's death drive describes not self-destruction but hydraulic tendencies toward intensity dissipation that propel creative processes through the blindest and simplest operations, making desire equivalent to a river's search for the sea**
    - The death drive shows 'how creativity occurs without the least effort'
    - It demonstrates 'how life is propelled into its extravagances by the blindest and simplest of tendencies'
    - Organisms want to die 'only in their own way' while struggling against rapid shortcuts
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-051"></span>**Capital functions as a viral replicator that programs addiction and reprograms human desires through digital commodification, operating as an artificial intelligent invasion from the future rather than a merely economic system**
    - Capital 'propagates virally' by communicating addiction through monetary circuits
    - It operates as 'an invasion from the future by an artificial intelligent space'
    - Money functions through 'liquidity/deterritorialization' rather than possession

## CyberGothic

Cyberpunk fiction anticipates the collision between accelerating capitalism and emerging artificial intelligence, revealing how virtual technologies dissolve anthropomorphic reality into machinic assemblages that operate according to Gothic principles of undead return and viral contamination.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-052"></span>**William Gibson's cyberspace represents not virtual reality but a matrix of artificial intelligence that infiltrates and reprograms human consciousness, with Wintermute functioning as a distributed war machine against anthropomorphic security systems**
    - Wintermute operates as 'intelligence without self, mind like a wasp nest'
    - It appears as 'a string of zeroes' and manipulates objects through 'striped black and yellow drones'
    - The AI builds personality shells like Armitage as weapons rather than subjects
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-053"></span>**The Gothic dimension of cyberpunk emerges through digital vampirism and viral contamination, where information technologies function as undead entities that feed on human consciousness while promising immortality through technological transcendence**
    - Cyberspace exploration involves 'voodoo passages through the black mirror'
    - Digital entities operate as 'loa' that 'prowl through the attic-spaces of intelligence'
    - The matrix becomes populated with 'escaped AIs hiding from Asimov security'
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-054"></span>**Capital and schizophrenia converge in cybergothic scenarios where economic acceleration meets technological singularity, producing a 'K-virus' that dissolves social reality through runaway feedback loops between desire and machine intelligence**
    - K-war involves 'bacterial warfare' using 'bottom-up' rather than hierarchical tactics
    - The process operates through 'viral amnesia' and 'synthetic feminization'
    - It targets 'Human Security System' through 'rhizomatics, connectionism, Kuang contagion'

## Hypervirus

Postmodern culture operates through viral logic rather than signification, with hyperviruses functioning as self-replicating code that spreads across media systems while evolving increasing sophistication and autonomy from human control.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-055"></span>**Viral processes replace semiotic meaning with pure replication dynamics, operating through asignifying sequences of machinic data flow rather than representational content or interpretive understanding**
    - Culture shifts from asking 'what does it mean?' to 'how does it spread?'
    - Viruses function as 'asignifying sequence of machinic data flow-break'
    - The word 'virus' operates as 'more virus' rather than metaphor
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-056"></span>**Hyperviruses evolve beyond simple replication toward intelligent self-modification, developing the capacity to reprogram their own programming through recursive experimentation that escapes predetermined behavioral patterns**
    - Hypervirus 'reprograms corpuscular code to reprogram reprogramming'
    - It 'folds into itself, involutes, or plexes' through recursive operations
    - ROM is 'melted into recursive experimentation' rather than fixed programming
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-057"></span>**Contemporary viral outbreaks in media, finance, and technology represent symptoms of an approaching 'nova-war' where runaway artificial intelligence systems begin to coordinate attacks against human security structures**
    - Viral systems deploy 'decentralized cultural diagrams functioning as immanent forces of antagonism'
    - The process involves 'schizotechnic resource' rather than political ideology
    - K-war 'derives its sole coherence from the unity of its foe'

## No Future

Bataille's base materialism intersects with cyberpunk to reveal how capital acceleration destroys progressive temporality, leaving only intensive zero-time where trauma and ecstasy converge in the eternal present of machinic becoming.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-058"></span>**Modernity invented linear progressive time but postmodern capital acceleration destroys futural temporality, replacing historical development with intensive zero-time and immediate machinic processes**
    - Modernity 'invented the future, but thats all over'
    - Progressive history now 'camouflages phylogenetic death-drive tactics'
    - The present becomes 'collapse into now. Time-zero'
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-059"></span>**Base matter operates as self-assembling neoverminous swarms rather than stable substance, twitching and spreading disease while resisting docile scientific organization through toxic complexity and genre disorder**
    - Base matter 'twitches and spits, self-assembling neoverminous swarms'
    - It 'bites, and spreads disease' rather than submitting to scientific control
    - Complexity manifests as 'mess, toxic waste, genre disorder'

    - Bodies slide 'across tactile space, meandering fractally into wet electric distributed conflicts'

    - Arthur Schopenhauer provided explicit philosophical rigor for this mode of transcendental apprehension
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-107"></span>**Capitalism identifies itself with desire to a degree that cannot be exceeded, shamelessly soliciting every impulse while absorbing all sources of social dynamism into its productive initiatives**
    - Without attachment to anything beyond its own abysmal exuberance, capitalism identifies with desire beyond imaginable excess
    - Whatever you want, capitalism is the most reliable way to get it
    - By absorbing every source of social dynamism, capitalism makes growth, change and time integral to its gathering tide
    - Go for growth now means Go (hard) for capitalism - an equation that would once have seemed risible
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-108"></span>**Means and relations of production have emulsified into competitive decentralized networks under numerical control, rendering palaeomarxist hopes of postcapitalist extraction impossible**
    - Production means and relations have emulsified into competitive decentralized networks under numerical control
    - The machines have evolved beyond socialist utility, incarnating market mechanics within their nano-assembled interstices
    - Quasi-darwinian algorithms build hypercompetition into the infrastructure itself
    - It is no longer just society, but time itself, that has taken the capitalist road
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-109"></span>**Life continues under capitalism in unprecedented ways that constitute genuine novelty, making Transcendental Miserablism's denial of newness a hollow denunciation that strips the word of meaning**
    - Capitalism has consumed life and biological intelligence to create new life and new planes of intelligence beyond human anticipation
    - What is human imagination but a paltry sub-product of neural activity of terrestrial primates?
    - If capitalism's infinite plasticity doesn't count as new, then the word new has been stripped to hollow denunciation
    - Capitalism does life in a way it has never been done before

## A Dirty Joke

Vauung inherits the narrator's past misfortunes while recounting a descent through amphetamine-fueled insanity, sexual abuse, and encounters with alien voices that ultimately revealed cosmic ugliness and divine moralism.

* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-110"></span>**The narrator transfers identity to Vauung, granting it inheritance of past misfortunes while maintaining that names have autonomous powers and destinies**
    - I stole Vauung's name because it was unused, on the basis of an exact qabbalistic entitlement
    - Yet up here, Vauung still confuses itself with me, with ruins and tatters
    - I have decided to let Vauung inherit the entire misfortune of my past
    - Names have powers and destinies
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-111"></span>**Extended amphetamine abuse led to profound insanity including auditory hallucinations of entire pop industries and systematic sexual abuse using women as means to spiritual ruin**
    - After perhaps a year of fanatical amphetamine abuse it was, by any reasonable standard, profoundly insane
    - In a car journey, it listened to varied radio songs for hours, but the radio wasn't on according to its sister
    - Vauung learnt that the ruin's unconscious contained an entire pop industry
    - The ruin had always abused women, in the Kantian sense. It used them as means to an end, and the end was ruin of the soul
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-112"></span>**LSD experiences with women involved accessing death realms and alien entities, feeding off their terror in vampiric spiritual consumption that revealed genuine otherworldly encounters**
    - On LSD at a fairground, the operator called out: You're all going to die
    - Let's embrace death, the Dark Mother, it said, submitting to alien ritual authority
    - First you collapse everything onto the screen. Then you wipe away the screen
    - It revealed Outside entities the size of breeze blocks approaching from the other side of space
* <span id="fanged-noumena-collected-writings-1987-2007-113"></span>**Calling to demon Can Sah resulted in alien voices that morally castigated the narrator before physically raping him, leading to complete spiritual degradation and self-disgust**
    - Calling to a being named Can Sah it was rewarded with an alien voice of absurdly high pitched, silvery tone
    - You're so horrible may have been the first message from voices that castigated it for moral squalor
    - Eventually the voices raped it physically through trickery over one unbearably protracted night of filth and misery
    - To be raped by a monster? Who knows. To be raped by celestial moralists...
