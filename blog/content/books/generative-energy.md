## Part One: Aspects of Wholeness

### Aspects of Wholeness

Holism—the recognition that parts are shaped by their participation in the whole—provides a more adequate framework for biology and medicine than genetic reductionism, because energy, not genetic information, is the central organizing force of life; optimizing biological energy through hormones, diet, and environment can reverse aging and degenerative disease.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-001"></span>**Genetic reductionism—the 'central dogma' that information flows only from DNA to RNA to protein—has been used to attribute intelligence, body proportions, and aging to genes while ignoring clear experimental evidence for environmental and transgenerational influences on these traits.**
    - When Temin and Baltimore described reverse transcription, professional biologists flatly refused to believe it was possible, illustrating how dogma blocks recognition of new evidence.
    - Marion Diamond found that pregnancy or progesterone treatment increases brain size in rats and inhibits age pigment, suggesting longevity is also increased by hormonal environment rather than fixed genetic programming.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-002"></span>**Estrogen excess, rather than estrogen deficiency, is a primary driver of aging and reproductive failure, because estrogen causes tissues to consume oxygen at rates that leave none available for the embryo or for efficient cellular function, while progesterone improves oxygenation and opposes these effects.**
    - Vitamin E deficiency causes unsaturated oils to oxidize into lipofuscin, which consumes both oxygen and fuel while producing no usable energy; estrogen synergizes with this deficiency by stimulating iron absorption and reducing oxygen availability.
    - Professor Soderwall's lab demonstrated over 25 years that aged ova were not responsible for infertility in hamsters—the uterine environment itself was unsuitable, and excess estrogen could terminate pregnancy at any stage.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-003"></span>**The physiology of aging overlaps closely with the physiology of chronic stress, as cortisol excess—triggered by estrogen dominance, unsaturated fats, and thyroid suppression—produces the characteristic features of senescence including atrophy of skin, muscle, bone, immune tissue, and brain.**
    - Thyroid hormone governs respiration and is suppressed by both estrogen and unsaturated fats; the resulting wasteful metabolism activates cortisol, whose main features of aging can be directly produced by administering excessive amounts.
    - Chronic loss of copper—displaced by heavy metals and cortisol binding to metallothionein sulfhydryl groups—accounts for loss of elasticity, depigmentation, and decreased respiratory capacity characteristic of senescence.
* <span class="pull-quote">The relation of energy to structure is, I think, the central question of biology.</span>
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-004"></span>**Life expectancy for older adults has barely improved in recent decades and was likely several years higher in ancient Rome, because industrial pollution and dietary degradation are shortening mature lives even as improved infant survival raises average population age—statistics that are deliberately obscured by 're-standardized' data.**
    - The cancer death rate for old people in the U.S. has been increasing in recent decades, contradicting claims that cancer is being defeated.
    - Removing toxic heavy metals from animal diets—without restricting calories—produces the same life-extending effect as caloric restriction, suggesting that food toxicity rather than metabolic rate is a key aging factor.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-005"></span>**Puberty triggers the aging mechanism through the action of estrogen and pituitary hormones including prolactin and ACTH, which suppress progesterone production and initiate atrophy; delaying puberty through diet, hormonal support, and a stimulating cultural environment could increase brain size, longevity, and intelligence.**
    - When pieces of breast tissue or skin were repeatedly transplanted from old to young syngeneic animals, they remained in good condition after ten 'lifetimes,' demonstrating that aging is a systemic energy problem rather than an intrinsic cellular lifespan limit.
    - Ashley Montague argued that neoteny—preserving and extending youthful functions—represents the direction of human evolution, because juvenile traits including large brain-to-body ratio and high adaptability are our most distinctively human qualities.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-006"></span>**Visible light, particularly penetrating red and infrared wavelengths, promotes mitochondrial restoration, normalizes hormone production, and regenerates key enzymes like SOD, making light deprivation (darkness and winter) a primary driver of cumulative biological damage and aging.**
    - The seed oil industry has created a phobia about saturated fats and cholesterol without scientific basis; unsaturated fats from cold-environment organisms lower metabolic rate and cause toxic peroxidation that damages the respiratory apparatus, immune system, and brain.
    - Many evolutionary trends—cephalization, G+C content of DNA correlating with oxygen use, increasing brain metabolic intensity with evolutionary level—all point toward energy intensification as the direction of biological development.

### Another View of Evolution

Life may have originated abundantly from volcanic geological processes rather than through improbable chemical accidents in a reducing atmosphere, and this shift in perspective—seeing life as an expression of the earth's own nature rather than a struggle against an inhospitable environment—reframes fundamental questions in biochemistry, evolution, and human consciousness.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-007"></span>**Order cannot emerge from disorder without a pre-existing 'formative tendency' in the starting material, so the development of biological organization is better understood as a snowball rolling downhill than as the preservation of random variations over geological time.**
    - Mendeleev demonstrated that dissolving high-carbon iron in hydrochloric acid produces a substance resembling natural petroleum in smell and character, supporting the hypothesis that petroleum is created by geological processes rather than being solely biological residue.
    - Gustaf Arrhenius at the Scripps Institute of Oceanography studied iron carbonates producing organic compounds, work separated from Mendeleev's proposals by 100 years due to a dominant worldview that sees life as a great improbability.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-008"></span>**Sidney Fox's demonstration that amino acids on hot volcanic rock spontaneously form catalytically active proteins, which then self-assemble into self-reproducing spheres the size of bacteria, suggests life originated easily and abundantly in volcanic environments rather than through rare molecular accidents.**
    - Deep ocean floor organisms discovered far below where light-dependent life should exist turn out to live on bacteria sustained by volcanic emissions, suggesting animals need not have evolved after green plants.
    - Heme-like porphyrin molecules in petroleum can catalyze respiration-like reactions, suggesting the first metabolic systems may have involved volcanic oxidants rather than molecular oxygen.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-009"></span>**Vernadsky's concept of the noosphere—a stage in geological development in which human consciousness becomes a guiding force in preserving and advancing the biosphere—frames civilization's choices as having literal geological consequences for the earth's developmental trajectory.**
    - Pierre Teilhard de Chardin coined the term 'noosphere' drawing on Vernadsky's biogeochemical work; Vernadsky distinguished it from the Gaia hypothesis by emphasizing that humans must consciously act for the world's good, as blind industrialization could destroy the biosphere entirely.
    - "Vernadsky wrote during World War II: 'I look at everything from the point of view of the noosphere and I think that in the wind and storm, in the horror and ordeal, a wonderful new future is being spontaneously born.'" —V. I. Vernadsky

### Vernadsky's Holistic Science

V. I. Vernadsky's 'cosmic realism' used quantitative physical concepts—mass, energy, migration of atoms—to derive lawful principles of biogeochemistry showing that evolution necessarily proceeds toward greater consciousness and greater influence on the environment, a framework that integrates human culture with natural science but was rejected by Anglo-American science because of its emphasis on order and direction over randomness.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-010"></span>**Vernadsky's first biogeochemical law—that migration of chemical elements tends toward a maximum—parallels Le Chatelier's principle and leads naturally to a second law: that evolution produces species which maximize elemental migration, implying a directional trend toward greater consciousness and environmental influence.**
    - Vernadsky described his view as 'cosmic realism,' seeing the biosphere as a system driven by solar energy in which biological reproduction intensity, metabolic rate, and degree of organization have orderly lawful interrelations.
    - "Vernadsky said his second biogeochemical principle 'shows, in a manner as precise as the corresponding principles in mechanics and physical chemistry, the direction in which the processes of evolution must proceed, namely, in the direction of increasing consciousness and thought.'" —V. I. Vernadsky
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-011"></span>**Anglo-American science's idealization of randomness made it systematically unable to receive Vernadsky's work, preferring instead to attribute the appearance of order to 'chaotic' processes and insisting the future is unforeseeable—a position Peat regards as terminologically confused and logically unsound.**
    - Although many of Vernadsky's concrete discoveries have been independently rediscovered in the United States, his name seldom appears in American geology or ecology texts because of his basic belief that 'chance does not exist' in nature's lawful integration.
    - Vernadsky's reading of Tolstoy shaped his moral framework: 'the basis of life is the search for truth,' and he described Tolstoy as 'an accumulator of the energy of human consciousness.'

### The Centrality of Anticipation

Biological structures do not merely record past and present conditions but anticipate future ones—a principle visible in how mitochondrial respiration supports differentiation over mere cell division, how fetal brain growth anticipates a lifetime of abundance, and how early embryonic anatomy sketches structural possibilities for a phylogenetic future not yet realized.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-012"></span>**Energy flow through matter builds structure spontaneously, as demonstrated by oscillating Belousov-Zhabotinsky reactions that generate spatial ring and spiral patterns—meaning 'structure building' involves the dimension of time in ways more interesting than simple energy dissipation.**
    - Warburg and Szent-Gyorgyi argued that glycolysis is a more primitive form of energy production supporting only cell division, while efficient mitochondrial respiration supports differentiation and complex function—a difference that matters more than most biologists have recognized.
    - ATP gradients within cells, created by localized mitochondria, have been dogmatically denied but are now recognized; polyamines associated with increased cell division shift metabolism away from respiration toward glycolysis.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-013"></span>**When a well-nourished fetus experiences low cortisol and ample progesterone, its brain grows faster than in stressed pregnancies—not merely reflecting present conditions but anticipating a lifetime of abundance, making prenatal environment a form of biological forecasting.**
    - The daily cortisol cycle produces metabolic adjustments anticipating the food-scarce night: in humans, the 'dawn phenomenon' raises blood sugar to daytime levels before sunrise, showing that even simple organisms learn cyclic repetition.
    - Ontogeny not only recapitulates phylogeny in early stages but then anticipates a phylogeny not yet realized—the fetal brain-to-body ratio continues increasing beyond the point where developmental recapitulation ends, sketching structural possibilities for future evolution.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-014"></span>**Pedomorphism or neoteny—the preservation of juvenile forms in adult descendants—can be understood as the 'aromorphic' response to a more nurturing environment, where the infantile form that requires nurturing persists to take advantage of richer energetic conditions.**
    - Baby apes resemble humans in body proportions and behavior far more than adult apes do, supporting the view that the infant represents our evolutionary future rather than our evolutionary past.
    - A proposed law of aromorphosis: 'the retardation of the flow of energy in living systems tends toward a maximum; in animals, this would imply a trend toward larger-brained, longer-lived, and probably warmer animals, having a higher energy charge.'

### The Life of Nature

Reductionism functions as a cultural mystique that uses the formal logic of deduction from parts to impose conclusions about the world—blocking biological discovery and supporting a product-oriented medical industry—while a richer 'rules of stability' approach, guided by imaginative extension rather than deductive application of current physics, can better account for the complex self-ordering of living systems.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-015"></span>**Reductionism is not merely a method but a mystique whose proponents treat it as self-evidently correct without philosophical examination, effectively functioning as the fundamentalism of 20th-century science—and this approach systematically blocks discovery even within physics itself.**
    - Michael Polanyi's multilayer adsorption isotherm was dismissed by leading German physicists at a 1915 Berlin conference as impossible given their understanding of the electrical nature of matter; the error cost physics an important branch of development for decades.
    - Sidney Fox's demonstration that amino acid polymers formed non-randomly and with catalytic activity was rejected by reductionists who insisted the forms must be random, because their belief required that biological properties arise only through long chance histories.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-016"></span>**The product-oriented medical culture is reinforced by reductionism because 'magic bullets' that destroy pathogens can be sold profitably, while the idea of the patient as a self-healing, self-organizing being requires investing in clean environments rather than marketable interventions.**
    - The dean of the Yale medical school refused to disclose the amount of money Ayerst (makers of Premarin) had given to his school while claiming research conclusions on Premarin's safety were unaffected by the gift.
    - The 'genetic defect' theory holds the promise of a gene-implant for every problem, but the scientific literature supporting thousands of claimed genetic diseases is far more anecdotal than the official literature admits—a doctor observing three family members with muscle twitches can enter a named syndrome into the literature without investigating nutritional or toxic causes.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-017"></span>**Rules of stability—simpler and more general than basic physical laws—can specify the nature of substance likely to exist in various situations, connecting practical matters of health directly to cosmology without requiring that formal deduction from known physics explain all biological phenomena.**
    - Polanyi's adsorption isotherm is a very simple rule of stability describing how gas molecules arrange themselves on a surface, making a general contribution to understanding the condensed state of matter—the same approach can yield rules governing biological fertility that simultaneously predict the organism's subsequent health, intelligence, and longevity.
    - A 'higher level of organization of substance, if insightfully described, can provide new knowledge about the nature of the parts making up the lower level'—this 'reducing up' is the inverse of the standard reductionist direction.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-018"></span>**A hypothesis must be not only consistent with facts but general enough to matter and non-deceptive by omission; the life and attitudes of the scientist shape their hypothesis, because deep involvement in a problem—rather than detached formal deduction—is what reveals the truth that is everywhere in the subject matter.**
    - Einstein explicitly recognized that a person's life cannot be separated from their hypothesis, and his attitude toward the German-dominated physics establishment should be understood in the context of his family's business being ruined by the German electric-machine monopoly.
    - Descartes explicitly claimed it didn't matter how a hypothesis was chosen—'Even supposing that I had assumed these principles at random…there would still be as good reason to suppose them the true causes'—which Peat identifies as a perfect example of the status-oriented theoretical personality Morris described.

### The Ex-Rainforests of the Pacific Northwest

Ancient rainforests function bioenergetically like a brain—maintaining intense internal metabolic flows that stabilize regional weather, water cycles, and the biosphere—making their destruction not merely an ecological loss but a self-reinforcing economic catastrophe whose costs far exceed the timber revenues extracted, while alternatives to wood-based industry exist in abundance.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-019"></span>**Rainforests are not biologically static systems but the most energetically intense zones on earth, comparable to volcanos in their power to stabilize global weather, wind, and ocean current patterns through biological rather than thermal energy—their destruction triggers self-reinforcing climate disruption.**
    - Darwin showed in the 19th century that plants' leaf-folding movements at sunset prevent radiant heat loss by radiating metabolically derived energy toward other leaves rather than the dark sky—demonstrating that organisms in a forest interact thermally and metabolically as a system.
    - According to A. V. Lapo, 'the content of water in the tissues of living organisms is approximately five times greater than in all the rivers of the globe,' and 'half the water in the roots of plants is renewed in several minutes'—making transpiration, not rivers, the primary determinant of the land water cycle.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-020"></span>**The argument that jobs in resource extraction must be protected against environmental concerns is a form of self-consuming pathology, as deforestation directly destroys the agricultural and hydrological systems on which those same regional economies depend.**
    - Gigantic Sitka spruce in Alaska are being turned into wood pulp for rayon shirts; ancient Oregon and Washington trees are made into newsprint and wrapping paper—uses that could be served by cotton, hemp, straw, bamboo, and other cellulose sources that do not require destroying irreplaceable ecosystems.
    - Biologist Curt Mitchell calculated that Oregon forests can produce at least $4,000 worth of materials per acre per year other than trees, and economist S. G. Strumilin showed money spent on education yields a 27-fold return—far exceeding extractive industry returns.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-021"></span>**A qualitative rather than quantitative economy—one that invests in education, science, culture, and human well-being rather than extracting and degrading natural systems—is both economically superior and the only path that preserves the biosphere's developmental potential.**
    - The coast redwood (Sequoia sempervirens) reaching 350 feet captures up to 50% of annual precipitation as fog caught on leaves—demonstrating that trees are not passive inhabitants of climate but active creators of it, so their removal permanently alters regional precipitation.
    - About 96% of the redwoods which once extended from San Francisco to southern Oregon are gone; only about 2 square miles remain in Oregon, illustrating the scale of irreversible loss already accomplished.

## Part Two: Energy Problems

### A Unifying Principle

Cellular energy charge governs the threshold for excitation and response in all tissues—from brain to blood vessels to immune cells—so that many diseases, from shock to arthritis to aging, can be understood as systemic energy failure, and the most effective therapies are those that simultaneously preserve existing energy and restore energy production rather than targeting symptoms directly.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-022"></span>**The cellular energy charge determines whether a cell or tissue is in a state of adaptive readiness, protective inhibition, or pathological exhaustion, and this single principle unifies the apparently diverse phenomena of brain excitability, vascular tone, immune function, and shock.**
    - Seizures can be produced by either hypoxia or hypoglycemia; in hypothyroidism, deep normal sleep is seldom possible—demonstrating that inadequate energy charge lowers the threshold for pathological excitation in brain cells.
    - The ideal therapeutic vector combines preservation of energy (via sedation or anesthesia decreasing wasteful use) with restoration of energy production (via thyroid and steroid hormones), rather than addressing symptoms in isolation.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-023"></span>**Shock is a self-defeating state in which arteriolar contraction reduces circulation, worsening the energy depletion that caused the contraction—and the appropriate treatment is restoration of cellular energy via glucose or ATP rather than adrenalin, which exacerbates arteriolar constriction.**
    - In the mid-1940s, researchers demonstrated that intravenous ATP caused animals to recover from shock that would otherwise have been fatal, but ATP is not patentable and has been dismissed as 'only a vasodilator' despite demonstrated therapeutic value.
    - Bacterial endotoxin enters the blood when stress reduces intestinal circulation and damages the gut barrier, contributing to a shock state with further circulatory impairment—a vicious circle particularly relevant to old age and 'winter sickness.'
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-024"></span>**Short and medium chain saturated fatty acids—present in coconut oil and in milk from grass-fed animals—are used more easily than glucose for cellular energy, induce hormone receptors, suppress stress enzymes, and inhibit cancer cell division, making them ideal energy sources that the modern grain-fed dairy industry has largely displaced.**
    - As little as one or two teaspoonfuls of coconut oil per day appears to have a strong protective effect against obesity and cancer, while commercial milk animals fed grain produce milk whose oils act in opposition to the short and medium chain fats naturally present.
    - Ketones such as alpha-hydroxybutyrate are used more easily than glucose in some circumstances, illustrating that the choice of fuel molecule has structural and functional repercussions beyond simple caloric value.

### Steroids

Cholesterol-derived steroid hormones—especially pregnenolone and progesterone—form a hierarchy in which upstream precursors stimulate their own synthesis, protect against the toxic excesses of downstream stress hormones, and have no harmful side effects, making them far superior to synthetic pharmaceutical steroids for therapeutic use.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-025"></span>**Progesterone and pregnenolone stimulate their own synthesis rather than suppressing it (unlike cortisol), meaning supplementation restores the body's own production capacity rather than creating dependency, and both can normalize thyroid function which is required to produce them in the first place.**
    - Progesterone also allows the thyroid gland to secrete its hormones when thyroid function has been inhibited by estrogen; since thyroid hormone is needed to produce progesterone, a supplement of either tends to normalize both, breaking a common vicious cycle.
    - Taking pregnenolone as a precursor allows the body to form specialized hormones including cortisol, aldosterone, estrogen, and testosterone in tightly regulated amounts—correcting deficiencies without creating excesses, while an excess of pregnenolone tends to balance rather than amplify specialized hormones.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-026"></span>**Progesterone dissolved in vitamin E—the only non-toxic solvent that dissolves an effective quantity—is absorbed via chylomicrons and reaches all tissues fully and quickly, making it approximately 20 times more potent than rectal or injectable preparations and effective at physiological doses of 10–20 mg.**
    - In this form progesterone is absorbed like fat into the lymphatic chylomicron pathway, bypassing the liver's inactivating glucuronide-transfer enzymes and reaching general circulation; red blood cells concentrate progesterone at twice the serum level, prolonging elevated hormone levels.
    - Progesterone's normal function during childbirth is to act as an anesthetic; in non-pregnant individuals, the first dose should never exceed 15 mg, determined by taking a few drops every 20 minutes, because excessive doses can cause serious loss of coordination.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-027"></span>**During pregnancy, very large amounts of progesterone protect and stabilize practically all maternal and fetal functions—including brain development and intelligence—while progesterone's unusual absence of harmful side effects after 50 years of medical use contrasts sharply with the serious toxicity of synthetic progestins and other pharmaceutical steroids.**
    - Pregnenolone stabilizes the cytochrome P-450 family of detoxifying enzymes against normal proteolytic degradation, increasing their activity without affecting their synthesis rate—a general stabilizing action likely explaining many anti-toxic and anti-aging effects.
    - The brain contains far more pregnenolone, DHEA, and progesterone than do other organs or blood, and these levels decrease progressively with age, suggesting that neurological decline partly reflects falling levels of these protective brain steroids.

### Thyroid

Measuring blood thyroid hormone levels is an unreliable diagnostic method because tissue response can be suppressed; accurate assessment requires functional indicators like temperature, pulse rate, and Achilles tendon reflex, and hypothyroidism—far more common than officially recognized—is the root of a wide range of conditions treatable by restoring thyroid function rather than addressing symptoms.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-028"></span>**A reliable estimate of thyroid function requires measuring temperature and pulse rate rather than blood hormone levels, because unsaturated fats and other factors can suppress tissue response to circulating hormone while leaving measured levels apparently normal.**
    - Oral or armpit temperature before rising should be around 98°F, rising to 98.6° by mid-morning; many hypothyroid people compensate with adrenalin production up to 40 times higher than normal, keeping skin cool and maintaining pulse while masking low metabolic rate.
    - The Achilles tendon reflex reveals thyroid status: in hypothyroidism the foot returns slowly and precisely to its resting position like a pneumatic door-closer rather than swinging freely and overshooting, because slow energy regeneration prevents the quick muscle relaxation that requires adequate energy repletion.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-029"></span>**The liver governs active thyroid hormone (T3) production from inactive T4, and this conversion requires glucose—so fasting or carbohydrate restriction sharply decreases metabolic rate even when thyroid gland function is adequate, and dietary protein combined with fruit can sometimes restore thyroid function without supplementation.**
    - Women often have above-average thyroxin with hypothyroid symptoms because T4 is not being converted to active T3, a problem sometimes solvable by diet alone—a piece of fruit or glass of juice between meals providing glucose for hepatic T3 formation.
    - When Cytomel (T3) is used, nibbling one tablet during the day approximates the physiological rate of formation; a large dose taken at once causes the liver to convert much of it to inactive reverse-T3 in a normal defensive response.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-030"></span>**Estrogen dominance and hypothyroidism form a self-reinforcing vicious cycle—estrogen blocks thyroid secretion, low thyroid allows estrogen to accumulate by impairing the liver's inactivation of estrogen—while the common 'health conscious' diet of undercooked vegetables, legumes, seed oils, and endurance exercise is among the most reliable ways to induce hypothyroidism.**
    - Many health-conscious people become hypothyroid by combining undercooked cruciferous vegetables, legumes instead of animal proteins, seed oils instead of butter, carotene instead of vitamin A, and aerobic exercise—a synergistic program of thyroid-suppressing inputs.
    - Thyroid function stimulates the liver to inactivate estrogen for excretion; estrogen dominance therefore tends to block thyroid secretion causing further estrogen accumulation, and progesterone—even in a single dose—can sometimes break this cycle.

### The Stress of Darkness

Darkness initiates a cascade of physiological stress—rising cortisol, falling glucose, mitochondrial damage peaking at dawn, and progressive winter accumulation of damage—that constitutes the primary environmental driver of aging, and penetrating red light is the fundamental anti-stress factor that allows mitochondrial restoration during daylight hours.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-031"></span>**Darkness directly damages mitochondria structurally and functionally beginning at sunset, with damage peaking at dawn and restoration peaking at sunset; in winter, daylight hours are insufficient to complete mitochondrial rebuilding, causing cumulative seasonal damage that only partially repairs in summer.**
    - Cortisol begins to rise as soon as there is darkness regardless of sleep or waking; artificial light and its absence can determine when this cortisol rise begins, and falling glucose is the physiological signal that triggers it.
    - In polar winter, healthy young people using ordinary artificial light for 16 hours show darkness-related signs normally seen only in older people—such as accelerated pulse—during the dark hours when lights are off.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-032"></span>**Penetrating red and infrared light—absorbed by biological copper proteins and melanin in mitochondria—promotes respiratory function restoration, and is probably the fundamental anti-stress factor for all organisms, explaining why chronic light deprivation is the best single explanation for the deterioration characteristic of aging.**
    - Red light applied within the first several minutes following x-ray or gamma irradiation protects organisms from bacteria to mammals against radiation injury, demonstrating activation of energy-dependent repair processes that extend far beyond simple photochemical effects.
    - Copper is an essential component of cytochrome oxidase, the crucial terminal respiratory enzyme, and both copper and light appear to be involved in cyanide detoxification and possibly thyroid hormone activation—suggesting copper-light interaction is fundamental to respiratory metabolism.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-033"></span>**Anti-darkness strategies—including high-thyroid function, living at altitude, GABA analogs, progesterone and pregnenolone, avoidance of unsaturated fats, extra salt at bedtime, and several hundred watts of incandescent light—collectively minimize nocturnal hypoglycemia, protect mitochondria, and oppose the stress cascade initiated by darkness.**
    - Both high elevation living and high thyroid function permanently increase the quantity of mitochondria per cell and the vascularity of tissues, increasing stress resistance—supporting the strategy of pretreatment rather than reactive treatment of stress damage.
    - Milk and various fruits contain close analogs of GABA, some of which specifically block formation of catabolic stress enzymes, making dietary choices a practical tool for minimizing darkness-induced stress.

### Pregnenolone

Pregnenolone—the mitochondrial precursor to all steroid hormones—acts as a master stabilizer of the stress-related steroid system, reversing signs of aging in skin and connective tissue, improving mental performance, and protecting enzymes against degradation, with effects that become most noticeable in winter or under stress when the body's own synthesis is most deficient.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-034"></span>**Peat's personal experience with pregnenolone during Oregon's unusually dark summer of 1983 demonstrated that a single pinch of pregnenolone could reverse within one hour a syndrome of arterial inflammation, dental abscesses, asthma, migraines, and colitis that had been worsening for months.**
    - The effect was discovered accidentally when Peat used a vitamin E bottle containing a pregnenolone residue during a two-week trip, felt perfectly well during the trip but progressively sicker before and after, and connected the recovery to the pregnenolone only on reflection.
    - Comparative passport photographs before and after ten weeks of 50–100 mg pregnenolone daily show dramatic reduction in drooping skin and eyelid heaviness, suggesting that connective tissue cells contract almost like smooth muscle under pregnenolone's influence.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-035"></span>**In a 1944 factory study, workers given pregnenolone on alternating weeks showed significantly higher piece-work output compared to placebo weeks, demonstrating that pregnenolone's effects on performance are measurable even in healthy adults under occupational stress.**
    - Healthy young people under no stress feel nothing from even very large doses (one gram) of pregnenolone, and no changes are detected in blood glucose, proteins, or salts—suggesting the hormone's effects manifest primarily when stress-related deficiency exists.
    - Papa and Kligman (1965) showed that applying pregnenolone or progesterone topically to aged skin restores youthful properties; Peat reports that grey-skinned depressed people turn pink and begin smiling shortly after taking pregnenolone.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-036"></span>**Pregnenolone stabilizes the cytochrome P-450 detoxifying enzyme family against proteolytic degradation, increasing their activity—a general stabilizing action that also explains anti-toxic effects including blocking hemolysis and controlling cell division.**
    - A woman with severely protruding eyes from Graves' disease reportedly experienced near-complete recession of the eyeballs within one hour of taking pregnenolone, consistent with the substance's known ability to reduce fluid accumulation in orbital tissues.
    - After several years of use, signs of aging began returning, suggesting that ongoing supplementation or enhancement of endogenous synthesis via coconut oil and incandescent light exposure is required to maintain effects, especially in northern winters.

### Restoring Hair Color

Hair depigmentation results from an imbalance of trace minerals—particularly excess iron displacing copper—combined with hormonal factors such as cortisol excess and thyroid deficiency that deprive melanin-producing cells of the oxygen and copper they require, and color can be at least partially restored by correcting these deficiencies through diet, topical application, and hormonal support.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-037"></span>**A Japanese researcher demonstrated that each hair color corresponds to a characteristic pattern of trace minerals, and that removing all trace minerals turns all hair white while adding specific mineral patterns restores specific colors—proving that inherited hair color is actually an inherited tendency to concentrate particular minerals.**
    - Black sheep produce white wool when fed excess molybdenum, supposedly by displacing copper from melanin-producing enzymes, paralleling the human pattern of iron accumulation with aging displacing copper.
    - Physicians have noted depigmented spots appearing in skin over sites of cortisone injection, and the association of severe stress with sudden greying of hair suggests that excess cortisol directly destroys melanin or the melanin-producing system.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-038"></span>**Peat's personal experiment in restoring eyebrow hair color through a low-iron diet, copper supplementation from a copper pan, vitamin A without preservative, DHEA, pregnenolone, and topical copper acetate resulted in measurable darkening of white hairs from the root within a few weeks.**
    - The experiment produced three hairs progressively darkening from the root upward over several weeks, with the third hair all black except for 3 millimeters at the tip that had begun growing before the dietary interventions took effect—demonstrating temporal correspondence between the intervention and pigment production.
    - Steroids known to reverse structural age changes in skin—progesterone, testosterone, pregnenolone—sometimes restore hair growth and occasionally produce pigmented hair, while estrogen and cortisone accelerate structural aging in skin.

### Arthritis and Hormone Balancing

Arthritis and other degenerative diseases typically arise from hormonal imbalances rooted in hypothyroidism and stress-induced depletion of progesterone and related protective steroids, not from unknown causes requiring lifelong pharmaceutical management, and can often be resolved by identifying and correcting the underlying hormonal deficiency.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-039"></span>**Hypothyroidism is a common and overlooked cause of arthritis because low thyroid function directly impairs joint tissue, causes adrenal insufficiency, and creates a vicious cycle in which stress produces cortisol deficiency while simultaneously being worsened by it—a case study of a 71-year-old man illustrates complete arthritis resolution within two weeks of thyroid supplementation alone.**
    - Hans Selye's research showed that stress exhausting the adrenal glands to the point where cortisone and related hormones fall triggers arthritis and other degenerative diseases in rats, establishing the hormonal basis of these conditions.
    - The patient in the case study lived to 89 without recurrence of arthritis after thyroid treatment, demonstrating that treating the root cause rather than the symptom produces genuine and lasting resolution.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-040"></span>**Natural protective steroids—progesterone, pregnenolone, and DHEA—can substitute for cortisone in treating stress-related diseases, protect against cortisone's toxic side effects, have anti-inflammatory and anesthetic actions, and have shown no toxic side effects after 50 years of medical use, making them vastly superior to pharmaceutical corticosteroids.**
    - Some forms of arthritis improve or disappear during pregnancy, when progesterone (the most abundant pregnancy hormone) exerts anti-inflammatory and anesthetic effects; the hormones of pregnancy can substitute for adrenal cortex hormones.
    - A drug company representative told Peat that a substance which cures arthritis is not a desirable product from his company's point of view—a good drug is one that must be used for the rest of the patient's life—illustrating the commercial incentive against effective treatments.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-041"></span>**Topical application of progesterone to inflamed tendons, damaged cartilage, or arthritic joints typically relieves pain within minutes; among more than 30 arthritis cases Peat observed using natural hormones, nearly all showed significant improvement, demonstrating practical clinical effectiveness.**
    - Meerson's work showed that the normal hormone of adaptation (cortisone) causes many harmful effects when prolonged or intense, including hypertension, osteoporosis, cataracts, and loss of immunity—and that heart attacks are provoked and aggravated by cortisone produced during stress.
    - DHEA is being studied for anti-aging, anti-cancer, and anti-obesity effects, but has not been widely investigated because as a natural substance it cannot be patented—illustrating how the commercial structure of medical research creates systematic gaps in knowledge of effective treatments.

### The Carpal Tunnel Syndrome

Carpal tunnel syndrome and related conditions of tendon and nerve compression typically arise from a systemic hormonal imbalance—estrogen-induced thyroid suppression leading to myxedematous fluid retention—rather than from local mechanical overuse, so surgical or cortisone interventions address symptoms while worsening the underlying systemic condition.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-042"></span>**Women are several times more likely than men to develop carpal tunnel syndrome and related conditions because estrogen blocks thyroid function, leading to compensatory adrenalin and cortisol elevation, hypoglycemia, histamine excess, and the leakage of glycoproteins from blood vessels that causes tendon, ligament, and nerve sheath swelling.**
    - Patients with myxedema (hypothyroid edema) typically have mitral valve prolapse, and women with premenstrual syndrome often have a mitral valve murmur premenstrually but not at other times, demonstrating that jelly-like fluid accumulation can form and resolve rapidly under hormonal influence.
    - In chronic hypothyroidism, cartilage in joints swells and deforms causing characteristic knock-kneed appearance; milder forms produce joint pain without inflammation, calf muscle swelling after activity, and the 'growing pains' common at puberty.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-043"></span>**Surgical treatment of carpal tunnel syndrome is inferior to conservative management because the underlying cause is systemic hormonal imbalance rather than a local mechanical problem—just as research shows surgery is inferior to bed rest for ruptured spinal discs, but the medical industry controls public opinion through media to protect profitable surgical interventions.**
    - When a tendon keeps recurring as a problem or appears in different body areas, there is probably a general hormonal problem rather than a local one, and local treatment produces only temporary results.
    - Natural anti-stress and anti-edema hormones have also relieved symptoms ascribed to glaucoma, Graves' disease orbital swelling, epilepsy, and multiple sclerosis—conditions sharing the common mechanism of fluid accumulation in sensitive tissues.

### The Premenstrual Syndrome

Premenstrual syndrome results from an imbalance in the estrogen-to-progesterone ratio recognized since the 1930s, and is most effectively treated with natural progesterone dissolved in vitamin E for chylomicron absorption—a form far more potent and efficient than suppositories or injections—alongside thyroid support, dietary measures, and light exposure that collectively normalize mitochondrial respiration.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-044"></span>**The toxic effects of estrogen—cancer, infertility, blood clotting, edema, seizures, birth defects, and shock-like stress reactions—were clearly documented by the early 1940s, but the pharmaceutical industry has continued promoting estrogen while suppressing the recognition that the hormone imbalance causing PMS also predisposes women to serious organic disease.**
    - As early as 1912, Armour & Co. was selling desiccated corpus luteum for ovarian failure and nervous symptoms accompanying menstrual abnormalities; by the mid-1930s pure progesterone was on the market in both the U.S. and Europe for treating PMS, pregnancy problems, and menopause.
    - Only natural progesterone—not synthetic progestins—is recognized as having general value in PMS treatment, because natural progesterone has broad spectrum intrinsic effects and is easily metabolized into other active hormones as needed, acting as a basic precursor substance.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-045"></span>**Progesterone dissolved in vitamin E in a monomolecular dispersion achieves absorption via chylomicrons—reaching the general circulation rather than the liver's inactivating enzymes—making it roughly 20 times more efficient than crystalline preparations, and a single 100 mg dose in a post-menopausal woman maintains normal luteal-phase serum levels for 24 hours.**
    - Suppositories provide only 5% absorption of their progesterone content and are expensive and messy; injectable progesterone in 10% benzyl alcohol causes nerve toxicity and can produce anaphylaxis, yet has been beneficial in spite of these problems due to progesterone's powerful protective action.
    - Progesterone concentrates in red blood cells at twice the serum level and at still higher concentrations in the brain, creating intracellular reservoirs that stabilize blood levels far more than triglyceride levels are stabilized after a fatty meal.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-046"></span>**A practical first-line approach to PMS includes avoiding thyroid-suppressing dietary and lifestyle factors, using a grated carrot salad with vinegar and coconut oil to normalize bowel flora and hormone levels, and only then supplementing with progesterone or pregnenolone if dietary measures are insufficient.**
    - The carrot salad works through multiple mechanisms: acetic acid from vinegar, medium-chain fatty acids from coconut oil, fiber as a timed-release system binding toxins and stimulating the bowel, and salt inhibiting excessive prolactin release.
    - Both thyroid supplement and progesterone normalize thyroid function and have catatoxic protective effects against a wide range of toxins—addressing the root metabolic cause of PMS rather than only its symptomatic expressions.

### Restoring Fertility

Fertility depends on normalizing oxygen availability through hormonal and nutritional balance rather than on age per se—historical records document pregnancy well past menopause, and miscarriages at any stage result primarily from estrogen-induced oxygen deprivation of the embryo, all of which can be corrected by restoring thyroid, progesterone, and adequate nutritional support.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-047"></span>**Historical medical records document healthy pregnancies in women well past menopause—including cases at 59, 60, 62, and reportedly even 70—suggesting that the assumption that childbearing ends definitively around age 50 is a cultural taboo rather than a biological law, and that hormonal balance matters far more than chronological age.**
    - A woman who had early menopause at 47, married a 22-year-old man at 52, and had children at 53 and 54 illustrates that a partner who optimizes the couple's fertility can extend a woman's reproductive window significantly beyond conventional expectations.
    - The assumption that calcified fetuses found at autopsy in very old women must have remained in utero for decades reflects cultural bias; no one actually knows how long a calcified fetus can persist in the uterus.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-048"></span>**Miscarriage is primarily caused by estrogen-induced oxygen deprivation of the embryo—a six-day-old embryo suddenly requires oxygen at implantation and even a small estrogen excess prevents implantation, while excess estrogen at any later stage can destroy the fetus by overriding progesterone's oxygen-supplying effects.**
    - The most common time for miscarriages is the ninth week, when the placenta takes over hormone production and there is normally a brief decrease in the progesterone-to-estrogen ratio—resembling the hormonal pattern of menstruation—which can trigger bleeding or resorption.
    - Morning sickness, temporary blindness, fainting, and seizures after the fifth month result from fetal hypoglycemia as the rapidly growing brain consumes increased glucose while estrogen dominance restricts oxygen and promotes excess glucose use.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-049"></span>**Women who used progesterone before getting pregnant had babies with roughly one-tenth the normal incidence of congenital defects, and women who used sizable amounts during pregnancy had outstandingly superior babies—demonstrating that the conditions which sustain fertility also optimize the life of the child.**
    - Both thyroid and progesterone should be considered for improving fertility and preventing miscarriage; thyroid triiodothyronine (T3) is a key energy element for pregnancy to begin, but sufficient circulating cholesterol is also essential to synthesize the required progesterone and other steroids.
    - Thyroid and pregnenolone are as important for male fertility as thyroid and progesterone are for female fertility; supplementary thyroid and pregnenolone can raise a man's sperm count by overcoming the effects of stress.

## Part Three: Regenerating Knowledge

### Youth, Energy, and Regeneration

Higher metabolic rate and greater biological energy—not caloric restriction or slow metabolism—are associated with greater longevity across species, and the aging process is best understood as a systemic energy failure driven by accumulated toxins, unsaturated fats, estrogen excess, and mitochondrial damage rather than as an intrinsic biological program, making regeneration possible through restoring the conditions of youthful energy metabolism.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-050"></span>**Multiple lines of evidence contradict the metabolic potential theory that faster living shortens life: birds live longer than same-weight mammals despite higher body temperatures, high-metabolizing strains of mice outlive low-metabolizing strains, and passerine birds produce four times more energy per gram per lifespan than mammals.**
    - A study of 18 strains of mice found a clear association between higher metabolic rate and greater longevity; M. A. Hoffman showed that the ratio of cortex-to-brain metabolic rates increases with evolutionary level of brain development.
    - A medium-sized parrot lives approximately 70 years at 104°F body temperature while a rat lives about 2 years at 98°F—directly contradicting the prediction that higher body temperature and metabolism should shorten lifespan.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-051"></span>**The removal of toxic heavy metals from the diet—without any caloric restriction—produces the same life-extending effect as caloric restriction in animal experiments, suggesting that the apparent benefits of calorie restriction are largely due to reduced toxic load rather than reduced metabolic activity per se.**
    - Animals on a low-protein diet have a higher rate of oxygen consumption than those eating more normal diets, and animals deficient in 'essential fatty acids' show signs of hyperthyroidism and a hyper-metabolic state—both contradicting the premise that food restriction works by slowing metabolism.
    - Dietary restriction enhances protein turnover rate in aging animals, reversing one of the basic metabolic changes of aging; unsaturated fats and the amino acid cysteine may both contribute to the age-related retardation of protein metabolism.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-052"></span>**Early sexual maturity—triggered by unsaturated oils, relative overfeeding, and low thyroid function—initiates aging by activating estrogen and prolactin which interfere with respiration; tropical populations with the latest average puberty (around 17) also show the heaviest birth weights, consistent with high-quality nutrition supporting both delayed maturation and superior fetal development.**
    - Marion Diamond found that pregnancy or progesterone treatment caused rat brains to grow—as did freedom and stimulation—while estrogen and stress caused brain shrinkage; the larger-brained animals had smaller faces, paralleling the relationship between prenatal progesterone and human cephalization.
    - W. Denckla's 'death hormone' hypothesis—a pituitary hormone appearing at puberty that suppresses oxygen use—is interpreted by Peat as simply describing puberty itself and the effects of estrogen and prolactin, not a distinct programmed death mechanism.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-053"></span>**H. G. Wells described Homo sapiens as a neotenous species—an 'experimental, playful, teachable, precocious child, still amenable to social subordination when already sexually adult'—and Peat extends this to argue that progesterone's effects in prolonging youthful traits represent the biological basis of our most characteristically human evolutionary features.**
    - Progesterone's 'neotenous' effects include larger brain-to-body ratio, smaller face-to-cranium ratio, less aggressiveness, and greater adaptability—traits that are also more pronounced in women than in men, consistent with women's evolutionary role as carriers of neotenous progress.
    - The cranial volume keeps increasing into old age and the DNA content of the brain keeps increasing during adulthood; the old doctrine that 100,000 brain cells die every day is now recognized as having no basis in fact, and brain cell regeneration in vivo has some experimental support.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-054"></span>**Optimizing mitochondrial protection through known factors—red light, short and medium chain saturated fats, pregnenolone—could give the average adult roughly a 50% increase in biological energy and, at sufficient levels, could make the regenerative healing abilities of a ten-year-old child available at any age.**
    - The Mayans and Eskimos studied by Crile produced 25% more biological energy at rest than people in the U.S. and Europe; what they share is likely the absence of thyroid-inhibiting substances in their diets, despite being otherwise culturally and nutritionally distinct.
    - Stress and energy depletion—not the wear and tear of living itself—are common causes of chromosome damage and free radical injury; since the DNA repair process is energy dependent, greater biological energy prevents mutations, directly contradicting the 'original sin of metabolism' theories of aging.

### The Tradition of Truth

Truth in science and art is not discovered by theoretical detachment but produced through deep engagement with skilled work—a tradition of apprenticeship in meaningful labor that develops the perceptual acuity necessary to see what is actually there—while status-oriented professional culture actively blocks truth by making it irrelevant to the rewards of belonging.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-055"></span>**The history of science shows a systematic difference between productive classes with craft apprenticeship traditions—who produce truthful, sensuous, realistic work—and governing classes whose inherited status produces theoretical, abstract, formulaic work divorced from direct engagement with the subject matter.**
    - Faraday, the blacksmith's son, represents the experimental truth-seeking attitude in physics, while Lord Kelvin represents the pompous errors of the status-oriented class; painters from poor craft backgrounds like Frans Hals show truth in their work, while noble-origin painters like Rubens show only flattery and grandiosity.
    - William Morris identified two cultures—constructive 'eye people' whose work is characterized by sensuous realism, and analytical minds concerned with formulas and formalisms—and observed that these types express themselves systematically differently in art, science, and social life.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-056"></span>**Broda Barnes solved much of 'the riddle of heart attacks' through thyroid supplementation 50 years ago, but his clearly presented and scientifically sound work has been systematically ignored by the health industry—illustrating how medicine's inherited professional status functions like nobility rather than apprenticeship, making truth irrelevant to institutional rewards.**
    - The resistance to Barnes's discoveries has the same explanation as Rubens's fame: those choosing the profession experience family status before any experience with healing or special skills, receiving an inherited sense that belonging is enough to be effective.
    - Ukhtomskii described clear perception as created by 'a great physical effort, a tradition handed down from others, and an attitude toward the world as toward a beloved, respected companion'—contrasting with the status-seeking that makes 'the truth everywhere' invisible.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-057"></span>**Julien de la Mettrie's 18th-century physiology of organization—proposing that thought is as natural for the brain as beating is for the heart, and that matter is an artist of unlimited capacities—was suppressed in favor of Descartes' mind-matter dualism because dualism could compromise with religious authority, and this compromise still shapes scientific institutions today.**
    - De la Mettrie observed that his thoughts were quicker when his pulse was accelerated by fever—a physiological observation verified by recent experiments nearly 250 years later—demonstrating that truthful physiology was available before its suppression.
    - Descartes claimed it didn't matter how a hypothesis was chosen—'Even supposing that I had assumed these principles at random…there would still be as good reason to suppose them the true causes'—a claim Peat identifies as the perfect expression of the status-oriented, non-apprentice scientific personality.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-058"></span>**When people gain a sense of possibility through liberation from oppressive conditions—as observed in Cuba after the revolution—they ignite mentally, creating, planning, and collaborating with a vitality that had appeared absent, demonstrating that the 'sense of possibility' is a biological and cultural phenomenon dependent on social conditions.**
    - William Blake identified factories as 'Satanic Mills' that used false science, devious moralizing, and illiteracy to create cultures of obedient drudgery; he saw oppression as causing the same psychic contraction that Peat identifies with low biological energy.
    - Philippe Aries showed in Centuries of Childhood that childhood as a separate life stage is a modern phenomenon; historically children participated in family work as apprentices from an early age, developing personal power through direct effectiveness rather than inherited status.

### The Expanding Earth

Geological, cosmological, and biological evidence points toward a universe of continuous creation and expanding matter—including an Earth that may have grown significantly over geological time—and this expanding-earth metaphor captures the fundamental orientation of Peat's entire project: that there is no foreseeable limit to the expansion of energy, perception, and understanding, only the inertia of fixed beliefs.

* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-059"></span>**Geological evidence—including the parallel ridges of the Atlantic Ocean floor, the ability to fit Pacific Ocean margins together with Australia against South America, and fossilized sand dunes too steep for present gravity—points to an expanding Earth whose surface may be growing at roughly 3 square kilometers per year.**
    - S. Warren Carey surveyed the physical processes that might account for the geological facts pointing toward expansion, arguing that all continents drifting apart simultaneously is impossible unless the Earth itself has expanded within the last few hundred million years.
    - N. A. Kozyrev theorized that time's asymmetry is a source of stellar energy, predicted that planets would have steady internal heat in proportion to mass, and measured hot lunar emissions in 1960—predictions later confirmed by space exploration that also matched observed Earth heat and implied Jupiter would be nearly star-like in heat emission.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-060"></span>**Fred Hoyle's spectroscopic evidence that cosmic dust more closely resembles bacteria than other proposed particles, combined with Vernadsky's biogeochemistry and Mendeleev's geological origin of petroleum, supports a view of the universe as fundamentally biological and creative rather than mechanically entropic and dead.**
    - Frederick Soddy suggested that cosmic rays might represent newly formed atoms, and that permeation of space by ionized atoms moving at high speed would produce evenly distributed microwave radiation—first proposing what others later used to argue for the Big Bang, but in the context of continuous creation.
    - H. C. Dudley argued that a neutrino background could explain nuclear processes and that unknown simple conditions might catalyze nuclear fission or fusion under ordinary conditions; experimentalist Anderson independently demonstrated non-random nuclear decay in monomolecular layers of radioactive carbon on aluminum foil.
* <span id="generative-energy-restoring-the-wholeness-of-life-061"></span>**The expanding earth is a metaphor for the entire argument of the book: that matter, life, and consciousness are not running down toward entropy but are developing, that there is no foreseeable limit to expansion, and that the only thing resisting the constructive organizing flow of energy is the absorptive principle of disorganization—which has a limit, while expansion does not.**
    - Peat applies Vernadsky's reformulation of Le Chatelier's principle to perception itself: 'systems use the energy that's available, and our perceptions and social understandings are supported by energy flowing into the whole system'—the availability of appropriate foods, fields, and stimuli being the only limiting factor.
    - S. W. Carey's image of the earth as an opening bud parallels Blake's image of 'the Gates of Paradise'—hatching from the Mundane Shell of fixed beliefs—as a metaphor for the situation of human knowledge at the threshold of qualitative development.
