## The Beast in the Cave

The introduction establishes that Crowley's 1904 Cairo reception of the Book of the Law and Lovecraft's simultaneous composition of 'The Beast in the Cave' represent a single occult transmission, and that the dark or 'nightside' of existence—concealed within religion, politics, and the unconscious—is the proper subject of this study of the Typhonian Current.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-001"></span>**Lovecraft's earliest story 'The Beast in the Cave,' written at age fourteen while Crowley was receiving the Book of the Law in Cairo in 1904, reveals a Beast that is ultimately a man—suggesting that Lovecraft was psychically receiving the same transmission Crowley was consciously channeling.**
    - Crowley identified himself as 'the Beast' throughout his life, linking himself to the Beast of Revelation; Lovecraft's story inverts this by revealing the monster in the cave to be human.
    - Lovecraft's stories frequently involve dream communications and psychic transmission through sensitive artists, making inadvertent reception of Crowley's Cairo working plausible within his own fictional framework.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-002"></span>**The 'dark side' or nightside of existence is the foundational territory of occultism, containing the prima materia of life itself, and is accessible only by those willing to peer into the suppressed depths of human consciousness.**
    - Kabbalists called this realm the Sitra Ahra, ruled by the damaged gods known as the qlippoth; Tantrikas, Freud, Reich, and Jung each approached it from different angles.
    - Modern surveillance technology and social media are progressively colonizing the unconscious, making genuine interiority and secret society activity increasingly difficult.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-003"></span>**The book's central thesis is that Kenneth Grant's Typhonian Order identifies the Thelemic, Necronomicon, and Egyptian magical currents as three strands of a single ancient tradition originating not on earth but in the stars, a claim that would require reconsidering all of human religious history.**
    - Grant insisted throughout nine volumes of Typhonian Trilogies that the Schlangekraft recension of the Necronomicon and Crowley's Thelema are not merely compatible but identical in their magical current.
    - "What Grant is saying is that all religions and especially all antinomian cults have their origin in a single cult, a single magical order, that has its origin not on earth but in the stars." —Kenneth Grant
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-004"></span>**The author positions this study as an independent deconstruction of Grant's Necronomicon Gnosis, explicitly disclaiming membership in any magical order and treating the material analytically rather than doctrinally.**
    - The author notes that many issues discussed will be considered heretical by doctrinaire Thelemites, but frames the works of Frater Achad, A.O. Spare, and Jack Parsons as potential 'gnostic gospels' to mainstream Thelema's official canon.
    - Kenneth Grant's gematria is described as 'off the charts and internally inconsistent,' yet his passion and discovery of profound connections justify serious engagement.

## Strange Aeons

This chapter examines the concept of the Aeon as used by Crowley—rooted in Egyptian mythology, Gnostic theology, and Golden Dawn ritual—and demonstrates that the Aeon of Horus, far from being a purely solar new age, conceals a darker stellar current associated with Set-Aiwass that connects to Lovecraft's own cosmological horrors.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-005"></span>**The Book of the Law was received in Cairo in April 1904 through Crowley's wife Rose Kelly as intermediary, structured around three Egyptian deities—Nuit, Hadit, and Ra-Hoor-Khuit—whose specific combination is unique to Crowley and cannot be found in standard Egyptological sources.**
    - Rose drew Crowley's attention to the Stele of Revealing (exhibit number 666 in the Boulaq Museum), a burial plaque for the priest Ankh-ef-en-khonsu of the god Mentu, which became the central icon of the new religion.
    - The three-chapter structure of the Book of the Law mimics Egyptian divine trinities (e.g., Osiris-Isis-Horus), but the specific triad Nuit-Hadit-Ra-Hoor-Khuit has no precedent in Egyptian texts and represents Crowley's unique synthesis.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-006"></span>**Crowley's intermediary entity Aiwass was identified by Crowley himself as Set-Aiwass or Shaitan-Aiwass, linking the communicating intelligence to the Egyptian god of chaos and storms and to the Mesopotamian adversary figure, and suggesting an origin in pre-dynastic Sumer rather than dynastic Egypt.**
    - "Aiwaz is not a mere formula, like many Angelic names, but it is the true, most ancient name of the God of the Yezidi, and thus returns to the highest antiquity." —Aleister Crowley
    - Set was said to reside in the Great Bear constellation and helped souls attain immortality; the Greeks at the time of Herodotus identified Set with their own sea monster Typhon, giving Kenneth Grant's order its name.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-007"></span>**The Book of the Law is only fully comprehensible within the context of Golden Dawn initiation rituals, which provided Crowley the symbolic vocabulary through which the Egyptian gods communicated, making Thelema historically a development of nineteenth-century British occultism rather than a genuinely independent revelation.**
    - The 'Equinox of the Gods' described in the Book of the Law directly mirrors the Golden Dawn Equinox ritual in which Horus (the Hiereus) replaces Osiris (the Hierophant) at the change of season—what was performed ritually in Golden Dawn temples occurred cosmically on March 20, 1904.
    - Certain phrases in the Book of the Law 'make no sense whatsoever without reference' to Golden Dawn rituals, indicating the Egyptian deities used Crowley's initiation as the channel for communication.
* <span class="pull-quote">The sciences, each straining in its own direction, have hitherto harmed us little; but some day the piecing together of dissociated knowledge will open up such terrifying vistas of reality, and of our frightful position therein, that we shall either go mad from the revelation or flee from the deadly light into the peace and safety of a new dark age.</span>
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-008"></span>**Frater Achad's proclamation of the Aeon of Maat in 1948, only forty-four years after Crowley's Aeon of Horus, is not necessarily contradictory because Gnostic Aeons run concurrently as divine emanations, Hindu yugas overlap, and Kabbalistic shemittot allow for sub-cycles within greater cycles.**
    - The Valentinian Gnostics understood thirty Aeons as divine male-female pairs (syzygies) existing simultaneously in the Pleroma—they were not sequential ages but concurrent emanations, making an Aeon of Maat running parallel to the Aeon of Horus entirely consistent.
    - Using Kabbalistic shemittot calculations based on the Sefer ha-Temunah, the Aeon of Osiris could have begun around 5,096 BCE and the Aeon of Isis around 12,096 BCE, providing the historical coordination that Crowley's 2,150-year precessional model fails to supply.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-009"></span>**Grant's anxiety, expressed throughout the Typhonian Trilogies, is that the earth is being infiltrated by extraterrestrial beings whose return was anticipated by ancient cults and is now being facilitated by magical operations—a position directly paralleled by Lovecraft's horror fiction about the Great Old Ones.**
    - The Necronomicon states that the Ancient Ones' times and seasons 'outside run uneven and strange to our minds, for are they not the Computors of All Time? Did They not set Time in its Place?'
    - The idea that a human being on earth is in secret telepathic communication with the Great Old Ones and preparing the way for their return is the central Lovecraftian theme that Grant maps onto Thelemic doctrine.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-010"></span>**Crowley's schema of Aeons reflects a universalist anthropological assumption—common in early twentieth-century occultism—that all religions share a common Ur-cult, but this position is Occident-centric and requires the global expansion provided by Grant's incorporation of Afro-Caribbean and Asian traditions.**
    - Crowley's interpretation of the Book of the Law focuses almost exclusively on defeating a western obsession with sin and Christianity, rendering it a form of 'spiritual colonialism' that ransacks Asian religions for tools useful within a Euro-centric framework.
    - The importance of the Kenneth Grant material lies in his attempt to create a more global character for Thelema by incorporating Afro-Caribbean and Asian elements in far more detail and with much greater insight than was available in the Crowley writings.

## Gods, the Beast, and Men

This chapter decodes the theological structure of Thelema through its Gnostic Mass Creed, Crowley's identification with the Beast 666 and Babalon, and the pivotal role of Set-Typhon as the Dark Lord—demonstrating through parallel passages from Crowley's Holy Books and Lovecraft's 'The Call of Cthulhu' that both men were receiving the same transmission from the same source.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-011"></span>**Crowley's Thelemic Creed in the Gnostic Mass identifies the first principle of the universe not as Osiris or Horus but as CHAOS—the Greek name for the Egyptian god Set—thus revealing that the dark stellar current of Set-Typhon is the true foundation of Thelema, not the solar current of Horus.**
    - The Creed identifies CHAOS as the 'Father of Life' and sole vice-regent of the Sun on earth, while BABALON is 'the Mother of us all' and the Earth itself—these two Ur-gods precede all other deities in the Thelemic schema.
    - The 'one Star in the company of Stars of whose fire we are created, and to which we shall return' implies extra-terrestrial origins for humanity and points—in Grant's reading—to the Pole Star and its circumpolar attendants the Big Dipper, known in Egypt as the Thigh of Set.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-012"></span>**Crowley's identification with the Beast 666 was originally a form of self-therapy—reclaiming the suppressive label his Plymouth Brethren mother applied to him—but became enshrined as a doctrine in which the Beast represents the enemy of all restriction, especially sexual restriction, equated with Set as the god of transgression.**
    - The Book of the Law states 'The word of Sin is Restriction,' a play on the etymology of 'religion' from the Latin ligare meaning 'to bind'—Crowley as the Beast thus aligns himself with the enemy of religion itself.
    - By identifying himself positively with the Beast, Crowley was taking the suppressed and repressed elements of a personality molded by a normative and restrictive Christian sect and liberating them by re-characterizing them as positive values.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-013"></span>**Crowley's Holy Books of 1907 contain direct verbal parallels to Lovecraft's 'The Call of Cthulhu,' including the word 'Tutulu' (cognate with Cthulhu), tentacled fish-monsters from the Abyss, earthquakes, and a sunken god—and Lovecraft dated the orgiastic Cthulhu rites in his story to the exact same day Crowley was writing these texts.**
    - In Liber Liberi vel Lapidus Lazuli (written October 30 - November 1, 1907) Crowley writes the word 'Tutulu' for the first time and claims not to know its origin; Lovecraft's story places the New Orleans Cthulhu ritual on Halloween 1907—the same day.
    - Liber Cordis Cincti Serpente contains: 'I trembled at Thy coming ... He was an old and gnarled fish more hideous than the shells of Abaddon. He enveloped me with his demon tentacles'—descriptions that match Lovecraft's Cthulhu not approximately but perfectly.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-014"></span>**Set's character in Egyptian religion—unnatural birth, sexual transgression including attempted sodomy of Horus, identification with foreign lands and alien forces, and association with the circumpolar stars—makes him the natural patron deity of Tantric transgressive sexuality and the Dark Lord of Grant's Typhonian system.**
    - The famous 'Contendings of Horus and Set' describe Set's attempt to sodomize Horus, which resulted in the creation of the Eye of Horus; Horus then deposited his own semen on lettuce (Set's favorite food), causing Set to be orally 'penetrated'—the Moon was born from Set's forehead as a result.
    - Grant's Typhonian system elevates Set above Horus and Osiris in relevance because where Osiris and Horus represent the solar-east aspect of Egyptian religion, Set represents the stellar-north aspect, identified with the Great Bear constellation and the circumpolar realm.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-015"></span>**Crowley's Star Sapphire ritual (Liber XXXVI), which so startled OTO founder Theodor Reuss that he immediately initiated Crowley into the IXth degree, invokes 'Set Triumphant' at its climax and encodes the secret of transgressive sexual magic as the key to opening the Nightside of the Tree of Life.**
    - The Star Sapphire's Latin formula pairs Father-Mother, Mother-Son, Son-Daughter, and Daughter-Father in incestuous couplings that, in sexual-magical interpretation, represent the consumption of a Sacrament composed of mingled male and female fluids—analogous to the Tantric amrita.
    - The invocation of Set Triumphant at the climax indicates the unlocking of a Gate between this world and the Nightside of the Tree of Life—not the death-and-resurrection gateway of Osiris but the Tunnels of Set leading to the Mauve Zone.

## Unspeakable Cults

Grant's expansion of Thelema beyond Crowley's Eurocentric framework required engagement with three 'unspeakable cults'—the Yezidis, Afro-Caribbean Vodun, and Indian Tantra—each of which preserves fragments of the ancient Typhonian current and provides technologies of transgressive sexuality and altered consciousness that the Western tradition had suppressed or lost.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-016"></span>**The Yezidi of Kurdistan, popularly maligned as 'devil worshippers,' are significant to Grant because their reverence for Shaitan (the Fallen Angel), their lettuce taboo (shared with Set in Egyptian myth), and their official seal bearing cuneiform and the eight-pointed Sumerian star for 'god' suggest they are remnants of an ancient Sumerian-Typhonian current directly connected to Crowley's Aiwass.**
    - The Yezidi claim Sumerian ancestry and their sacred seal depicts the eight-pointed star used in cuneiform as the symbol for 'god'—consistent with Grant's thesis that the Thelemic current originates in Sumer.
    - Recent scholarship has shown that the two published 'sacred texts' of the Yezidi—the Black Book and the Book of Revelation—are most likely nineteenth-century forgeries by an antiquities dealer, yet they are acknowledged to accurately reflect actual Yezidi beliefs.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-017"></span>**Michael Bertiaux's synthesis of Haitian Vodun, Thelema, and Lovecraftian cosmology provided Grant with a verification that the Thelemic current had to be globally inclusive, and his concept of points chauds—'hot points' or power-zones that serve as portals to other dimensions—became central to Grant's understanding of the Mauve Zone.**
    - Bertiaux's Voudon Gnostic Workbook combined western esotericism, Afro-Caribbean concepts, and sex magic within a Thelemic context by 1972, offering Grant confirmation that analogues to the Typhonian current existed in the Haitian tradition.
    - The points chauds exist simultaneously on the human body and at specific terrestrial and cosmic locations, in perfect microcosm-macrocosm symmetry—physical stimulation of bodily power-points is equivalent to opening celestial gates.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-018"></span>**The Afro-Caribbean religions of Haitian Vodun, including the secret societies such as Bizango and Zobop, contain genuine magical technologies—including the zombie powder and possession rituals—that correspond to the Typhonian emphasis on contact with supramundane entities and cannot be dismissed as mere superstition or colonialist projection.**
    - Wade Davis's ethnobiological fieldwork, culminating in The Serpent and the Rainbow, demonstrated that Haitian secret societies perform much the same function as alleged Illuminati or Freemasons in European countries, operating behind the scenes as real masters of Haitian communities.
    - The obeah and wanga mentioned in the Book of the Law (AL I:37) refer explicitly to Afro-Caribbean magic rather than religion, specifically to practical occult methods used by spiritual specialists—suggesting the author of Liber AL intended these darker arts to be incorporated into the Thelemic arsenal.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-019"></span>**David Curwen's deep knowledge of Indian Tantra, obtained through actual practice in India, revealed to both Crowley and Grant that the sexual mysteries at the heart of the OTO were best explained and amplified through Tantric texts and practices—particularly Sri Vidya and the Kaula circles—which Grant then synthesized with Western ceremonial magic.**
    - Curwen, 'outed' in 2006 as the author of the alchemical work In Pursuit of Gold (1976) written under the pseudonym Lapidus, maintained an intimate correspondence with Crowley from 1944-1947 on magic, alchemy, and Tantra, eventually leading to Crowley's humbling realization that Curwen knew more than he about Indian occultism.
    - The Sri Vidya cultus—worship of Lakshmi Tripura Sundari through the Sri Chakra mandala—operates through four ascending forms of practice from purely mental worship to the Vamachara rites conducted in cremation grounds, which Grant identified as the closest Asian parallel to the dark mysteries of the OTO.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-020"></span>**The concept of kalas—the vaginal secretions of a female practitioner on each day of the lunar cycle, each with distinct occult properties—represents the central missing link that connects Tantra, Kabbalah, alchemy, and the OTO's sexual mysteries into a unified technology for opening the gates between worlds.**
    - The best blood is of the moon, monthly (AL III:24)—this Thelemic verse, combined with the Tantric concept of sixteen kalas associated with sixteen lunar digits, the sixteen vowels of Sanskrit, and the Goddess Sixteen (Shodashi), forms the core of Grant's ritual system.
    - Tantric scholar David Gordon White's work on the royal consumption origin myth shows the Moon purchasing its vital fluid (soma) with a red cow whose name rohini is also the name of the star Aldebaran—a myth connecting Jewish red heifer purification rites, Merkavah mysticism, and Tantric alchemy in ways that validate Grant's cross-cultural synthesis.

## The Necronomicon Gnosis

The Necronomicon Gnosis is the system by which Lovecraft's fictional mythology maps onto Crowley's Thelema and Grant's Typhonian Tradition: Cthulhu as the High Priest of the Great Old Ones is simultaneously a Sumerian psychopomp, a Jungian Shadow archetype, and the sleeping Kundalini serpent—and the sunken city of R'lyeh is both the collective unconscious and the Nightside of the Tree of Life.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-021"></span>**Grant's claim that Lovecraft's stories represent 'genuine magical manifestations'—transmissions received by a sensitive artist from extra-dimensional forces rather than pure invention—is the pivotal interpretive move that allows the Cthulhu Mythos to function as scripture for the Typhonian Tradition.**
    - "Certain fugitive elements appear occasionally in the works of poets, painters, mystics, and occultists which may be regarded as genuine magical manifestations in that they demonstrate the power and ability of the artist to evoke elements of an extradimensional and alien universe." —Kenneth Grant
    - In 'The Call of Cthulhu,' artists worldwide experience identical disturbing visions from February 28 to April 2, 1925—the same period bookended by a real earthquake and the Surrealist Memorandum of Revolution, suggesting a real event was being received by sensitive human antennae.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-022"></span>**The Schlangekraft Necronomicon translates Cthulhu as Kutulu—'Man of Kutu' or 'Man of the Underworld'—connecting the fictional deity to the ancient Sumerian city of Cutha, sacred to Nergal as the entrance to the Underworld and the hometown of the Prophet Muhammad's tribe, forging an unexpected link between Sumerian religion, Islam, and the Lovecraftian mythos.**
    - The Tibetan word kusulu—referring to a shaman who was once dead and has returned from death—provides another cognate for Cthulhu, consistent with Lovecraft's description of the entity as 'dead but dreaming' and with shamanistic death-and-rebirth initiatory traditions worldwide.
    - The pronounciation of 'cthonic' in the Oxford English Dictionary is 'ka-tonic,' which may have given Lovecraft the pun necessary for his fictional Miskatonic University where the Necronomicon is kept.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-023"></span>**When the 'stars are right' in Lovecraft's mythos refers to a real astronomical tradition centered on the Great Bear constellation, which circumpolar asterism is identified in Egyptian religion with Set, in the Necronomicon with the timing of Gate-openings, and in worldwide mystery cults from Mithraism to Daoist ceremonial magic as the ultimate stellar clock.**
    - The Necronomicon specifies that Gates may be opened 'when the Great Bear hangs from its tail in the sky'—at midnight on April 30, when the Big Dipper is positioned with its handle pointing straight down toward the Pole Star—a date corresponding to the cross-quarter day Walpurgisnacht, laden with historical resonance.
    - April 30 correlates with Christopher Columbus receiving his commission (1492), George Washington's inauguration (1789), Adolf Hitler's alleged suicide (1945), and the fall of Saigon (1975)—a pattern Grant interprets as evidence of the Gate's influence on world history.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-024"></span>**Cthulhu, as Grant interprets him through the lens of depth psychology and evolutionary biology, represents the reptilian brain—the 500-million-year-old survival mechanism at the top of the human spinal cord—which is simultaneously the seat of the Jungian Shadow, the domain of Set, and the sleeping Kundalini serpent that must be consciously mastered before genuine magical practice is possible.**
    - The physical reptilian brain is at least 500 million years old, predating the arrival of mammals and corresponding exactly to the Lovecraftian time-span of the Great Old Ones who came to earth before humans walked it—our reptilian brains may be their literal legacy in our DNA.
    - "Until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate." —C.G. Jung
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-025"></span>**The Necronomicon itself functions as a manual of dream control—a satanic semiotics that speaks directly to the unconscious in Cthulhu's own language—providing both the means of interpreting the messages received from R'lyeh (the collective unconscious) and the means of communicating back.**
    - Lovecraft's R'lyeh, analyzed through depth psychology, is the unconscious itself—a sunken, buried civilization of suppressed psychic material where Cthulhu the Shadow waits to be discovered—and it communicates with its human followers through the dream stream in exactly the way Freud identified dreams as 'the royal road to the unconscious.'
    - The Necronomicon's requirement of lunar periods of sexual abstinence ('thou mayest worship at the Temple of Ishtar, provided thou lose not thine Essence') parallels Tantric requirements for seminal retention, connecting the Sumerian text to the Indian tradition through a shared understanding of subtle biological energies.

## Sons of God, Daughters of Men… and Wilbur Whateley

The most controversial aspect of Grant's Typhonian Tradition—the deliberate creation of hybrid or 'magical' offspring through psycho-sexual ritual—is examined through parallel readings of Valentinian Gnostic sacred marriage theology, Tantric Kaula circle practice, and Lovecraft's 'The Dunwich Horror,' all of which describe a process of intentional conception that uses sexuality as a gate for the incarnation of non-human intelligences.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-026"></span>**Clement of Alexandria's account of the Valentinian Gnostics reveals that early Christians practiced 'consciousness-raising' during sexual intercourse to produce divine offspring, explicitly using the Greek word thelema (will) as the distinguishing quality of legitimate versus defective reproduction—making Crowley's Thelema the direct inheritor of this Gnostic sexual theology.**
    - Clement of Alexandria writes that Christians should 'do nothing from lust. Our will is to be directed towards that which is necessary. For we are children not of lust but of will (thelematos)'—thus children of Thelema.
    - The Valentinian sacred marriage (hierogamos) involved 'some sort of consciousness-raising during sexual relations to insure that the children would resemble God'—physical intercourse was a matter of will or intention, not lust, in precise accord with the Thelemic formula 'love under will.'
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-027"></span>**Lovecraft's 'The Dunwich Horror' encodes a precise account of Tantric ritual conception—old Wizard Whateley performing rites that charged Lavinia's kalas at the correct stellar moment to enable impregnation by the alien entity Yog-Sothoth, producing Wilbur Whateley and his monstrous unnamed twin as magical offspring.**
    - Wilbur Whateley was born on February 2, 1913—Candlemas or Imbolc, meaning 'in the belly' in Old Irish—a date that is one of the cross-quarter days between solstices and equinoxes, places 'between' that attract both Lovecraft's and Grant's attention as liminal power-moments.
    - "Yog-Sothoth knows the gate. Yog-Sothoth is the gate. Yog-Sothoth is the key and guardian of the gate. Past, present, future, all are one in Yog-Sothoth." —H.P. Lovecraft
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-028"></span>**The Kaula Circle ritual of maithuna—the Tantric sexual embrace performed within a sacred circle led by a guru—requires both participants to have identified with their respective deities (Shiva and Shakti) before the embrace begins, so that the union is not between humans but between gods, producing 'angelic offspring' that represent magical attainments rather than biological children.**
    - The coven of thirteen represents the true chakra or Kaula Circle; within it the pancatattva or ritual of the Five M's—culminating in maithuna (the sexual embrace)—is performed after systematic breaking of Vedic taboos one by one, demonstrating that nothing is inherently unclean.
    - "Grant's goal for the Kaula-style ritual is explicitly Lovecraftian: 'In due course is born a being that is adapted perfectly to existence on a chosen star.'" —Kenneth Grant
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-029"></span>**The central practical technique of Grant's Typhonian system—the collection of magically-charged vaginal kalas from a priestess brought to the edge of orgasm but not permitted to climax—requires the female practitioner to be an initiate capable of consciously raising Kundalini, and the priest to perform mudras over her body without direct contact, so that the released secretions become carriers of stellar ojas rather than merely biological fluids.**
    - Neither priest nor priestess should achieve orgasm prematurely because it 'earths' or 'grounds' the raised energy before it has been fully utilized—the point of the ritual is not sexual satisfaction but the deliberate accumulation of psycho-biological force at a specific moment and its direction toward a magical goal.
    - The seminal fluid of the male operator is equally important: the Shiva Samhita states that the Tantrika should never lose his semen under any circumstances, as 'it takes at least a fortnight to replace the semen expended in an act of sexual intercourse'—the same fourteen-day period as the lunar fortnight from New Moon to Full Moon.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-030"></span>**Lovecraft and Crowley represent opposite poles of the same axis—one celibate and horrified by sexuality as a gateway to alien invasion, the other polymorphously active and enthusiastic about sexuality as a means of communion with higher forces—yet both were receiving identical information about the same supramundane reality.**
    - Lovecraft instinctively understood that extreme or non-traditional forms of sexuality were somehow linked to magic, occult power, and contact with supramundane entities—the same insight that underlies Crowley's magical revelations—but where Crowley embraced the connection, Lovecraft was repelled by it.
    - The tension between their two diametrically-opposed points of view is where the most valuable information resides: Grant's genius was in recognizing that both men were dreaming the same dream.

## The Mauve Zone

The Mauve Zone—Grant's name for the intermediary realm between dream-consciousness and dreamless sleep, accessible through psycho-sexual Tantric practice, the Sri Chakra's occult topology, and the Kabbalistic Tunnels of Set—was breached catastrophically by Jack Parsons's Babalon Working of 1946, after which the UFO phenomenon began, marking a genuine incursion of extraterrestrial intelligences into the earth's psychic environment.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-031"></span>**The Sri Chakra mandala of the Sri Vidya tradition is, for Grant, not merely a diagram of the Goddess's body but a map of the Mauve Zone whose 28 marmas (triple-line intersection points) and 24 sandhis (double-line intersection points)—summing to 52, the number of letters in the Sanskrit alphabet—correspond to power-zones on the human body and to entry-points into other dimensions.**
    - The Anuttara Amnaya—the 'Supreme Tantra' among the six subsidiary vidyas of the Sri Vidya tradition—is ruled by Shiva in his dark form as Hatakeswara, Lord of one of the seven infernal hells, from whose mouth the Lords of Hell pour forth, making the Highest Tradition simultaneously the domain of the Dark Lord.
    - "The Sri Chakra conceals in its symbolism more than the possibility of alien contact, it conceals the keys to doors Outside." —Kenneth Grant
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-032"></span>**Crowley's Liber 231—received in the same 1907 white heat of inspiration that produced the Tutulu texts—maps 22 'qlippothic' or demonic anti-paths on the reverse of the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, which Grant and his Nu-Isis Lodge identified as the Tunnels of Set: the pathways through the Mauve Zone toward alien dimensions.**
    - Daath—the 'anti-sefirah' shown with a dotted circle on diagrams of the Tree of Life, associated by Jacob Frank and subsequent Kabbalists with transgressive spiritual power—is the Outer Gateway to the Mauve Zone in Grant's system.
    - Grant's Nu-Isis Lodge worked extensively with Liber 231 and the qlippot in the 1950s and 1960s, attempting to map the Tunnels of Set and equating them with kalas—the kalas may be the fuel enabling travel through the Tunnels or the keys to opening their gates.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-033"></span>**Jack Parsons's Babalon Working of January-March 1946—OTO sex-magic rituals conducted with L. Ron Hubbard intended to incarnate the Scarlet Woman and create a Thelemic messiah—'blew a hole in the space-time continuum' whose effects manifested as the UFO phenomenon beginning in June 1947, the discovery of the Dead Sea Scrolls, the founding of the CIA, and the death of Crowley.**
    - At the completion of the first phase of the Babalon Working, a red-headed woman—Marjorie Cameron—appeared at Parsons's home; Parsons took this as proof the ritual had succeeded, since the Scarlet Woman is traditionally red-haired.
    - Grant claims the atomic explosions of 1945 disturbed the delicate psychic membrane covering our planet to the extent that other forces began massing at the rent in the veil and by 1947 began pouring through—connecting Parsons's magical operations to the UFO phenomenon in a scenario 'straight out of H.P. Lovecraft.'
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-034"></span>**The bizarre case of German occultist Carl Tanzler (Count von Cosel) in Key West—who spent nearly ten years in necromantic cohabitation with the embalmed corpse of the tuberculosis patient Maria Elena de Hoyos—provides a real-world parallel to Grant's account of an Assam-based Tantric Entity 'cohabiting' with a metallic image of Nu-Isis, suggesting that the most transgressive Typhonian operations have actual historical precedents.**
    - Tanzler, who had studied with Sri Lankan Buddhist monks connected to Crowley's mentor Alan Bennett and published an article in the Rosicrucian Digest, built a special cabinet for the corpse in his home and lived with it until discovered by the dead woman's family in October 1940—nearly ten years after her death in 1931.
    - H.P. Lovecraft visited Florida precisely during the period Tanzler was engaged in this operation (1931 onward), staying near the young Robert Barlow in DeLand; Lovecraft's story 'Herbert West—Re-Animator' predates the events but covers identical themes, raising the question of whether Lovecraft saw future events.

## The Dark Lord

Set—the Dark Lord—is presented as the key to understanding the Typhonian Tradition because he represents everything humanity has suppressed: alien sexuality, the reptilian brain, the stellar rather than solar current, and the force of retroversion that reverses normal psychic processes to open the Gate; his throne in the collective unconscious is the ultimate destination of all genuine magical practice.

* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-035"></span>**The Typhonian Order's stated 'occult policy'—communion with discarnate and extraterrestrial Intelligences through sexual magic—is the logical conclusion of everything Grant has developed, marking a decisive break from solar-based Thelema toward a stellar-based tradition that uses the human organism as a conduit for contact with forces originating in deep space.**
    - The central concern of Magick is communion with discarnate or extraterrestrial Intelligences.
    - Grant's magic is stellar-based while mainstream Thelema is solar-based; he emphasizes Set over Horus and accepts input from Parsons, Frater Achad, and Lovecraft as legitimate expansions of the Thelemic current, seeking a scope for Thelema that extends beyond the visible solar system into other dimensions.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-036"></span>**Set is polymorphously perverse, born unnaturally, identified with alien lands and the circumpolar stars, and represents the force of retroversion—reversing the normal flow of energy in the universe—making him the patron deity not only of transgressive sexuality but of the entire magical project of using the body against its own survival instincts to open awareness to other realities.**
    - Set is the avatar of all such Devil's Advocates—the Ghost in the Machine, the Father of Lies, the King of Demons—but more importantly, the Master of Deception and Dissimulation who has his throne in 'the deepest, darkest recesses of the human unconscious,' which is the gateway to all the other zones of reality that comprise the magician's neighborhood.
    - Set is the reptilian brain—that ancient legacy from the Great Old Ones, from 500 million years ago, all fight or flight—and his asterism, the Thigh of Set (the Big Dipper), made of magnetic iron (Lovecraft's 'Tutulu metal'), performed the Opening of the Mouth ceremony to reanimate the mummified dead.
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-037"></span>**Grant's Typhonian Tradition requires the narratives of H.P. Lovecraft because they provide the only available mythological vocabulary adequate to describe the entities encountered in the Tunnels of Set—beings older than Egyptian religion, older than Babylon, and immune to the Seal of Solomon, requiring an entirely new grimoire.**
    - The K'rla Cell—one of Grant's power-zone groups—identified Betelgeuse as the home of the Elder Gods and a black hole in the constellation Sagittarius as the home of the Great Old Ones, and conducted psychosexual operations using 'psychosexual dynamics concealed in the Necronomicon' to make contact with Azathoth and Yog-Sothoth.
    - "Grant argues for a new narrative to replace the Judaeo-Christian one: 'Yog-Sothoth is the zenith of which Tutulu is the nadir... The full complex formulates the Great Cross which typifies the crossing over from matter to spirit. This is represented on the Tree of Life by Daäth, and by Death in the Voodoo imagery of Baron Samedhi.'" —Kenneth Grant
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-038"></span>**Jack Parsons's Book of Antichrist (1948) describes an initiatory experience—taken to cyclopean ruins, shown four previous lives, told 'things were done to me of which I may not write'—that reads as a genuine alien abduction account and prefigures his death in an explosion four years later, demonstrating that the Lovecraftian scenario of the magician-scientist summoning discarnate entities at tremendous personal cost is historically real.**
    - Parsons writes in The Book of Antichrist: 'And thereafter I was taken within and saluted the Prince of that place, and thereafter things were done to me of which I may not write ... It is not certain that you will survive...'—he then assumes the identity of the Antichrist and dies in an explosion less than four years later at age 38.
    - "The force is completely blind, depending upon the men and women in whom it manifests and who guide it. Obviously, its guidance now tends towards catastrophy." —Jack Parsons
* <span id="the-dark-lord-hp-lovecraft-kenneth-grant-and-the-t-039"></span>**The Typhonian Tradition, unlike solar-based religion, posits no end-point or apocalypse—its stellar orientation reflects the infinite cycling of yugas and kalpas—and its ongoing magical project is the deliberate, controlled opening and monitoring of the Gate between human consciousness and the realm of the Great Old Ones, which Lovecraft's stories simultaneously warn against and describe.**
    - "The cult of Cthulhu in 'The Call of Cthulhu' will never die till the stars came right again: 'Then the liberated Old Ones would teach them new ways to shout and kill and revel and enjoy themselves, and all the earth would flame with a holocaust of ecstasy and freedom.'" —H.P. Lovecraft
    - Grant does not want us to worship Cthulhu—but we must be aware of the forces these names represent because they are real forces; the Necronomicon Gnosis is perfectly consistent with both the Egyptian and Thelemic currents, and the psycho-sexual techniques of Tantra can be employed in rituals of the Necronomicon, breathing new life into each.
