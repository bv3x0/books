## Book I

### The arousing of thought

Gurdjieff introduces himself and his unconventional authorial method, warning readers that his writings are intentionally structured to bypass 'fictitious consciousness' and reach the subconscious, using the principle 'if you go on a spree, then go the whole hog, including the postage' as the governing creative law of the entire work.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-001"></span>**Gurdjieff refuses to write in 'bon ton literary language,' arguing that conventional literary style operates through automatized associations that bypass genuine understanding and lull readers rather than awakening them.**
    - The language of grammarians is composed by people who have no notion of the 'law of associations' governing all human mentation, which the ancient Korkolans identified as the fundamental process of thought.
    - He compares Russian and English to 'solianka,' a dish into which anything goes, suitable only for swapping anecdotes or discussing frozen meat, not for philosophical nuance.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-002"></span>**Human beings possess two independent consciousnesses — a 'fictitious' waking consciousness formed from mechanical impressions, and a real 'subconscious' formed from hereditary materialized results and intentional confrontation — and Gurdjieff's writings are deliberately structured to reach both.**
    - Most new impressions, including almost all words, blend only with the fictitious consciousness and produce no lasting transformation in the presence of the being.
    - The subconscious, formed partly from heredity and partly from intentionally evoked associative confrontations, should predominate in the common presence of a human being but rarely does.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-003"></span>**Three specific psychic data crystallized during Gurdjieff's youth together determine his authorial personality: his grandmother's injunction to never do as others do, a wisdom tooth knocked out in a fight that engendered incessant curiosity about causes, and a Russian merchant's drunken formulation of 'go the whole hog, including the postage.'**
    - "His grandmother's dying words — 'Either do nothing — just go to school — or do something nobody else does' — became the chief directing lever of his entire being." —Gurdjieff's grandmother
    - The knocked-out seven-pronged wisdom tooth caused a chronic oozing that, as later explained by a meteorologist friend, aroused an instinctive tendency to seek the causes of every suspicious actual fact.
    - The principle 'if you go on a spree, then go the whole hog, including the postage,' first uttered by an anonymous Russian merchant, was identified by Gurdjieff via Allan Kardec as having become one of the chief life-principles on all planets of the Universe.
* <span class="pull-quote">I shall expound my thoughts intentionally in such a sequence and with such logical confrontation that the essence of certain real ideas may pass automatically from this 'waking consciousness,' which most people in their ignorance mistake for the real consciousness, but which I affirm and experimentally prove is the fictitious one, into what you call the 'subconscious.'</span>
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-004"></span>**The story of the Transcaucasian Kurd who kept eating red pepper pods because he paid for them illustrates the human inherency Gurdjieff deliberately exploits: people compelled to consume to the end what they have paid for, motivating him to print the first chapter so it can be read without cutting the book's pages.**
    - "The Kurd continued eating despite burning pain, saying 'Even if my soul departs from my body, I will go on eating,' because he had paid his last six coppers." —Transcaucasian Kurd
    - Gurdjieff uses this principle as one of the foundational structural devices of his new literary form, creating discomfort that nonetheless compels continued reading.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-005"></span>**The story of Karapet of Tiflis, who cursed all directions before releasing the station steam whistle each morning, illustrates that preemptively directing negative intent neutralizes the maleficent vibrations others involuntarily send toward those who disturb them — a principle Gurdjieff claims governs his own relationship with readers he is about to disturb.**
    - Karapet reasoned that everyone woken by the whistle blasts curses him, sending vibrations of malice toward his person, and that pre-cursing them prevents these vibrations from taking effect.
    - This story serves Gurdjieff as a self-exculpation for violating Mullah Nasr Eddin's commandment 'Never poke your stick into a hornets' nest.'
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-006"></span>**Gurdjieff announces that his chief literary hero will be Beelzebub himself, chosen partly by cunning: paying Beelzebub the attention of making him a protagonist will enlist his help, since even beings 'of a different clay' respond to vanity — and the scale of the work will be the entire Universe rather than the paltry Earth.**
    - Contemporary writers take Earth-scale themes; because of his grandmother's injunction and the 'whole hog' principle, Gurdjieff cannot confine himself to mere terrestrial subjects.
    - The heroes of these writings will be of such a kind that readers must sense them as real somebodies — not the Toms, Dicks, and Harrys who cultivate lasciviousness, mawkishness, and envy.

### Prologue Why Beelzebub was in our solar system

The narrative frame is established: Beelzebub, traveling on the trans-space ship Karnak with his grandson Hassein and servant Ahoon, is a once-exiled being who intervened in cosmic governance in his youth and was banished to the remote solar system Ors, later pardoned after the mission of Ashiata Shiemash.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-007"></span>**Beelzebub was exiled from the Sun Absolute to the solar system Ors because, as a young being with unformed Reason, he found something 'illogical' in the government of the world and, rallying comrades, nearly brought the central kingdom of the Megalocosmos to revolution.**
    - His intervention was driven by youthful and impetuous mentation with unequally flowing associations — natural for beings not yet fully responsible.
    - His Endlessness, despite all-lovingness and all-forgivingness, was constrained to banish Beelzebub and his comrades, assigning them the planet Mars with the privilege of existing on other planets of that solar system.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-008"></span>**On Mars, Beelzebub established an observatory that became famous throughout the Universe for its ability to observe remote cosmic concentrations and the conditions of existence on neighboring planets, turning exile into productive cosmic scholarship.**
    - The exiled colony on Mars accommodated itself gradually, with many beings finding occupations on Mars or neighboring planets that had been neglected due to their remoteness from the Center.
    - Beelzebub's observatory would later house a teskooano capable of increasing visibility of remote cosmic concentrations by 7,000,285 times.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-009"></span>**Beelzebub's pardon was granted at the petition of Ashiata Shiemash, a Messenger sent from the Sun Absolute to the planet Earth, whose mission Beelzebub had assisted — demonstrating that conscious service to higher purposes can redeem even serious cosmic transgressions.**
    - After his pardon, Beelzebub's influence and authority had greatly increased rather than declined, since all recognized that long years in unaccustomed conditions had inevitably broadened and deepened his knowledge.

### The cause of a delay in the falling of the Karnak

The Karnak's course is blocked by the comet Sakoor's trail of noxious zilnotrago, and Beelzebub decides to stop and wait rather than detour, turning the forced delay into an opportunity for conversation and storytelling — establishing the narrative occasion for all that follows.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-010"></span>**When faced with an obstacle created by forces immeasurably greater than one's own — here, the comet Sakoor's zilnotrago trail — the only wise response is submission rather than resistance, choosing whichever option causes least damage to one's own resources.**
    - "Beelzebub cites Mullah Nasr Eddin: 'You can't jump over your knees, and it's absurd to try to kiss your own elbow!' as the guiding wisdom for such situations." —Beelzebub
    - He chooses waiting over detour specifically to spare the ship useless mechanical damage, prioritizing long-term integrity over marginal time savings.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-011"></span>**Hassein's joy at the delay — because it means more of his grandfather's tales — reveals his understanding that his grandfather's stories are the primary medium for crystallizing new information in his presence, while Ahoon's accusation of 'egoism' prompts Hassein to articulate the distinction between selfish pleasure and grateful reception of wisdom.**
    - Hassein argues that his grandfather had already decided to stop after weighing all circumstances with wise Reason, so the delay serves everyone; his joy is not egoism but gratitude for a providential coincidence.
    - Hassein explicitly frames the tales as occasions where 'new and interesting information is crystallized in our presences' — using Gurdjieff's technical vocabulary of psychic crystallization for learning.

### The law of falling

The captain explains how Saint Venoma discovered the 'law of falling' — that everything in the Universe falls toward the nearest stability (a sun or planet as center of gravity) — and engineered the first ships to exploit this law for locomotion, though these ships proved difficult to maneuver near large cosmic concentrations.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-012"></span>**Saint Venoma, while residing on the Holy Planet Purgatory, discovered the 'law of falling,' which states that everything existing falls toward the nearest stability — defined as the center of gravity of the nearest sun or planet — and that this cosmic property could be utilized for intersystem locomotion.**
    - The centers of all suns and planets are precisely the points of stability toward which forces from all directions of a given region inexorably converge, making them the 'bottom' for their surrounding space.
    - Saint Venoma's ships used rays of elekilpomagtistzen passed through special glass to destroy atmospheric resistance in their path, creating an absolute vacuum through which the ship could be moved by even an infant's push.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-013"></span>**Despite being ideal in atmosphereless space, Saint Venoma's ships became extremely difficult to navigate near suns and planets because the law of falling would immediately pull the ship off course, requiring constant highly skilled maneuvering and reducing speed hundreds of times — demonstrating that every cosmic law exploited for convenience also imposes corresponding difficulties.**
    - The beings entrusted to direct these ships had to be prepared by beings of very high Reason due to the constant danger of falling off course near large concentrations.
    - Navigation was particularly dangerous in regions with large aggregations of comets.

### The system of Archangel Hariton

The captain describes Archangel Hariton's invention — a cylinder-barrel whose specially arranged inner materials cause any atmospheric substance entering it to expand and escape through a hinged lid, driving levers and fans — which solved Saint Venoma's maneuverability problems by using atmospheric resistance itself as the motive force.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-014"></span>**Archangel Hariton's cylinder exploits the property of its inner wall materials — arranged in a specific order and isolated by amber — to cause any atmospheric substance entering it to expand beyond the interior's capacity, driving a hinged lid that operates levers and fans; the denser the surrounding substance, the faster the mechanism works.**
    - In atmosphereless space, the ship still operates by the law of falling toward the nearest stability, requiring no work from the cylinder; the cylinder activates only when atmospheric resistance is present.
    - Contemporary ships using this system can be given an impetus in any direction and fall wherever intended without the complicated maneuvers required by Saint Venoma's system.

### Perpetual motion

Beelzebub recognizes Archangel Hariton's cylinder as the 'perpetual motion' that terrestrial beings obsessively and fruitlessly sought for generations, noting the irony that what they imagined as a simple miracle had already been realized on a cosmic scale, while Earth's 'cranks' destroyed themselves pursuing it without the necessary understanding.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-015"></span>**The mania for perpetual motion on Earth illustrates how three-brained terrestrial beings, lacking genuine knowledge, collectively pursue a real cosmic principle while remaining incapable of actualizing it — the 'rage' consuming thousands and sending many to lunatic asylums was only ended when an old authority proved by private calculations that it was impossible.**
    - Beelzebub visited a town where a large collection of models and descriptions of proposed perpetual motion mechanisms had been gathered, finding that the idea of using 'force of weight' — a falling weight to lift itself again — predominated.
    - Hariton's cylinder is the genuine realization: as long as the Universe contains atmospheres around planets, the cylinder will work perpetually without requiring outside materials.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-016"></span>**The cylinder's principal parts are made of amber with platinum hoops and inner surfaces of anthracite, copper, ivory, and a special mastic — designed for extreme longevity — while outer levers and cogwheels require periodic renewal, illustrating the cosmic distinction between enduring structural principles and their transient mechanical expressions.**
    - The special mastic is unaffected by paischakir, tainolair, saliakooriap, or even the radiations of cosmic concentrations.

### Becoming aware of genuine being-duty

Hassein's melancholy reflection on the labor of past beings who created present conveniences — labor performed for entirely unknown future beneficiaries — leads Beelzebub to counsel patience: the young are not yet obliged to pay for their existence, but must daily practice bringing consciousness into contact with their unconscious parts to prepare for responsible life.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-017"></span>**Genuine being-duty — the obligation to pay for one's existence through conscious work — is not yet required of young beings, but daily practice of bringing waking consciousness into intentional contact with unconscious presence is the indispensable preparation for this duty in responsible age.**
    - Beelzebub advises that every day at sunrise Hassein should create a state of contact between his consciousness and the unconscious parts of his common presence, convincing those parts that if they hinder general functioning now, they will be unable to enjoy good in responsible age.
    - The recognition that all contemporary advantages were created by past beings who consciously or unconsciously labored for entirely unknown future others generates an authentic impulse Hassein calls 'remorse of conscience.'

### The impudent brat Hassein, Beelzebub's grandson, dares to call men "slugs"

Beelzebub reacts with theatrical alarm to Hassein's casual insult of humanity as 'slugs,' using this occasion to illustrate the peculiar psychology of terrestrial three-brained beings — specifically their elaborate ritual of anathema — as a characteristic example of the strangeness of their psyche.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-018"></span>**When insulted, contemporary terrestrial beings respond not by direct retaliation but by elaborate institutional ritual — convening solemn councils, electing presidents, conducting trials by majority vote, and ultimately issuing formal anathemas — a procedure Beelzebub presents as a characteristic expression of their bizarre collective psyche.**
    - The anathema would take the form of specially designated officials in churches, chapels, synagogues, and town halls formally wishing the offender such things as premature gray hair, food turning to coffin nails, and a wife's tongue growing three times its length.
    - The process of trial and anathema would proceed through multiple institutional levels — first a local solemn council, then referral to the Holy Synod — before arriving at the conclusion that the accused is unreachable.

### The first growl

The story of a Russian writer whose 'gospel' was officially anathematized by power-possessing beings demonstrates the paradox central to terrestrial psychology: public condemnation by authorities functions as the most effective advertisement, because beings there form convictions not through their own reflection but entirely through the opinions of others.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-019"></span>**A contemporary Russian writer who composed a new 'gospel' — motivated partly by cultural aspiration and partly by the favorable exchange rate of English pounds and dollars — found that official anathematization by power-possessing beings caused his reputation to explode, because the three-brained beings there take their convictions entirely from what Smith or Brown says rather than from their own verification.**
    - The power-possessing beings anathematized him because his gospel appeared at the moment when ordinary beings had finally awakened from torpor due to excessive taxation, and the authorities needed to suppress anything preventing a return to hibernation.
    - The result was that his name became known almost everywhere as that of an excellent writer, and anything he ever wrote was regarded as indisputable truth — even though most who discussed him had never read a single one of his books.

### Why in man's Reason fantasy may be perceived as reality

Beelzebub explains that terrestrial beings confuse fantasy for reality because they have ceased to fulfill 'being-partkdolgduty' — the active confrontation of new impressions with previously acquired data — and instead crystallize convictions solely from the opinions of others, a degeneracy caused not by kundabuffer but by the abnormal conditions of existence they themselves established.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-020"></span>**The root cause of terrestrial beings' inability to distinguish fantasy from reality is not the organ kundabuffer or its consequences but the abnormal conditions of external ordinary being-existence they gradually established themselves, which fostered 'self-calming' — their chief 'inner evil god' — as a habitual substitute for genuine active reflection.**
    - Being-partkdolgduty requires confronting each new impression with data already deposited in all three being-centers, producing subjective conviction through active mentation rather than passive absorption.
    - When Beelzebub personally met the celebrated writer in question, he found him extremely limited — 'unable to see further than his nose' — and a complete ignoramus regarding the real psyche of his planet's beings.

### Introducing a perspective that promises nothing very cheerful

Beelzebub outlines the fundamental abnormalities of terrestrial three-brained beings — including the unique cosmic phenomenon of 'war,' and properties such as egoism, vanity, suggestibility, and credulity — and announces he will recount his six personal descents to Earth in chronological order to give Hassein a clear understanding of their strange psyche.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-021"></span>**The most terrible abnormal property of terrestrial beings' common psyche is 'suggestibility,' which is unique to their planet and works in tandem with egoism, self-love, vanity, pride, conceit, and credulity to make them susceptible to collective psychoses such as war — the process of mutual destruction of existence found nowhere else in the Universe.**
    - All these abnormal properties are crystallized in every terrestrial being regardless of where they arise and exist, making them universal features of that planet's three-brained beings rather than regional or individual variations.
    - Great Nature was compelled by the deteriorating quality of terrestrial beings' radiations to gradually transform their common presence through various compromises and to increase their numbers across all lands in order to maintain the vibrations required for the welfare of the former planetary fragments.

### Beelzebub's first descent to the planet Earth

Beelzebub's first descent was occasioned by a young kinsman who made an ill-advised wager with King Appolis of Samlios, pledging to fund the treasury if the king governed only by the kinsman's indicated methods — which proved disastrous — and was resolved by replacing all government officials with beings of Beelzebub's tribe to absorb the rage of the inevitable revolution.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-022"></span>**The wager between the naive young kinsman and King Appolis reveals how the consequence of kundabuffer — the crystallized habit of fulfilling obligations only under threat — makes benevolent governance impossible: when the king adopted the kinsman's humane methods, subjects not only stopped paying taxes but began pilfering what had already been deposited.**
    - King Appolis, called 'Arch-Cunning' behind his back, used varied and reasonable intimidation methods to extract what was needed from subjects in whom kundabuffer's consequences had been thoroughly crystallized.
    - The kinsman had signed the wager agreement with his blood — and for beings of Beelzebub's tribe, a voluntarily assumed obligation signed in blood is absolutely binding regardless of consequences.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-023"></span>**The solution — replacing all government officials with beings of Beelzebub's tribe and conducting propaganda so the people's revolutionary fury fell on the officials rather than the king — succeeded perfectly, illustrating that terrestrial revolutionaries invariably destroy precisely those beings who have progressed furthest in freeing themselves from kundabuffer's consequences.**
    - "King Appolis himself acknowledged responsibility, saying his long experience with terrestrial conditions should have allowed him to foresee what the naive but highly Reasoning kinsman could not." —King Appolis
    - The ordinary beings who fell into revolutionary psychosis 'grieved' for their king and pitied him, believing he had been surrounded by disloyal subordinates — exactly the outcome that had been engineered.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-024"></span>**Atlantis at this period was the principal continent and greatest community on Earth, and the young kinsman had arrived there 350 Martian years earlier — establishing that Beelzebub's observations of Earth span an immense period predating the continent's eventual submergence.**
    - The ship Occasion, which transported Beelzebub's tribe to the solar system, had been placed under his supreme command from Above and served as the vessel for interplanetary communication throughout this period.

### The relativity of the concept of Time

Beelzebub explains that Time has no objective source of arising and can only be evaluated by comparison — establishing objective and subjective scales of temporal experience — and demonstrates through calculation that although Hassein is twelve years old by Karatas reckoning, this is equivalent to 4,668 Earth-years, before connecting the relativity of time to the catastrophic shortening of terrestrial beings' lifespans.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-025"></span>**Objective Science defines Time as having no source of arising and no existence in itself — it is only the totality of results issuing from all cosmic phenomena in a given place — making it uniquely subjective among cosmic phenomena, defined only by the standard unit of 'sacred egokoolnatsnarnian sensation' experienced by Holy Cosmic Individuals on the Sun Absolute.**
    - Time cannot be sensed by any gradation of instinct, perceived by any outer or inner being-function, or understood by Reason alone — it can only be evaluated by comparing different cosmic phenomena occurring under the same conditions in the same place.
    - The Heropass — another name for Time — 'issuing from nothing but always blending with everything while remaining self-sufficiently independent' is unique in the whole of the Universe, comparable only to 'divine Love' in flowing independently and blending proportionately with all arisings everywhere.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-026"></span>**The drop of water example demonstrates that subjective time-flow is consistent across all cosmic scales: the infinitesimal beings ('microcosmoses') in a single drop of water experience the complete sequence of birth, growth, sex, illness, suffering, and death in what appears from outside to be only minutes or seconds — the same proportional temporal experience as beings at every other cosmic scale.**
    - Even 'hypochondriacs' among such microcosmoses might experience time as dragging phenomenally tediously — exactly as hypochondriacs do among terrestrial beings — despite their entire existence lasting only a few terrestrial seconds.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-027"></span>**Because a single Karatas year equals 389 Earth krentonalnian revolutions, Hassein's twelve Karatas years correspond to 4,668 Earth-years — illustrating both the vast temporal scales of cosmic existence and the profound relativity of what terrestrial beings consider meaningful units of time.**
    - Normal three-brained beings sense the sacred egokoolnatsnarnian action 49 times more slowly than Sacred Individuals on the Sun Absolute, making the time-flow for Karatas beings 49 times faster than on the Sun Absolute.
    - These calculations were provided partly by the Arch-Engineer Archangel Algamatant during the first misfortune to Earth, and partly by the captain of the trans-space ship Omnipresent.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-028"></span>**The shortening of terrestrial lifespan from the fulasnitamnian principle (existing until the kesdjan body is fully coated and perfected in Reason) to the itoklanotz principle (existing as one- or two-brained beings do, for purely local cosmic purposes) was forced upon Great Nature by terrestrial beings' abnormal existence and their destruction of other planetary beings — reducing the duration of their existence to what Beelzebub calls 'almost nothing' in the objective sense.**
    - The fulasnitamnian principle requires existence until the astral or kesdjan body is completely perfected in Reason — something contemporary terrestrial beings no longer achieve because of their abnormal conditions.
    - Great Nature herself was compelled to bring terrestrial three-brained beings' presence into conformity with the itoklanotz principle because their emanations no longer served common-cosmic purposes and they were destroying the other beings needed for this.

### The arch-absurd: According to the assertion of Beelzebub, our sun neither lights nor heats

Beelzebub introduces the 'arch-absurd' — that terrestrial beings are categorically convinced light and heat come directly from the sun, when in reality the sun's surface is covered in ice — as an entry point for explaining the Omnipresent Okidanokh and the cosmic processes that actually produce atmospheric phenomena such as daylight, darkness, heat, and cold.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-029"></span>**The sun's surface, like all ordinary suns in the Universe, is covered in ice rather than being a source of heat and light — atmospheric phenomena on planets arise from the Omnipresent Okidanokh and the trogoautoegocratic process, not from direct solar radiation, yet no terrestrial scientist has ever suspected this, with the single exception of a Chinese being named Choon Kil Tess.**
    - Not a single terrestrial scientist has distinguished between 'emanation' and 'radiation' — two entirely different cosmic processes that Mullah Nasr Eddin compared as alike as Shakespeare's beard and French armagnac.
    - Terrestrial beings with normal three-brained presence and education based on the commandments of the Uni-Being Himself would find the terrestrial conviction about solar heat and light a cause of 'irrepressible inner laughter.'
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-030"></span>**The common-cosmic trogoautoegocratic process — the 'exchange of substances' or 'reciprocal feeding' of everything existing — was established by the Endless Creator to prevent the merciless Heropass from having a maleficent effect on the Sun Absolute, and operates through the two fundamental laws: the sacred Heptaparaparshinokh and the sacred Triamazikamno.**
    - All phenomena in the Universe without exception exist and are maintained solely on the basis of this trogoautoegocratic process, which requires all cosmic concentrations with their atmospheres to participate in the exchange of substances.
    - Ethernokrilno is the primordial substance filling the whole Universe and serving as the basis for all arising and maintenance; Objective Science declares that everything in the Universe without exception is material.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-031"></span>**The Omnipresent Okidanokh — the only cosmic crystallization arising from all three holy sources of the sacred Theomertmalogos — participates unfailingly in the formation of all cosmic concentrations everywhere and is the principal cause of most cosmic phenomena, including atmospheric effects, through its two key particularities: 'djartklom' (dispersal into three primordial parts upon entering any cosmic unit) and the sacred Aieioiuoa (remorse, or separation into independent parts when touched by emanations of a sun).**
    - When Okidanokh enters a cosmic unit it immediately undergoes djartklom — dispersion into the Holy Affirming, Holy Denying, and Holy Reconciling forces — which exist independently within that unit for as long as it exists, only reblending when the unit is completely destroyed.
    - The sacred Aieioiuoa process causes Okidanokh, when directly touched by solar emanation, to temporarily separate into its three independent parts — explaining why solar influence produces distinctive atmospheric effects rather than simply transmitting light and heat.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-032"></span>**Three-brained beings have three independent brain-localizations corresponding to the three holy forces of Triamazikamno, and by consciously fulfilling being-partkdolgduty they can utilize the three forces liberated by djartklom to bring their presence to the 'sekronoolantsaknian' state — becoming Individuals with their own sacred law of Triamazikamno and the possibility of consciously coating the 'All-Holy' that actualizes objective Reason.**
    - In terrestrial beings, the third being-brain (corresponding to the Holy Reconciling force) has been fragmented by Great Nature into separate nerve nodes distributed throughout the planetary body, with the greatest concentration in the chest region they call the 'solar plexus.'
    - Because terrestrial beings have entirely ceased to fulfill being-partkdolgduty due to abnormal existence conditions, almost none of the Holy Affirming and Holy Reconciling forces are transubstantiated for their own presence — only the Holy Denying crystallization serves for coating their own being, leaving most with only a planetary body, destroyed forever.

### The arch-preposterous

Beelzebub begins recounting his visit to Gornahoor Harharkh on Saturn, whose invention the 'khrkhakharkhtsakha' demonstrated the properties of the Omnipresent Okidanokh experimentally — specifically what occurs when one of the three active parts is intentionally excluded from the process of striving to reblend, producing the 'non-law-conformable' phenomenon terrestrial beings call 'artificial light.'

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-163"></span>**Societies formed to abolish war consistently fail because beings with 'resurrected conscience' who found them are rapidly overwhelmed by other important power-possessors who join not from conscience but from social obligation, and who then redirect affairs toward personal egoistic aims.**
    - Beelzebub traces five such societies: in Tikliamish with the motto 'God is where man's blood is not shed'; in Mongolplanzura with 'Love one another and God will love you'; in Egypt with 'Only if you learn to create a flea can you dare to kill a man'; in Persia with 'All men are divine, but if a single one is killed by another, then all will be reduced to nothing'; and in Mosulopolis, 'The Earth Belongs to Our Common Creator and Is Equally Free for All His Creatures.'
    - The Mosulopolis society came closest to success because it contained no power-possessing beings and consisted only of old, disillusioned, honorable men with fewer properties of egoism and vanity—yet it too failed.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-164"></span>**Beings with Objective Reason—who could actually contribute to ending war—are structurally excluded from power-possessing societies because 'importance' requires either great wealth or fame, and those qualities are now only attained by beings in whom the sacred function of Conscience is entirely absent, while Conscience is always connected with Objective Reason.**
    - "The result is a closed loop: those with the Reason and Conscience to solve the problem cannot become 'important,' and those who are 'important' lack both the Reason and Conscience to solve it—what Mullah Nasr Eddin called 'pull at the tail and the mane gets stuck, pull at the mane and the tail gets stuck.'" —Mullah Nasr Eddin
    - The contemporary League of Nations will provide its members with plausible excuses for 'cocktail parties' far from their wives' watchful eyes, constituting its primary practical benefit.
* <span class="pull-quote">This particularity, with all the results ensuing from it, is the chief cause of all their abnormalities and of their so to say 'muddled logic.'</span>
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-165"></span>**Even if power-possessing beings could not abolish war immediately, they could achieve lasting benefit for future generations by abolishing two specific practices: the glorification of war participants as 'heroes' with decorations, and the pseudo-scientific doctrine that periodic reciprocal destruction is biologically necessary to prevent overpopulation.**
    - The practice of calling soldiers 'heroes' and rewarding them with honors automatically predisposes the growing generation's psyche toward the state habitual during war-processes; eliminating this would destroy 'automatic factors' shaping children's formation.
    - The doctrine that war prevents intolerable overpopulation is one of the 'idiotic ideas' transmitted as 'articles of faith' from generation to generation that destroys the instinctive sensing of cosmic truths proper even to one-centered and two-centered beings throughout the Universe.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-166"></span>**The psycho-organic need of all terrestrial beings 'to teach others sense' and 'put them on the right road'—itself arising from the property of seeing defects only in others and never in oneself—is the master key to managing relations with them, since pretending to want to learn from anyone produces instant goodwill and 'adoration.'**
    - Every being there without exception—old and young, men and women, even the 'misbegotten'—has this need; a being 'under somebody's thumb' to the point of humiliation will be more indignant than anyone if a king cannot control thousands of subjects, and will write manuals on governance.
    - The constant indignation at others' defects produces futile 'moral sufferings' that act oppressively on the psyche and make beings 'instruarnian' (nervous) even in manifestations unrelated to the original cause, rendering their existence 'arch-tragicomic.'
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-167"></span>**The 'criminal property' of reciprocal destruction has become so embedded in human flesh and blood after hundreds of centuries that it cannot be eradicated in a few decades; the only effective work is toward future generations, not contemporaries, and requires acknowledging that an inherency fixed for centuries cannot be decrystallized quickly.**
    - Since even beings of earlier epochs who had attained 'self-remembering' could achieve nothing effective against war, beings whose Reason has only reached the degree Mullah Nasr Eddin describes as 'Look! He already begins to distinguish mama from papa!' are wholly incapable of resolving it.
    - The first reason the League of Nations will fail is structural (no Objective Reason present), and the second is that this property has been crystallizing as organic function through hereditary transmission for so many generations that it functions identically to the consequences of the organ kundabuffer.

### Beelzebub's opinion, man's understanding of justice is in the objective sense an accursed mirage

The widely held concept of external Good and Evil, first introduced in a specific technical sense by the Atlantean sage Makary Kronbernkzion, was later catastrophically misunderstood by subsequent generations, causing humanity to externalize responsibility for their own actions and thereby block the possibility of genuine self-perfecting.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-168"></span>**The righteous souls on the Holy Planet Purgatory, learning through Beelzebub's etherograms to Tooilan about the psyche of Earth beings, identified the idea of external Good and Evil as the fundamental cause of all psychic abnormalities in terrestrial three-brained beings, and petitioned the Creator to send a Messenger to uproot it.**
    - Fifty candidates for return to the Sun Absolute undertook the investigation, and the entire holy planet debated the findings in all its zarooariakhs, ultimately adopting a near-unanimous resolution.
    - Their investigation revealed that the first being responsible for the idea was Makary Kronbernkzion, whose perfected highest being-body was already dwelling on the holy planet as a candidate for the Sun Absolute.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-169"></span>**Makary Kronbernkzion was a learned member of the Society of Akhldanns on Atlantis who, after a sincere recognition of his own nullity, began conscious labor and intentional suffering, and eventually engraved a boolmarshano titled 'The Affirming and Denying Influences on Man' using the terms Good and Evil in a precise technical sense to describe the two fundamental opposing cosmic forces.**
    - In his boolmarshano, 'Good' denoted the descending, passive, involutionary force flowing from the Prime Source, and 'Evil' denoted the ascending, active, evolutionary force striving to return — both necessary components of the universal Triamazikamno.
    - Beelzebub personally located a surviving copy of the boolmarshano — made from chirniano tusks — through an Aisorian priest in Urmia and by excavating a buried half near an undisclosed populated center in Asia.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-170"></span>**Makary Kronbernkzion was not personally guilty for the maleficent form the idea of Good and Evil later took; after his death, a priest named Armanatoorga built a full religious teaching on a distorted version of the idea, populating the world with invisible spirit-spreaders of good (angels) and evil (devils), which transformed a cosmic principle into a psychology of external blame.**
    - Armanatoorga taught that devils could be seen by sufficiently righteous men, and that their invisibility to ordinary people resulted from the devils' own 'suggestion' — thereby making non-perception itself a sign of spiritual inadequacy.
    - The real names of beings from Beelzebub's own tribe, who had existed among humans in ancient times and whose long lifespans had led humans to call them 'immortal,' were later assigned to these devil-beings in the religious mythology.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-171"></span>**The crystallization of the idea of external Good and Evil in human psyches became the chief mechanism of 'self-calming': by attributing the causes of all bad manifestations to external forces rather than to their own criminal essence-egoism, humans eliminated the inner friction necessary for genuine self-development.**
    - The data for external Good and Evil became a 'determining factor' in the crystallization of all subsequent human worldviews — from science, philosophy, and politics to religion, law, and ethics.
    - Beelzebub personally differs from the holy planet's conclusion, arguing the real culprit was not Makary's original usage but the subsequent degeneration of his technical terms into popular religious mythology.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-172"></span>**Our Common Father Endlessness, petitioned by the holy planet, commanded that Makary Kronbernkzion's highest being-body should remain on the Holy Planet Purgatory rather than pass to the Sun Absolute — holding open the possibility of his ultimate redemption if Earth's beings ever truly reform their existence.**
    - The Creator evidently hoped that Earth beings might yet realize their errors and begin to exist as proper three-brained beings, which would make the severe punishment of Makary's being-part unnecessary.
    - The fate of Makary Kronbernkzion's highest being-body now depends exclusively on whether the three-brained beings of Earth choose genuine self-perfecting.

### In the opinion of Beelzebub, man's extraction of electricity from Nature, and its destruction during its use, is one of the chief causes of the shortening of human life

The extraction and destruction of 'electricity' — which is in fact a blended form of the omnipresent sacred substance Okidanokh — depletes a cosmic substance essential to the atmosphere of every planet and to the higher being-development of all three-brained beings, and the abnormal existence of Earth's beings is already measurably impairing the self-perfecting of beings on other planets like Mars.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-173"></span>**The toof-nef-tef (king) of Mars, an extremely old being of twelve thousand years, asked Beelzebub to consult high Individuals about why the 'noorfooftafaf' (will-lessness) of Martian beings was increasing each year while their capacity for active mentation was proportionally decreasing.**
    - The toof-nef-tef had known Beelzebub since his youth when he was a plef-perf-noof (physician of body and spirit) — the professional path by which beings on almost all planets become their community's chief.
    - Despite years of intensive meditation on the problem, the toof-nef-tef could find no explanation and asked Beelzebub to seek the answer from Individuals of correspondingly higher Reason.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-174"></span>**Gornahoor Rakhoorkh, son of Beelzebub's friend Gornahoor Harharkh on Saturn, independently discovered through experimentation that the operation of electrical dynamos ('life-chakhans') reduced his own capacity for active mentation on the days they operated, leading him to dedicate his existence to studying the omnipresent substance Okidanokh.**
    - Gornahoor Rakhoorkh's investigations proved that Okidanokh is a strictly proportioned component of every planetary atmosphere, and when it is depleted in one location it must be replenished from elsewhere — including from other planets' atmospheres.
    - His research showed that the possibility for three-brained beings to perfect themselves and ultimately blend with the Prime Cause depends exclusively on this substance; its destruction is therefore spiritually catastrophic.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-175"></span>**Electricity as used and destroyed by Earth beings is in fact the result of the blending and reciprocal destruction of two parts of Okidanokh, and humanity's ever-increasing industrial use of electricity is depleting this sacred substance from Earth's atmosphere, forcing compensatory flows from neighboring planets including Mars.**
    - Earth beings have repeatedly throughout history accidentally discovered means of extracting parts of Okidanokh for naively egoistic purposes, but never previously on the current industrial scale.
    - The atmospheric depletion of Okidanokh on Mars — caused by flows compensating for Earth's destruction — directly explains the increasing will-lessness and mental deterioration of Martian beings that had puzzled the toof-nef-tef.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-176"></span>**Gornahoor Rakhoorkh's discoveries caused his producer Gornahoor Harharkh to completely decrystallize all his previous convictions, destroy his famous non-radiating lamp and all apparatus for investigating Okidanokh, and declare his lifelong study of the substance 'the greatest misfortune' and an 'irredeemable sin.'**
    - The elder Gornahoor Harharkh had been Beelzebub's essence-friend since the beginning of his exile and had invented a non-radiating lamp to study Okidanokh without destroying it — now his own son's research convinced him the entire investigation was harmful.
    - This reversal illustrates how even well-intentioned scientific inquiry into cosmic substances can, through the acquired knowledge itself, reveal that the inquiry was itself part of the problem.

### Beelzebub explains to his grandson the significance of the form and sequence which he chose for expounding the information concerning men

Beelzebub explains that his tales were deliberately structured to produce the 'Reason of understanding' rather than the 'Reason of knowing' in Hassein, because only when new information is processed through the 'zernofookalnian friction' of all three centers does it crystallize as genuine being-knowledge rather than inert stored data.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-177"></span>**Beelzebub structured his entire account of Earth beings to crystallize in Hassein not ready-made convictions but the raw material for his own logical confrontation, deliberately refraining from offering personal opinions first so that data would be processed through active mentation rather than passive reception.**
    - The two organizing principles were: never state personal opinion before giving facts, so conclusions do not arrive pre-formed; and relate events in a specific sequence so that the process of active comparison produces 'zernofookalnian friction' between the three centers.
    - Beelzebub also consistently avoided evoking the impulses of indignation, offense, or vexation in Hassein, because these impulses block the free crystallization of genuine understanding.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-178"></span>**The 'Reason of understanding,' proper to three-brained beings throughout the universe, blends new information permanently into a being's essence through zernofookalnian friction and thereby also generates the sacred substances abrustdonis and helkdonis needed to coat and perfect the higher being-bodies.**
    - In the Reason of understanding, the Holy Affirming is a newly perceived impression, the Holy Denying is corresponding data already in another localization, and the third force is the 'being-autokolitzikners' — results of actualized being-partkdolgduty that are formed only through conscious labor and intentional suffering.
    - The Reason of knowing, by contrast, deposits impressions at random without classification, so they remain mere information that can evaporate or shift based on circumstances, and produces no lasting spiritual substance.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-179"></span>**Contemporary Earth beings almost exclusively possess the Reason of knowing, in which all three centers function independently without genuine co-ordination, so that every impression settles in whatever localization happens to be active rather than being processed through the integrated whole being.**
    - Beelzebub learned the 'laws of fixing and unfixing of ideas in the localizations of beings' during his sojourn as a professional hypnotist, and applied this knowledge in designing his pedagogical approach with Hassein.
    - The decrystallization time of impressions stored through the Reason of knowing depends on the quantity and quality of impulses in a given being — strong emotional reactions temporarily fix data but cannot create genuine understanding.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-180"></span>**Beelzebub commands Hassein to allow his planetary body to rest for a period equal to the duration of their voyage before resuming intensive active mentation, because tempos change only 'katznookitzkernally' (with law-conformable gradualness), and forcing one part of the presence to change tempo faster than the whole produces 'dezonakooasanz' (lopsidedness).**
    - The planetary body, though the largest part of a being, is unconscious and dependent; the essence must therefore be just toward it and not require more than its possibilities allow at any given time.
    - Most contemporary Earth beings, through abnormal education, become permanently lopsided — one center overdeveloped while the others atrophy, making harmonious active mentation impossible.

### The inevitable result of impartial mentation

The culminating chapters show Hassein's genuine compassion for Earth beings triggering the reunion ceremony in which Beelzebub's horns are restored and he is revealed as a sacred Podkoolad — one of the rarest degrees of Reason in the universe — and Beelzebub offers his final verdict that the only means of saving Earth beings is implanting in them an awareness of their own inevitable death.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-181"></span>**Hassein's involuntary weeping at the thought of half-formed higher being-bodies trapped in Earth beings — while he himself was experiencing bliss during the collective meal — demonstrates that the data for genuine being-compassion have been successfully crystallized in him through Beelzebub's carefully structured teaching.**
    - Beelzebub identifies this weeping as proof that the impulse expressed in the inscription over the gate of the Holy Planet Purgatory — 'Only he may enter here who enters into the position of the other results of My labors' — is beginning to function in Hassein.
    - Beelzebub is glad precisely because this crisis occurs at the threshold of responsible age, when the crystallizing data will become part of Hassein's permanent being rather than passing states.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-182"></span>**A great cosmic egolionopty arrives bearing a ceremonial procession, and through a sacred ceremony in which all beings of Beelzebub's nature contribute particles of their own horns, Beelzebub's horns are restored — growing to five forks, revealing that he has attained the Reason of the sacred Podkoolad, the last gradation before the sacred Anklad.**
    - The five-fork formation appeared spontaneously beyond what the contributed substances could account for, indicating that the final degree of Reason had arisen from Beelzebub's own merits, not merely from the ceremony.
    - Even the venerable archangel leading the procession prostrated himself before Beelzebub, because the archangel's own Reason was only at the level of sacred Degindad — three degrees below the Anklad — while Beelzebub now stood one degree higher.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-183"></span>**When Hassein asks what Beelzebub would say if the Creator Himself asked for a summary judgment on whether Earth beings can be saved, Beelzebub answers that the only possible means would be to implant a new organ causing every person to constantly sense the inevitability of their own death and the death of everyone around them.**
    - Such a constant awareness would destroy the egoism now crystallized as the dominant psychic principle of Earth beings and simultaneously uproot the tendency to hate others that flows from egoism and generates all their destructive mutual relationships.
    - This proposed organ is explicitly compared to kundabuffer — the organ whose consequences have destroyed Earth humanity — but with opposite properties: instead of preventing perception of reality, it would force a single inescapable perception of ultimate reality.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-184"></span>**Ahoon's prostration before the newly revealed Beelzebub — confessing that his long service had been marred by disrespectful manifestations rooted in his inability to sense Objective Reason beneath a physically ordinary exterior — illustrates how even devoted proximity to wisdom cannot substitute for the developed inner capacity to perceive it.**

    - Ahoon attributes his blindness specifically to the lack of elders in his preparatory age who could have crystallized in him the psychic factors for actualizing being-partkdolgduty — locating the failure in educational conditions rather than innate deficiency.

### From the author

Gurdjieff concludes the first series by appending a lecture on human individuality — comparing the four-part composition of a real man (body, feeling, thought, and 'I') to a carriage, horse, coachman, and passenger — arguing that contemporary education produces only mechanical beings lacking genuine will or independent 'I,' and that liberation from this slavery, though rare, is possible through sincere self-observation and conscious labor.

* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-185"></span>**Every complete man should consist of four distinct personalities: the automatic thought-body (coachman), the feeling-organization (horse), the motor-reflex body (carriage), and the 'I' (passenger) — but contemporary education produces beings with only the first three, making them hackney carriages driven by any passing chance.**
    - The Institute for the Harmonious Development of Man had as one of its fundamental aims the education of each of the three independent parts separately and then in their reciprocal relationships, and the development of a genuine 'I' in each pupil.
    - The coachman-cabby analog: semi-literate, competent at habit but incompetent at principle, motivated by tips and driven by appetite, working only under the lash — this is the ruling function of the ordinary contemporary person.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-186"></span>**Gurdjieff categorically denies that ordinary people possess genuine will; what they call 'will' is only the resultant of competing desires, and all their actions are automatic reactions to external impressions — a position he claims to share with contemporary 'exact positive science,' which also regards man as a complex stimulus-response mechanism.**
    - The vivid example of a man terrified by a mouse yet unafraid of his own certain death demonstrates the incoherence of attributing rational will to ordinary humans: they fear what Nature permits them to fear (small frictions that sustain activity) and do not sense what would overwhelm their capacity to function.
    - Real will — the ability to act from one's own initiative according to one's whole presence — exists only in those who have done the long inner work of harmonious development; it is a sign of high being, not an ordinary human faculty.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-187"></span>**Contemporary language has become so degraded — words carrying only relative meanings that vary with each speaker's automatic associations — that genuine communication of ideas between ordinary people is impossible, and people confuse the shared sound of a word for shared understanding of its meaning.**
    - The word 'world' is offered as an example: asking educated contemporaries for an exact definition produces contradictory cosmological, philosophical, theological, and spiritualist definitions, none mutually acceptable — each person substitutes the first familiar sound-association for genuine comprehension.
    - The Institute's establishment of a special 'correct language' for its work was a necessary precondition for the study of mechanicality, because without it even the attempt to communicate findings about human psychology collapses into the same automatic association-play being studied.
* <span class="pull-quote">Man is a being who can do, and 'to do' means to act consciously and by one's own initiative.</span>
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-188"></span>**All human impressions are recorded on three sets of 'reels' in three separate localizations, and 'memory' as ordinarily understood is only the tiny fraction of this store accessible to conscious attention — but under hypnosis or other special states, every impression without exception, including experiences from the first days of life, is recoverable.**
    - New impressions are recorded both alongside similar previous impressions (by nature) and in chronological order, so each impression has multiple registration sites — but automatic associations replay whichever registration is most activated by current vibrations, independent of the person's wish.
    - The memory of a harmoniously developed real man tracks all impressions without exception, unlike the ordinary man whose accessible memory is only a small and shifting fragment of what was actually recorded.
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-189"></span>**Great Nature has providentially protected humans from sensing the terror of their own inevitable death and other genuine existential horrors — because if they could truly sense these things, they would be paralyzed — but this protection is only possible because humanity has degenerated to a level where they serve only Nature's immediate purposes, not higher cosmic ones.**
    - The small daily fears permitted by Nature — embarrassments, minor pains, social slights — function precisely to generate the stimuli, impulses, and strivings that keep ordinary people active and alive; without them, nothing would motivate action.
    - The real man who has attained genuine being serves both Nature's common purposes and his own 'egoistic' perfecting simultaneously — while the mechanical man serves only Nature and disappears forever when no longer needed, 'like a sheep slaughtered for its meat.'
* <span id="beelzebubs-tales-to-his-grandson-190"></span>**Gurdjieff dissolved the Institute for the Harmonious Development of Man after his near-fatal automobile accident because, without his direct involvement, the enormous material requirements could not be met — and several senior pupils, revealed as hasnamuss-inclined, abandoned the work and opened their own derivative 'workshops' exploiting naive seekers.**
    - The Institute had grown to the point of preparing eighteen branches in different countries for the following year, all of which had to be abandoned along with everything created through what Gurdjieff calls 'almost superhuman labor.'
    - Gurdjieff states categorically that the Institute is 'liquidated completely and forever,' partly to prevent naive people from being exploited by those falsely claiming its continuation.
