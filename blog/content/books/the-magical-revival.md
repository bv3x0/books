## Return of the Phoenix

The present magical revival is the resurgence of the pre-Christian Gnostic cult of Shaitan/Set, whose flame was reignited in 1904 when Aleister Crowley received The Book of the Law from the praeter-human Intelligence Aiwaz in Cairo, inaugurating the Aeon of Horus and establishing Thelema as a dynamic solar-phallic formula for the new aeon. The Phoenix symbol encodes the cyclical nature of this return, linking astronomical, physiological, and magical processes in Crowley's system.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-001"></span>**Crowley's identification with 'black magic' is a historical distortion rooted in Christian misunderstanding; his actual Cult of Shaitan/Satan reflects the pre-Biblical, Akkadian worship of Set, which carried no connotations of evil prior to Christian reinterpretation.**
    - In 1933 Crowley publicly denounced black magic in newspaper articles, declaring he despised it as an abuse of spiritual gifts for petty personal ends.
    - To an individual living five thousand years ago, Crowley's Cult of Shaitan would have evoked no feelings of perversity and guilt.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-002"></span>**The modern magical revival was prepared by Helena Blavatsky's Theosophical Society (founded 1875) and the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn (emerged 1886), both functioning as outer and inner expressions of a continuous Western occult Brotherhood tracing back through figures like Eliphas Levi, Adam Weishaupt, and the Knights Templars.**
    - The Golden Dawn was the Inner Mystery School of the Order that formulated itself in the outer world as the Theosophical Society.
    - Blavatsky's primary intention in founding the Theosophical Society was the destruction of Christianity in its historic as opposed to its 'eternal' form.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-003"></span>**Weishaupt's Order of the Illuminati (founded 1776) was a key vehicle of the same current, framing its core doctrine as the recovery of man's sovereignty over himself ('God is Man'), which Crowley later developed into the Thelemic law 'Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.'**
    - Weishaupt adopted the symbol of the Point within the Circle to represent the Current that found expression through him.
    - When Crowley assumed control of the O.T.O., he chose as its motto Deus est Homo (God is Man), and in Liber Oz declares 'There is no God but Man.'
* <span class="pull-quote">It was Aleister Crowley who fanned the flame to furnace heat, which he did when the 'world was destroyed by fire' in 1904.</span>
* <span id="the-magical-revival-004"></span>**The Phoenix symbol in Crowley's system encodes the formula of cyclic aeontic return (0=2, or the union of Will and Love back to Zero), the Sirius/Sothis star cycle of 26,000 years, and the physiological reversal specific to the XIth degree of the O.T.O., which Crowley adopted as his secret O.T.O. name.**
    - One authority ascribes to the Phoenix cycle a period of 666 years, which may have been another reason why Crowley chose the Phoenix as his secret name in the O.T.O.
    - The Phoenix or Ibis was the Bird of Cyclic Return; according to Plutarch, the ibis instructed men in the use of the enema, which she administered to herself with her beak, thus influencing Crowley's choice as the emblem of the XIth degree O.T.O.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-005"></span>**Crowley, Spare, and Fortune each addressed one of the three major dimensions of magical consciousness identified by Dion Fortune: Spare developed the voodoo/subconscious line through Atavistic Resurgence and Sigils; Crowley developed the solar line through congress with the Holy Guardian Angel; and Fortune developed the lunar/personality line through sexual polarity and controlled dreaming.**
    - Spare's formula of Atavistic Resurgence develops the 'voodoo' line by means of a system of Sigils deriving from his Alphabet of Desire, each letter of which represents a phase of sexual consciousness.
    - Fortune stressed the close relationship between the endocrine system and the chakras, the centres of magical power in the human organism.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-006"></span>**The Book of the Law, received by Crowley via Aiwaz in Cairo in 1904, is not merely prophetic but actively charged—its publication has been followed by major international catastrophes, and Crowley believed full compliance with Aiwaz's instructions would effect total civilizational transformation.**
    - Each publication of The Book of the Law was followed within a comparatively short time by international disasters: the Balkan war, the First World War, the Sino-Japanese war and World War II.
    - The formula 'Do what thou wilt' is the dynamic first half of a magical equation; its complement, 'Love is the law, love under will,' is the passive feminine principle—together they constitute the Law of Thelema.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-007"></span>**Set/Shaitan is identified as the formula of Return to Supreme Consciousness (Nuit), functioning as the Opposer not of goodness but of the outward projection of light—the force that absorbs Horus's projected energy back into the Absolute, making Set the true initiator and the formula of the Phoenix.**
    - Called evil to conceal its holiness, it is desire that prompts man to know himself—'through another' (i.e. through his own double, or 'devil').
    - Set represents the absorption of the projected energy of Horus; it is in the silence of the 'death' of desire that the Child of the 'blasphemous union' realizes its identity with Absolute Consciousness.

## Metaphysical Bases of Sexual Magick

Sexual magick operates on the metaphysical principle that no cause can be baulked of its effect: when the normal physical result of sexual congress is inhibited by will, the discharged energy forms a subtle astral image of the dominant idea at the moment of orgasm, which then becomes a living magical entity or talisman. The O.T.O.'s secret knowledge lies in correctly directing this phallic-yonic current through precise control of will, imagination, and the choice of female partner, whose vaginal kalas contain sacred emanations crucial to the rite.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-008"></span>**The foundational principle of sexual magick is that inhibiting the physical result of orgasm does not dissipate the energy but redirects it, forming an astral image of whatever idea occupies the mind at climax—making precise mental one-pointedness at that moment the central technical requirement.**
    - The mental exaltation generated by a magically controlled orgasm forms a lucent lens-like window past which stream the vivid astral imagery of the subconscious mind; specific images are evoked and 'fixed' and become instantly and vitally alive.

    - "I did not know until June 1912 the tremendous importance of the knowledge held by the O.T.O., and even when I knew, I did not realize it." —Aleister Crowley
    - "Through the O.T.O. we possess a form of energy stronger and subtler than any yet known; and its virtue is this, that it cannot be employed successfully by men ignorant of spiritual laws and untrained by spiritual methods." —Aleister Crowley

* <span id="the-magical-revival-013"></span>**The formula of the Beast conjoined with the Woman—exemplified in Tarot Key XI (Lust) and across world mythology from Leda and the Swan to Mary and the Dove—represents the ancient magical intention of obtaining a divine Messiah through non-standard procreation, the underlying principle being that non-normal generation produces results of a magical character.**
    - "This is the great idea of magicians in all times: to obtain a Messiah by some adaptation of the sexual process." —Aleister Crowley

## Dark Dynasties

The Thelemic current is identified with the worship of Set/Shaitan, astronomically represented by Sirius (the Dog-Star), which was the hidden god of the Yezidi of Sumer and the foundation of Egypt's 'dark dynasties'—those Typhonian or Draconian periods when the worship of the unmated mother and her star-son held sway before being suppressed by Osirian solar patriarchy. Crowley's magical revival is shown to be the restoration of this most ancient stellar tradition, which underlies the later Hermetic, Tantric, and Qabalistic systems.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-014"></span>**Crowley identifies the heart of the Thelemic current with Sirius/Sothis—the 'Sun behind the Sun'—which served as the hidden god of the Yezidi of Sumer and as the astronomical vehicle of Shaitan, whose formula underlies the XI° of the O.T.O. and the Phoenix symbol.**
    - "Aiwaz is not a mere formula, but is the true, most ancient name of the God of the Yezidi, and thus returns to the highest antiquity." —Aleister Crowley
    - Dion Fortune notes that Venus is ultimately transcended in Sirius; and the only physical explanation of this phenomenon is as explained above—relating to the XI° formula.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-015"></span>**The Devil in pre-Christian mythology was not the epitome of evil but the Double or twin—the primal duality of Set-Horus representing darkness and light—whose degradation to moral evil occurred only at a comparatively late stage of human history when patriarchal solar religion displaced the original stellar and lunar mother cults.**
    - The primal pair of twins was Set-Horus, and it was based upon man's awareness of darkness and light. The alternation of night and day was the first natural phenomenon of a dual nature to be observed.
    - Baphomet, the name which Crowley adopted in the O.T.O., means quite simply 'Father Mithras', the Bull-slaying deity; the Bull is the Sun, and Mithras—like Sirius—was a sun-slayer.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-016"></span>**Ancient Egyptian religious history cycles through alternating Draconian (Typhonian/Set-worshipping) and Osirian dynasties, the dark dynasties being those periods—particularly the XIIIth through XVIth—when the Cult of Set and the unmated mother reigned, their pharaoh-names terminating with ABRA, a prefix later perpetuated by the Gnostics as Abrasax and by Crowley as Abrahadabra.**
    - The Sevekh-hepts of the XIIIth Dynasty (c. 3180 B.C.) numbered among their pharaohs those whose names terminated with ABRA—a secret name of the Son of the Mother which the Gnostics later perpetuated in the name of their supreme deity, Abrasax.
    - The cult of Amen-Ra in the XXVIth Dynasty was in itself a magical revival of the worship of the Star Goddess Sevekh, who was worshipped under the form of a dragon or crocodile in the dark dynasties.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-017"></span>**The Yezidi of Mesopotamia preserved the most ancient form of Set-worship under the name Shaitan; their practice of facing North when invoking Shaitan reflects the astronomical fact that when the sun enters Capricorn (House of Shaitan) it turns northward—the worshipper identifies with the sun (Horus) that 'dies' and is reborn in the House of Set.**
    - "Dion Fortune observes that 'Crowley gives the North as the holy point towards which the operator turns to invoke, instead of the East, whence light arises. Now the north is called the place of the greatest symbolic darkness, and is only the holy point of one sect, the Yezidees, or devil worshippers.'" —Dion Fortune
    - Set is the initiator, the Opener of man's consciousness to the rays of the Undying God typified by Sirius—the Sun in the South—even as he is, astronomically speaking, the opener of the zodiacal year and the Great Cycle of 1460 years.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-018"></span>**The myth of Set and Horus encodes a vast astronomical and sociological transformation: Set was originally the stellar god of summer who announced the Nile inundation, but as soli-lunar reckoning replaced stellar reckoning and patriarchy replaced matriarchy, Set was permanently relegated to darkness and became the 'fallen' Lord of Hell—a mythological shift that Crowley's Thelemic system aims to reverse.**
    - When it was discovered that the moon was not self-luminous but informed by the rays of Ra (the sun), the lunar phase of mythology yielded to the solar, and the moon was degraded and cast out—thus the goddess became the witch.
    - The perpetual strife between the worshippers of the 'virgin' mother and her son and the Osirians who acknowledged the individualized fatherhood raged and ravaged Egypt for thousands of years.

## Centres of Power

The magical doctrines of the Cult of Set operate through a network of macrocosmic and microcosmic power centres—the earth's sacred sites correspond exactly to the seven major chakras of the human subtle body, which in turn relate to the endocrine glands—and Crowley's achievement was to revive this Sumerian stellar tradition as a scientifically precise system, correlating ancient cult centres (Cairo, Sumer, Glastonbury, Arunachala) with specific chakras and demonstrating through experiment that these subtle centres are real and describable.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-019"></span>**All previous attempts to rehabilitate the Cult of Set—by Weishaupt, Cagliostro, Levi, and Blavatsky—failed to achieve a complete restoration; Crowley succeeded because he received initiation of a higher order and made scientific and magical use of sex and drugs in conjunction with the ancient formulae.**
    - Blavatsky and Levi were of the Grade of Master of the Temple (8°=3°), and Cagliostro, like MacGregor Mathers, was an Exempt Adept (7°=4°); Weishaupt's position is doubtful.
    - Crowley described his mission in a past incarnation as 'to bring oriental wisdom to Europe and to restore paganism in a purer form', and in his most recent incarnation expressly described his Work as 'the rediscovery of the Sumerian Tradition.'
* <span id="the-magical-revival-020"></span>**The earth contains macrocosmic power centres corresponding to the seven major chakras; Grant identifies specific global locations—Arunachala (Sahasrara/Crown), the trans-Himalayan Gobi region/Shamballa (Ajna), Cairo (Visudha/Throat), a submerged Andean mountain and Glastonbury (Anahata/Heart), Sumer (Svadisthana), and California (Muladhara)—as the cult-centres of the present Aeon.**
    - The supreme seat of energy—the Sahasrara chakra—is not located within the physical body at all, but above the cranial suture; on the earth-plane it is represented by the Sacred Hill of Arunachala in South India.
    - The occult centre in California is only now becoming dynamic; it will prove to be a magazine of tremendous power during the evolution of both man and the planet in the present Aeon of Horus.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-021"></span>**The seven major chakras function through the endocrine glands—pineal and pituitary (Ajna), thyroid (Visudha), thymus and pancreas (Anahata), adrenals (Svadisthana), gonads (Muladhara)—and their subtle emanations, the kalas, form the true basis of sexual magick, representing a science as yet unknown to orthodox physiology.**
    - The affinity between the secretions of the endocrine glands and the vibrations radiating from the subtle chakras explored by yogins forms the basis of sexual magick which utilizes these vibrations in a way as yet unknown to science.
    - Wilhelm Reich in 1939 discovered a radiating energy in bions derived from sand, later found present in soil, atmosphere, solar radiation and the living organism—empirically corroborating the existence of subtle radiations described in occult anatomy.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-022"></span>**Crowley conducted extensive personal experiments into chakras not described in standard yoga texts, including three additional 'invertebrate' lotuses below the Muladhara—anal, prostatic, and glans penis—providing detailed phenomenological descriptions, and noting analogous though differently configured centres in the female body.**
    - "The anal lotus is of eight petals, deep crimson, glowing to poppy colour when excited; the prostatic lotus is like a peridot, extremely translucent, with thirty-two petals; the third lotus in the glans penis is of a startlingly rich purple with a golden centre." —Aleister Crowley
    - "The second chakra in the female, situated between the urethra and the cervix uteri, is a very large lotus with myriad petals, extremely sensitive and absorbent, and constitutes the greatest danger to women—foreign influences easily invade it during the catamenia." —Aleister Crowley

## Drugs and the Occult

Crowley used drugs systematically as tools for exploring suprasensible realms, comparing states induced by specific substances with those of mystical exaltation, establishing through personal experiment that the Will—not prohibition—is the only genuine safeguard against addiction, and demonstrating that judicious drug use in the context of Thelemic practice can facilitate contact with inner planes inaccessible by other means. The chapter also traces the ancient sacred use of intoxicants for communion with spirits and situates Crowley's experiments within this tradition.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-023"></span>**Allan Bennett (Frater Iehi Aour), Crowley's first magical teacher in the Golden Dawn, introduced him to drugs between 1898 and 1899; Bennett's own spontaneous experience of Shivadarshana at age eighteen—an annihilating mystical state normally achieved only after years of training—permanently oriented both men toward the extreme peaks of consciousness.**
    - "In Allan Bennett at eighteen, without training or preparation of any sort, Shivadarshana spontaneously occurred! He was instantly thrown out again. The effect, even upon the man well-trained by years of arduous work, is absolutely blasting." —Aleister Crowley
    - One of the great ironies lay in Crowley's belief that by prolonging Bennett's life and enabling him to travel East, Bennett would plant the banner of Thelema in Asian lands; instead, Bennett brought Buddhism to the West.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-024"></span>**The ancient use of intoxicating substances—from the Egyptian sycamore fig and African toddy-palm to the Indian Peepul and Vedic Soma—was a universal technique for achieving communion with spirits and entering divine states; intoxication was literally understood as a mode of 'becoming a spirit', which explains the enduring double meaning of the word 'spirits.'**
    - Intoxication was a mode of communing with spirits and of becoming a spirit, hence the term 'spirits'—even yet—suggests both the shades of the disembodied dead, and the dead drunk!
    - The typical sacred Tree, or Tree of Life, was itself based upon actual trees whose fruit or bark yielded alcoholic or narcotic juices, mythified as nectar—a drink fit for gods—because mortals who drank of it were transformed and could penetrate the spirit-world.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-025"></span>**Crowley's empirical experiments with drugs—conducted systematically rather than hedonistically—led him to conclude that popular misconceptions about drug danger are grossly exaggerated, that the Will is the sole genuine safeguard against addiction, and that specific drugs correspond to specific elements and magical functions.**
    - He refused to believe that the theory of irresistible fascination was correct and determined to prove that the magically consecrated Will was the sole safeguard against addiction—that prohibition increased the danger and made the drug-taker a criminal into the bargain.
    - Cocaine was assigned to Fire for fortifying the will; hashish and mescal to Water for image-making and opening the gates of Pleasure; ether to Air for mental analysis; directly hypnotic drugs to Earth for restoration of devitalized vehicles.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-026"></span>**Crowley's struggle with heroin—prescribed medically for severe asthma contracted after his 1901 Himalayan climbing—is documented in Liber 93, which records his ultimate triumph over physical addiction while achieving what he believed to be the Grade of Ipsissimus (10°=1°), the highest grade of the A.'.A.'.**
    - In 1923 Crowley found that a physical though not a moral habit of heroin had been firmly established; he tested his power to break it and found he was able to do so, but with great distress for three or four days at a stretch.
    - From his Magical Record it appears that he attained to the highest possible Grade of the Great White Brotherhood, i.e. the Grade of Ipsissimus, 10°=1° A.'.A.'., during the ordeal recorded in Liber 93.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-027"></span>**H.P. Lovecraft serves as a counterexample to Crowley: Lovecraft's dreams spontaneously generated imagery identical in character to Crowley's occult encounters, but because he recoiled from crossing the final threshold—and deliberately refused drugs that might have propelled him there—his tales are among the most powerful ever written precisely because of the unresolved terror at their centre.**
    - "I never took opium, but if I can't beat him for dreams from the age of three or four up, I am a dashed liar! Space, strange cities, weird landscapes, unknown monsters, hideous ceremonies, Oriental and Egyptian gorgeousness—daily commonplaces to me before I was six years old." —H.P. Lovecraft
    - Lovecraft forever recoiled on the brink of the Abyss, and spent his life in a vain attempt to deny the potent Entities that moved him—little wonder the tales he wrote are among the most hideous and powerful ever penned.

## Barbarous Names of Evocation

Communication with occult entities requires a systematic language—the barbarous names of evocation (Enochian, Goetic, Gnostic, Tantric) work not despite being unintelligible to the conscious mind but because of it, exalting consciousness to a pitch of magical suggestibility that unseals the subconsciousness; Crowley's restoration and systematic use of these formulae, especially through Dee's Enochian system and the Bornless One invocation, enabled him to achieve a synthesis of all major magical traditions and establish contact with extra-terrestrial intelligences. The chapter draws a detailed parallel between Crowley's motifs and those of H.P. Lovecraft, showing both accessed the same archetypal patterns of the New Aeon.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-028"></span>**Crowley's restoration of the Bornless One (Headless One) Invocation—derived from a Graeco-Egyptian source and forming the basis of the Preliminary Invocation of the Goetia—constitutes the most important practical ritual he revived, because it establishes the magician in the sphere of his Holy Guardian Angel, the foundational attainment of Western magick.**
    - Crowley translated the term acheralon as the Bornless One to indicate the fact that the True Will is subject to neither birth nor death; the invocation of the Bornless One therefore forms the practical basis for contacting the most hidden of all gods or daemons—the Holy Guardian Angel.
    - Knowledge derived from qabalistic analysis of the names of ancient deities enabled Crowley to restore rituals of vital importance; the names of deities being nothing less than magical formulae, their restoration supplies the key to their invocation or evocation.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-029"></span>**Crowley's systematic exploration of the Enochian Aethyrs with Victor Neuburg in 1909—using Dee and Kelley's system of angelic communications—enabled him to harmonize all major systems of magical attainment and produce the reference work 777, the first comprehensive dictionary of magical correspondences.**
    - "The conjurations given by Dr. Dee are in a language called Angelic, or Enochian—it possesses a structure of its own, with traces of grammar and syntax; it works. Even the beginner finds that 'things happen' when he uses it." —Aleister Crowley
    - The visions accompanying these invocations enabled Crowley to harmonize all major systems—Asiatic cults, Chaldean, Hebrew and Greek Qabalahs, Gnostic mysteries, ancient Egyptian, Pagan, Celtic and Druidical ritual, Mexican, Polynesian and African traditions—in a synthesis not previously achieved.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-030"></span>**The disastrous outcome experienced by both Edward Kelley in the 16th century and John W. Parsons in 1945 after invoking the 7th Aethyr demonstrates that working with these forces without proper preparation produces psychic disintegration—in Kelley's case personality dissociation and crime, in Parsons' case material ruin through the theft of his money and mistress by his scribe (L. Ron Hubbard).**
    - Parsons invoked the 7th Aethyr in 1945 with appropriate rituals backed by the powerful sexual magick of the VIII° O.T.O.; shortly afterwards his scribe absconded with his wife and tricked him out of money and possessions—his scribe is still at large, having grown wealthy and famous by a misuse of the secret knowledge he had wormed out of Parsons.
    - The Voice speaking through Kelley resulted in a sinister dissociation of Kelley's personality, and shortly after this episode he robbed Dee, absconded with his wife, and began a career of crime.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-031"></span>**Qabalistic analysis of spirit-names and numbers provides the primary method for verifying the authenticity of communications from non-terrestrial entities—if the numerical value of a spirit's name accords with the general nature of the vision, the contact is genuine; if alien, the spirit is an intrusive elemental to be banished.**
    - If a spirit gave as its number 761, this would be checked: 7+6+1=14, Key XIV (Art); if the symbols of this Key were consonant with the meaning of the number itself, it offered good ground for assuming a bona fide spirit—but further tests would still be necessary if doubt remained.
    - Because each man has an 'Angel'—a Guardian Angel—he has also a true number, because his number is identical with that of his Angel with whom he is One in the depth of his being; this is at the root of primitive man's dread of revealing his name.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-032"></span>**A systematic comparison of H.P. Lovecraft's fictional mythology with Crowley's Thelemic system reveals striking structural correspondences—from Al Azif/Liber AL to Yog-Sothoth/Sut-Thoth to Azathoth/Hadit at the centre of infinity—demonstrating that both men, independently, were channelling the same New Aeon archetypal patterns, but whereas Crowley interpreted them as initiatory, Lovecraft interpreted them with horror and moral revulsion.**
    - Lovecraft was unacquainted both with the name and the work of Crowley, yet some of his fantasies reflect, however distortedly, the salient themes of Crowley's Cult.
    - Whereas to Crowley the motifs conveyed no moral message, to Lovecraft they were instinct with horror and evil—a difference of interpretation, not of the underlying archetypes accessed.

## Star Fire

The tantric science of kalas—the 16 or more sacred secretions emanating from the female body's psychosexual zones, corresponding to the lunar cycle and to the marmas (power zones) of the subtle body—constitutes the true hidden foundation of sexual magick as taught in the higher degrees of the O.T.O., and Crowley's work represents a fusion of this Oriental science with the Western Hermetic tradition of Babalon and the Beast. The chapter establishes that the Scarlet Woman's role is oracular, her emanations serving as living talismans and as channels for extra-terrestrial intelligence, while the formula of the 'whore' exalts the principle of unrestricted creative flow over virginal restriction or settled motherhood.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-033"></span>**The kalas are subtle sacred secretions emanating from the female body's 28 marmas and 24 sandhis—each zone corresponding to a letter of the Sanskrit alphabet—which when magnetically activated by appropriate ritual passes flow to the lotus/vagina and serve as living magical talismans and oracular media, far beyond the crude interpretation of 'sex-worship.'**

## Blood, Vampirism, Death and Moon Magick

Blood functions as the supreme materializing agent for spirits because it is the vehicle of Life/Spirit itself; Crowley's two blood rites—the Mass of the Phoenix and a secret Gnosis rite of the O.T.O.—enact the sacrament of sacramental self-offering, while vampirism (the pathological inversion of this formula) is identified as black magic that enslaves departing souls. The chapter reframes death in the New Aeon as illusory—equated with sexual orgasm and mystical annihilation of the personality—dissolving the Osirian doctrine of suffering and replacing it with a doctrine of rapture and ecstatic union.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-038"></span>**Blood is the materializing agent for spirits because it is the vehicle of Spirit itself (ruach/prana), enabling both the manifestation of elemental forces and the appearance of the shades of the dead; the Mass of the Phoenix (Liber XLIV) and a secret O.T.O. gnosis rite are Crowley's two working rituals employing blood as a sacramental and electro-chemical formula.**
    - In the Mass of the Phoenix, the magician gashes his breast and absorbs his blood orally; in the rite of the Gnosis he unites himself with the source of the manifestation of Form (the yoni) and consumes the wafer soaked in blood—the act thus a sacrament imbued with the magical current of his own energy expressed in terms of Will.
    - Blood also forms the physical basis for the materialization of elemental and daemonic forces; Homer and other writers of antiquity described magical rites involving blood that manifested the shades of the departed to visible appearance.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-039"></span>**Vampirism—both in its literal form (the magician exhausting a victim's vital energy for personal empowerment) and its legendarily mummified form—is identified as black magic that imprisons the soul of the victim as a familiar spirit, a practice used by Oscar Wilde, the Horos couple, and S.L. Mathers, but contrary to the Law of Thelema which forbids interference with the will of other individuals.**
    - Experts may push the practice of vampiric exhaustion to the point of the death of the victim, thus not merely obtaining physical strength but imprisoning and enslaving the soul. This soul then serves as a familiar spirit.
    - The practice is held to be dangerous. It was used by the late Oscar Wilde, and by Mr. and Mrs. 'Horos' also, in a modified form by S. L. Mathers and his wife.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-040"></span>**In the New Aeon of Horus, death is reframed not as the apotheosis of suffering (the Osirian view) but as an illusion equivalent to sexual orgasm—the annihilation of apparent duality—and physical life is recognized as a sacrament; this reorientation produces more profound changes in consciousness than may be immediately realized.**
    - In the Aeon of Horus, physical life is recognized as a sacrament. Death is the disintegration of the gross body, but there is no interruption in the continuity of consciousness that once bound the bodily particles together.
    - Hadit exclaims: 'I am the flame that burns in every heart of man, and in the core of every star. I am Life, and the giver of Life, yet therefore is the knowledge of me the knowledge of death.'
* <span id="the-magical-revival-041"></span>**The Egyptian concept of the khaibit (sunshade/astral double) reveals that what is ordinarily called the soul comprises two discrete aspects—the dark shade (Set/stellar) and the golden hawk of light (Horus/solar)—which become unified during sleep but discrete in death, with sinister priest-magicians of ancient Egypt enslaving departing dark shades as familiars by abnormal posthumous hunger, giving rise to the vampire legend.**
    - Every individual assumes and relinquishes this habit, or shade, each time he sleeps and awakens. During sleep, the light and dark aspects of the shade are unified and appear identical; unless the sleeper is an initiate, in which case he is conscious of the distinction.
    - If victuals were not periodically supplied by relatives or friends of the deceased, the corpse would turn its attention to living people; posthumous rites and food-offerings were originally of a propitiatory kind designed to offset hideous hauntings.

## Strayed Gods

The careers of Crowley's magical heirs—Charles Stansfeld Jones (Frater Achad), Wilfred T. Smith, and John W. Parsons—illustrate the catastrophic consequences of taking magical oaths beyond one's grade, succumbing to ego-inflation at the moment of genuine attainment, and failing to cast the final drop of blood into the Cup of Babalon; despite genuine attainments (Achad discovered the Key of The Book of the Law), each ultimately failed, proving that the Thelemic system works but requires absolute surrender of personal ego at every stage.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-042"></span>**Charles Stansfeld Jones (Frater Achad) was both the living proof of the praeter-human origin of The Book of the Law—discovered as Crowley's magical 'son' through IX° operations—and a cautionary example of how prematurely taking the Oath of a Master of the Temple without completing prior grades produces eventual insanity and expulsion from the Path.**
    - Achad's attainment justified the methods Crowley adopted in his training of aspirants in the A.'.A.'.; it was an unequivocal fulfilment of the prophecy in The Book of the Law which declared that the Beast himself would not understand all the Mysteries the Book contained.
    - Achad reversed the order of the Paths of the Tree of Life and turned the Serpent of Wisdom upside down, and declared a new Aeon of Maat was at hand—almost before the Aeon of Horus had started.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-043"></span>**Achad's discovery of the Key of The Book of the Law—the word AL (=31), revealed at the Winter Solstice 1917—is documented as a genuine qabalistic revelation that caused Crowley to rename the Book from Liber Legis to Liber AL vel Legis; the formula LAShTAL (3×31=93) unifies Nuit (LA), Hadit (AL), and Set (ShT) in the supreme magical formula of Thelema.**
    - The word Achad (Unity) adds up to 13, which is 31 in reverse; 31 is the number not only of AL but also of LA (Nuit), and by the Tarot 31 equals ShT (the god Set or Shaitan). The formula LAShTAL, comprising 3×31, adds up to 93—the sacred number of Thelema, Agape and Aiwaz.
    - Crowley accepted the Key, and in consequence the title of The Book of the Law—originally Liber Legis—was changed to Liber AL vel Legis.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-044"></span>**Wilfred T. Smith (Frater 132), head of the Agapé Lodge in Pasadena, was identified by Crowley through astrological analysis as potentially 'the Incarnation of some God'—his birth chart containing eight planetary conjunctions comparable only to Shakespeare's—yet his failure during the Grand Magical Retirement to identify the indwelling deity resulted in total desolation and is recorded in his despairing letters to Crowley.**
    - "A complex of more than five planets is rare; of eight, Frater 666 knows one only—William Shakespeare—besides Wilfred Smith!" —Aleister Crowley
    - "Smith wrote in his final letter: 'Ill started, ill maintained, ill terminated. The shrine is desolate of the divine; has ever been; and I am completely empty; so much so, I do not know if I write accurately about myself.'" —Wilfred T. Smith
* <span id="the-magical-revival-045"></span>**John W. * <span id="the-magical-revival-046"></span>**Crowley's assessment of his failed magical heirs—Jones, Fuller, Neuburg, Mudd, Smith, Parsons—identifies a common mechanism of failure: the ego retaining one 'drop of blood' rather than surrendering entirely to the Cup of Babalon, causing the attainment to short-circuit into obsession and megalomania.**

## Dion Fortune

Dion Fortune (Violet Mary Firth, 1891–1946), a member of the Golden Dawn tradition, developed a system of lunar magick centered on the doctrine of sexual polarity and the dynamic sakti of the feminine principle, working through controlled dreaming and ritual enactment of the Rite of Isis to create a vortex in the ether through which Black Isis—the transformative, liberating aspect of the feminine—could manifest; her work connects Crowley's solar system and Spare's subconscious system through the mediating lunar personality-current.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-047"></span>**Fortune's entire occult vocation was triggered by a traumatic psychic attack from a domineering employer who had lived in India—an assault that shattered her mentally and physically, caused her etheric body to 'leak', and drove her to study analytical psychology, join the Theosophical Society, and eventually found the Fraternity of the Inner Light at Glastonbury.**
    - Fortune published her researches on psychic attack in Psychic Self Defence, a book unique of its kind filled with profoundly penetrating observations on the underworld of black magic and the sordid hinterland of pseudo-occultism.
    - The brush with her employer had so seriously damaged Fortune's etheric body that it 'leaked'—rents in the etheric sheath lead to loss of energy on all planes—and it was only when she received initiation in the Golden Dawn that the damage was repaired.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-048"></span>**Fortune recognized that Woman—considered passive in Western tradition—is in fact the dynamic awakener of the solar-phallic current and therefore the factor that makes the male positive; she enacted this doctrine through rituals, novels, and a play, all of which dramatize the eternal polarity of male-female modes of the Creative Fire.**
    - Fortune realized that Woman, considered in the West to be the negative or passive aspect of the Creative Energy, was on the contrary the dynamic awakener of the solar-phallic current, and as such the factor that made the male positive.
    - Her two novels The Sea Priestess (1938) and Moon Magic treat of the revival of Paganism—not the Isis of simple Nature, but the power of Nu or Black Isis, the primordial essence of Woman (sakti) in her dynamic aspect, capable of effecting profound transformations in human consciousness.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-049"></span>**Fortune arrived—through the Western Esoteric Tradition—at conclusions identical to those of the Tantrics regarding the interpenetration of mind and body through the chakras and their endocrine correlates, and she demonstrated through fictional case studies how traumatic introversion could tap past-life memories, enabling direct access to True Will.**
    - Fortune knew that the chakras, the zones of magical energy in man, were situated in the region of the endocrine system within the highly complex web of man's nervous anatomy.
    - From the debris of the everyday personality, Fortune showed a way out of the impasse of wasted lives, regrets and nostalgias, opening a direct path to the True Will that enabled the soul to know its own past and chart its future.

## Austin Osman Spare and the Zos Kia Cultus

Austin Osman Spare (1889–1956) developed an entirely original system of sorcery derived from his relationship with 'Mrs. Paterson'—an old Salem-lineage witch—which uses a personal Alphabet of Desire (sigils derived from subconscious sexual consciousness), the technique of deliberate forgetting, and sexual formulae to project desires directly into manifestation via the subconscious, bypassing the conscious mind's interference; his system, the Zos Kia Cultus, centers on the Sabbath as a communal ritual of wish-fulfilment through mass erotic exhaustion and the evocation of the 'Watcher' (Rehctaw) through atavistic resurgence.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-050"></span>**Spare's magical system originated in his early friendship with 'Mrs. Paterson'—a descendant of Salem witches who could materialize thoughts to visible appearance—whose teachings he absorbed and systematized over decades into an Atavistic Alphabet and a Grimoire of the Zos Kia Cultus that he was completing at the time of his death.**
    - Mrs. Paterson, when visited for fortune-telling, would objectivize the event in a visual image and the querent would see, in some dark corner of her room, a clearly defined if fleeting image of the prophesied event—and this never failed to follow at the appointed time.
    - One moment she was the lined and wizened old crone, then in a flash she appeared to him as a syren equipped with all the allurements of sex-appeal—a projection of glamour Spare was never able to discover the secret of, though he tried to emulate it.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-051"></span>**Spare's central technical innovation is the sigil—a personal, non-pictorial symbol derived from the Alphabet of Desire that represents a desired outcome without pictorially resembling it—which works by bypassing the conscious mind's interference through a technique of deliberate forgetting: the operator creates the sigil, then forgets what it represents, invoking it only from the subconscious.**
    - The reason he gives for forgetting the desire at the time of invoking it lies in the fact that for the operation to succeed the conscious mind must have no inkling of the transaction; consciously formulated desires take time to materialize, while subconscious desires can materialize very swiftly.
    - Several perennial desires are sigillized, scrambled together, and laid aside for several days; on reassembling the cards, the operator is unable to remember precisely what sigillizes what—the rite then becomes comparatively easy to accomplish.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-052"></span>**The Zos Kia Cultus Sabbath prayer to Rehctaw (Watcher, spelled backwards to invoke the mechanism of atavistic resurgence) is an elaborate ritual of concentrated wish-fulfilment through mass erotic exhaustion structured around four main sexual gestures (the quadriga sexualis)—constant congress, abstract creating by mantric vibration, simulation/Death Posture, and 'abortive congress'—culminating in the exteriorization of a pre-determined wish as an objectified hallucination.**
    - Prolonged voluntary abstinence, repression and sacrifice, is released in mass sexual congress and sublimated to one end: the exteriorization of a wish, which is thus achieved by a great saving and a total spending.
    - Rehctaw (Watcher) is spelt backwards because the 'backward' symbolism conceals the key to the reification of desire, the final absorption of the ego-current in its source—the Self; hence Spare's emphasis on Self-Love, or autotelic ecstasy.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-053"></span>**Spare's formula of the Earthenware Virgin—a specially constructed urn used in auto-erotic magical operation to create a vacuum that consecrates the sigillized wish at the moment of orgasm—is identified as derived from an ancient Greek practice (the 'urning') and as the origin of the legend of the genie of the brazen vessel in the Arabian Nights tradition.**
    - Spare describes the Earthenware Virgin as 'the most formidable formula known; it never fails and is dangerous. Hence, what is not written down must be guessed. From this formula was derived the legend of the genii of the brazen vessel associated with Solomon.'
    - The greatly enhanced pleasure induced by the suctional power of the vacuum increased the size of the penis and caused an unusually prolonged orgasm; at the critical moment the desire was to be vividly visualized and held steadily in mind for as long as possible.

## The Death Posture and the New Sexuality

The Death Posture—Austin Spare's central magical technique—is a simulation of death through utter negation of thought that concentrates all consciousness through sexuality, allowing suppressed desires to emerge and take form; it represents the philosophical crux of the Zos Kia Cultus, in which the Kia (the Atmospheric 'I', the true Self beyond all thought) is recognized as the sole reality, and the New Sexuality is revealed as the one constant sense that can be extended to embrace everything, dissolving the illusion of individual ownership of consciousness.

* <span id="the-magical-revival-054"></span>**The Death Posture is a simulation of death—utter negation of thought, prevention of desire from belief, and concentration of all consciousness through sexuality—that allows the suppressed dream to emerge and translate into body, functioning as the summing-up of the four gestures (Will/Hand, Imagination/Eye, Simulation, Reorganization) constituting the magical spells of Zos.**
    - "By the Death Posture the body is allowed to manifest spontaneously and is arbitrary and impervious to action. Only he who is unconscious of his actions has courage beyond good and evil, and is pure in this wisdom of sound sleep." —Austin Osman Spare
    - Death is 'the coming forth of the suppressed; the becoming by going; the great chance: an adventure in Will that translates into body'—this is the key to the Death Posture, really an imposture, a simulation of death for the purpose of allowing the suppressed dream to emerge.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-055"></span>**The Kia—Spare's term for the Atmospheric 'I', the state of 'Neither-Neither' or inbetweenness—is the sole Reality underlying all manifestation; by hindering belief and semen from conception they become 'simple and cosmic', and desire freed from conception merges with the Infinite, creating a unity of Self-Knowing that transcends subject and object.**
    - "By hindering belief and semen from conception, they become simple and cosmic." —Austin Osman Spare
    - The concept of the Universal Woman—she with whom Zos 'strayed into the path direct'—is both the inceptor and preceptor of the New Sexuality which transcends duality; in Crowley's Cult she is Nuit, of whom 0=2 is the mystical formula.
* <span id="the-magical-revival-056"></span>**Spare's doctrine of the New Sexuality differs from Crowley's Thelema in the way Taoist non-action differs from Hindu positive yoga: where Crowley's system is dynamic and solar (Do What Thou Wilt, Love Under Will), Spare's is receptive and autotelic (Self-Love, the Neither-Neither), focusing on the immediate realization of the Self rather than its directional expression through will.**
    - As the Negative Path of Taoism is to the Positive Path of traditional Hinduism, so is this creed of Self-Love and Zos-Kia to that of Thelema and Love under Will.
    - "Spare directly criticized ceremonial magicians in The Book of Pleasure: 'Why assume ceremonial robes and masks, and simulate attitudes of the Gods? Most emphatically, there is no need for repetition or feeble imitation. You are alive!'" —Austin Osman Spare
* <span id="the-magical-revival-057"></span>**Spare's magical philosophy anticipates the Sudden School of Ch'an Buddhism—the doctrine that Reality cannot be reached through gradual means or simulation, only through immediate spontaneous being—and his emphasis on 'Do it now, not eventually' and on the living act over all ceremonial rehearsal constitutes the theurgy of making the Word flesh in the most literal sense.**
    - "Realization is not by the mere utterance of the words 'I am I', nor by self-abuse, but by the living act." —Austin Osman Spare
    - These teachings, evolved by Spare before the First World War, are very close to the doctrines of the Sudden School of Ch'an Buddhism, the texts of which have only recently been made generally available.
